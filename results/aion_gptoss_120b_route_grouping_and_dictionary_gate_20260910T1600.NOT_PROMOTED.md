Real eight-token routes provide no expert group at the measured Metal batch-16
crossover. Perfect layer-wise grouping saves 42.64% of repeated compressed
bytes but averages only 1.74 activations per loaded expert. A six-expert
per-layer dictionary fits within 3 GiB yet covers only 30.90% of the four-token
temporal holdout. Unconstrained batching and a naive fixed dictionary are
therefore stopped.
