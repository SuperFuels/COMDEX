\subsection{What We Are Building}

AION Goal Engine is the guarded planning and orchestration layer for Aion Business.

It takes a business goal, decomposes it into child goals and agent/workflow proposals, gathers evidence, compares outcomes, prepares review-only execution packets, and surfaces everything in the Boardroom without unsafe autonomous writes.

The current work is not about free autonomous execution yet. The current work is about locking the contracts that make future execution safe.

\subsection{Canonical Lessons From The Latest Locks}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A guarded child-run request must expose \texttt{child\_run\_creation\_request\_valid}.
  \item A human-approved child-run action must expose canonical \texttt{runtime\_seed}.
  \item The executor bridge must trust only \texttt{runtime\_seed}.
  \item \texttt{child\_run\_seed} may remain for compatibility only.
  \item Conflict resolution remains review-only and must not auto-resolve, execute, grant permissions, or mutate parent/child goals.
  \item The focused lock suite now protects membership and ordering of critical chain tests.
\end{itemize}
