\section{Purpose}

Phase 22E.50 adds a king-safety threat-removal policy above the initiative hygiene stack.

The purpose is to prevent AION from treating king safety as passive king-walking.

\section{Failure Source}

The Level-3 game exposed repeated king movement during king-safety mode:

\begin{verbatim}
e1f1
f1g1
g1g2
g2f3
f3e2
e2f1
f1e1
e1d1
d1c2
c2b1
b1a1
\end{verbatim}

This moved the king but did not remove the attacking resources.

\section{Rule}

When AION is in \texttt{king\_safety}, passive king movement SHOULD be rejected if a stronger legal move exists that:

\begin{itemize}
  \item captures an attacking piece;
  \item blocks a mating mechanism;
  \item gives check;
  \item reduces king pressure;
  \item prevents immediate mate.
\end{itemize}
