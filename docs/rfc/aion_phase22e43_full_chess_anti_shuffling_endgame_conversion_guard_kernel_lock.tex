Phase 22E.43 prevents repeated rook or piece shuffling in endgame conversion positions.

\begin{verbatim}

b8b7

\section{Rule}

If a selected move repeats a recent move, reverses a recent move, or cycles through repeated squares without capture, check, promotion, or pawn progress, AION MUST treat the move as a shuffle.

If a deterministic legal conversion alternative exists, AION SHOULD replace the shuffle with that alternative.
