\section{Purpose}

Phase 22E.36 locks the Proactive Plan Primacy and Intent Driver.

The purpose of this phase is to make AION play with its own active intent rather than only reacting move by move.

AION SHOULD choose a strategic intent, preserve that intent across moves, and bias future selection toward moves that advance the active plan.

\section{Core Principle}

The locked principle is:

\begin{quote}
AION MUST play with strategic intent first. Reactive safety layers MAY override the active plan only when a tactical emergency or critical threat exists.
\end{quote}

This prevents winging the game from move to move.

\section{Emergency Override}

Emergency override is allowed when:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item the king is in check;
  \item a critical threat exists;
  \item the current plan fails a safety gate;
  \item a forced conversion or tactical survival condition appears.
\end{itemize}

The emergency override intent is:

\begin{verbatim}
neutralise_critical_threat
\end{verbatim}

\section{Integration Target}

It prepares a new input signal for it.

Phase 22E.37 MUST integrate the active intent and recommended move bias into the Strategic Regression Well Optimiser.

This phase does not call Stockfish, does not use LLM move judgement, and does not use Lichess analysis.
