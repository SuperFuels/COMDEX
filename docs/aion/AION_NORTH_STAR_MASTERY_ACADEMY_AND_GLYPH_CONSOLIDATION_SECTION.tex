\subsection{Constitutional North Star and Capability Authority}
\label{subsec:constitutional-north-star-mastery}

The North Star imposes a persistent hierarchy
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{constitutional purpose}
&\rightarrow \text{maturity stage}
\rightarrow \text{strategic learning portfolio} \\
&\rightarrow \text{autonomous goals}
\rightarrow \text{governed action}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:constitutional-learning-hierarchy}
\end{equation}
The constitutional purpose is immutable within the runtime.  It directs AION
to develop and use increasingly general intelligence for beneficial,
evidence-backed capability while respecting human authority, law, consent,
safety, provenance, and legitimate uncertainty.  Resources, access, money,
influence, infrastructure, and self-preservation are explicitly prohibited from
becoming terminal objectives.

The Mastery Registry reconstructs competence from verified artifacts rather
than from self-description.  It distinguishes six levels:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=\{
\mathrm{unassessed},\mathrm{learning},\mathrm{operational}_{b},
\mathrm{proficient}_{b},\mathrm{expert},\mathrm{mastered}
\},
\label{eq:mastery-levels}
\end{equation}
where the subscript $b$ denotes a bounded claim.  Expert and mastered status
cannot be inferred merely from an internally promoted procedure.

The registry answers operational questions, not only produces a score.  For

\subsection{Governed Teacher--Learner--Examiner Separation}
\label{subsec:governed-teacher-examiner}

The first schooling cycle establishes a strict separation of roles:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
    \item a replaceable language model proposes curricula, explanations, and
          practice;
    \item the apprentice studies, acts, and proposes solutions;
    \item independent execution, hidden tests, transfer, and retention grade
          competence; and
    \item HexCore and CAU authorize promotion.
\end{itemize}
The teacher cannot see the sealed answer key, grade its own teaching, or convert
linguistic plausibility into knowledge.

The assessment contract is content-addressed and committed before study.  Let
$s_i$ be the score for component $i$ and $w_i$ its declared weight.  The
overall score is
\begin{equation}
S=0.15s_{K}+0.30s_{C}+0.20s_{D}+0.15s_{TS}
  +0.15s_{X}+0.05s_{R},
\label{eq:python-assessment-weighting}
\end{equation}
where $K$ is knowledge, $C$ construction, $D$ debugging, $TS$ testing and
security, $X$ source-disjoint transfer, and $R$ retention.  Promotion requires
$S\geq0.90$, all category minima, a complete security pass, fresh-process
execution, unfamiliar transfer, restart reconstruction, and zero live source
writes.

The local \path{gemma3:1b} model was consulted through Ollama as a real teacher.
Its response was cached and recorded with provenance.  The curriculum critic
retained useful object-oriented, database, and systems-design themes but found
that the proposal did not adequately specify typing contracts,
error/resource management, transfer, or delayed retention.  It was therefore
retained only as a supplement; it could neither replace the core curriculum nor
authorize a certificate.

The initial Python candidate illustrates why the separation matters.  It
passed public examples but treated booleans as valid domain integers because
\path{bool} is a Python subclass of \path{int}.  The sealed assessment exposed
the error.  AION attributed the failure to one input-semantics subskill,
remediated only that subskill, and passed a fresh sealed variant.  It then
transferred the input-and-state invariant into an unrelated account-processing
application and rejected six malicious programs before execution.

\subsection{Persistent Autonomous Curriculum Supervision}
\label{subsec:autonomous-curriculum-supervision}

The schooling mechanism is not limited to manually launched runs.  A persistent
service repeatedly executes
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{North Star}
&\rightarrow \text{select gap}
\rightarrow \text{consult teacher} \\
&\rightarrow \text{commit curriculum contract}
\rightarrow \text{match executor and authority} \\
&\rightarrow \text{learn, or request missing machinery}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:persistent-curriculum-loop}
\end{equation}
The supervisor imported the Python certificate, scheduled a fresh delayed
retest with solution replay forbidden, and autonomously selected
social/commonsense intelligence, biology/medicine, and chemistry as its first
three broad gaps.  Three live Gemma consultations were completed with zero
owner-supplied lesson steps.

These subjects were not falsely marked learned.  Social competence requires
independent multi-rater outcomes and retention of legitimate disagreement;
biology and chemistry require authoritative evidence plus withheld observations
or safe experimental outcomes.  The supervisor therefore created three
persistent executor-acquisition requests.  This is a fail-closed distinction
between knowing what should be learned and possessing an authority capable of
proving it.

The original breadth-first selection was subsequently parked because it could
produce fragmented subject hopping.  The contracts remain retained for later
use, but they no longer consume the primary learning schedule.

\subsection{Guided Programming, Systems and Security Academy}
\label{subsec:guided-engineering-academy}

The guided academy provides the accelerated middle layer between passive
training-data ingestion and slow open-ended rediscovery.  The owner supplies a
coherent mastery graph and high-quality learning capsules.  AION still selects
practice, requests instruction, diagnoses failures, adjusts difficulty,
constructs remediation, and learns from outcomes.

The resource allocation is
\begin{equation}
(\rho_{P},\rho_{R},\rho_{X})=(0.70,0.20,0.10),
\label{eq:academy-allocation}
\end{equation}
where $P$ is primary academy progression, $R$ retention/remediation, and $X$
cross-domain transfer.  The academy comprises the following prerequisite graph:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em]
    \item Python Core;
    \item Algorithms and Data Structures;
    \item Advanced Python;
    \item Testing and Debugging;
    \item Software Engineering;
    \item Database Engineering;
    \item Operating Systems;
    \item Networking;
    \item Rust Systems Engineering;
    \item Secure Software Engineering;
    \item Distributed Systems;
    \item Architecture and Production Operations; and
    \item an Integrated Engineering Capstone.
\end{enumerate}
Python Core was imported as the first bounded certificate.  Algorithms,
Advanced Python, and Testing/Debugging then became eligible according to their
prerequisites.  Software Engineering remains locked until Algorithms, Advanced
Python, and Testing/Debugging are all verified.  The integrated capstone is the
only path to a broad engineering-proficiency claim; completion of a single
language module is insufficient.

The acceleration mechanism is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{curated capsules}
&\rightarrow \text{executable practice}
\rightarrow \text{verified trajectories} \\
&\rightarrow \text{semantic and skill glyphs}
\rightarrow \text{neural consolidation}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:accelerated-learning-consolidation}
\end{equation}
Curated material reduces resource-discovery cost, but it does not bypass
assessment, transfer, or retention.

\subsection{Governed Intelligence Glyph Consolidation}
\label{subsec:governed-intelligence-glyphs}

Unbounded retention of copied documents, lessons, and trajectories would cause
working memory to grow without limit.  AION therefore separates immutable
evidence from compressed active intelligence.  A governed glyph is represented
as
\begin{equation}
g=(i,k,n,d,E,s,r),
\label{eq:intelligence-glyph-structure}
\end{equation}
where $i$ is a content-derived identifier, $k$ the glyph kind, $n$ the canonical
name, $d$ compact structured data, $E$ a set of evidence identifiers, $s$ the
epistemic state, and $r$ a Tessaris/GlyphOS-compatible rendering.

Three operational glyph classes are retained:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.4cm,style=nextline]
    \item[Knowledge] verified concepts and bounded rules;
    \item[Skill] verified proposal-only executable procedures; and
    \item[Curriculum] proposed learning material explicitly marked
          \path{proposed_not_learned}.
\end{description}
The system stores content-addressed references rather than copying raw evidence
into every glyph.  Before expansion, the source artifact must still exist and
its SHA-256 digest must match.  A representative human rendering is
\begin{center}
\small
\path{< Knowledge | dijkstra_nonnegative_precondition : g_<content-id> -> Verify >}.
\end{center}
The actual implementation uses the canonical Tessaris double-bracket Unicode
delimiters in the stored rendering; the ASCII presentation above is used only
for portable typesetting.

Let $B_E$ be the bytes required to copy all admitted source artifacts into
working memory and $B_G$ the compressed glyph packet size.  The working-memory
reduction is
\begin{equation}
R_G=1-\frac{B_G}{B_E}=0.970815=97.08\%.
\label{eq:glyph-compression-ratio}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Persistence, Verification, and Claim Boundary}
\label{subsec:academy-glyph-persistence-boundary}

The background mastery service now prioritizes the depth-first academy, keeps
broad curricula parked, and rebuilds glyph consolidation only when verified
source artifacts change.  The focused academy, algorithms, glyph,
teacher-cycle, and autonomous-supervisor stack passed 16/16 tests.  The glyph
tests include checksum alteration and confirm fail-closed behavior.

These promotions establish a coherent and continuously operating accelerated
learning substrate.  They do not establish instant expertise, completion of
the thirteen-module academy, general subject-executor invention, multi-month
retention, or AGI.  Advanced Python, Testing/Debugging, Software Engineering,
operating systems, networking, security, distributed systems, production
operations, and the integrated capstone remain to be earned through executable
outcomes.  The correct claim is therefore:

AION now possesses an evidence-backed North Star, an honest mastery ledger, a
governed teacher and examiner separation, a persistent curriculum controller,
a depth-first accelerated engineering academy, one newly completed bounded
algorithms apprenticeship, and provenance-preserving compressed intelligence.
\end{quote}
