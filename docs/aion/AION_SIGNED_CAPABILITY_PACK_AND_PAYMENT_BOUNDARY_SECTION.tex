\section{Signed Department Capability and Payment Boundaries}

\subsection{Sales and Lead Response reference pack}

The first real department capability is packaged as a deterministic signed archive rather than a
label in a catalogue. Its prompt, operating procedure, tool declaration and policy are separately
versioned components. The procedure admits an inbound lead, reads only the permitted Business Map
projection, performs bounded classification, prepares a draft, requires exact recipient and
content approval, uses an authorised delivery adapter, verifies the provider receipt and records a
bounded outcome.

The pack contains no customer brain, customer data, provider credential or embedded network
destination. Its signed manifest declares least-authority identity scopes, tools, permitted
execution actions, time, cost and authorisation ceilings. Installation leaves it in an
\emph{unqualified} state. Activation requires successful dry-run, adversarial-permission and
outcome tests followed by an accountable human owner and approval receipt. Consequently, adding
the pack cannot silently send an email or change a CRM.

The generated development pack is cryptographically signed, integrity checked and reproducible.
A production Tessaris publisher identity, live connector qualification and real outcome evidence
remain release requirements. The same package structure can now be applied to Marketing, Support,
Finance and industry-specific capabilities without copying or forking the customer's learned
brain.

The core library now also contains deterministic Marketing Content, Customer Support Triage,
Finance Monitoring, Operations Coordination and Field Services Dispatch packages. Each has its
own purpose, permitted read set, reversible draft operation, named consequential action and
forbidden-claim rule. Every package defaults to preparation for review, carries no network
destination or credential and remains unqualified until tested with its eventual authorised
adapter and representative outcome evidence.

\subsection{Boardroom customer controls}

The installed Boardroom now displays a collapsible \emph{Departments, usage and value} panel
directly below the Business Map. It reads a private, content-free summary from the mother brain,
shows active, paid or review-only department state, verified-action allowance, managed-
intelligence budget and value evidence with its quality labels. A customer can start a bounded
thirty-day department trial with automatic renewal visibly disabled, refresh its state and cancel
through a separate confirmation. The underlying API also requires a distinct approval reference
before overage can be enabled. This surface does not expose prompts, messages, documents,
credentials or provider secrets.

\subsection{Provider-neutral payment lifecycle}

The billing boundary accepts opaque references to a customer and payment method held inside the
encrypted vault. It rejects raw payment identifiers and never writes card numbers, security codes,
bank credentials, provider API keys or webhook secrets into its lifecycle ledger. A proposal
records exact tenant, provider, plan, price, currency, interval, offer version, consent, trial
relationship and grace period. Idempotency keys cannot be reused for a different billing record.

Private approval must repeat the exact plan, amount and currency displayed to the customer. A
mismatch fails closed. Approval creates a pending-provider state; it does not claim payment.
Provider events are accepted only after signature verification, are idempotent by event identity,
and move the lifecycle through authorised, active, grace, cancelled or refunded states. Public
status omits even the opaque vault references. Cancellation remains pending until a verified
provider event confirms it.

Six focused tests prove vault-reference enforcement, proposal idempotency, exact-term approval,
signed provider events, verified cancellation and absence of card/provider secrets. The boundary
is ready for a certified provider adapter, but no live payment processor has been connected or
claimed in this increment.
