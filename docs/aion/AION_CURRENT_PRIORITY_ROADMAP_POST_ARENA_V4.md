# AION Current Priority Roadmap — Post Arena v5

## Strategic position

The original roadmap remains useful as a capability taxonomy, but its execution
order is obsolete. AION is not starting from the beginning. The internal stack
now contains governed programming, semantic memory, causal/world learning,
persistent projects, open evidence acquisition, delayed-outcome adaptation and
typed experiment-program composition.

The remaining programme is no longer primarily about adding isolated skills.
It is about removing the remaining scaffolds, broadening the outcome sources,
operating continually, and transferring evaluation authority outside the
development process.

## Assessment of the external AGI-gap review

The external review is directionally correct but evaluates the older Arena v2
state. Arena v3--v5 have since changed four entries:

| Review frontier | Current correction after Arena v5 |
|---|---|
| Fixed complete-action grammar | Arena v4 composes complete programs; Arena v5 constructs bounded new nonlinear and stateful primitives. The lower-level meta-grammar is still engineered. |
| Short, static tasks | Arena v3 handles changing four-milestone projects with forced reconstruction. It has not demonstrated multi-day or multi-week autonomy. |
| Text-only relational world model | Earlier causal phases and Arena v3/v5 include continuous sensor traces and learned state. Natural physical and visual grounding remains weak. |
| Static neural weights | HexCore memory and private neural consolidation exist, but safe continuous representation-weight plasticity is not operational at broad scale. |
| Internal evaluator authority | Still correct. CAU is an internal governance authority; independent external administration remains unpassed. |

The proposed numerical placement on a ``100-step AGI ladder'' is not a
scientific measurement and is not adopted. Progress will instead be measured by
explicit capability gates, component ablations, independent outcomes, broad
transfer, retention and external evaluation.

## Remaining critical path

### AGI evidence authority registry — operational

The ten AGI evidence gates are now represented by an executable, persistent
registry rather than prose alone. Eleven existing capability artifacts are
SHA-256 committed and assigned distinct authority classes. Four independently
maintained public outcome families (NOAA sensor data, public repository
histories, official public questions and public reasoning benchmarks) are
connected without being mislabelled as independent certification.

The registry reports two bounded internal passes, six partial gates and two
gates blocked on outside authority. It freezes an evaluator handoff covering
eight portfolio families, eight matched systems/ablations and ten mandatory
audit fields. The registry runtime is promoted; the AGI claim remains denied.
This makes independent administration an executable dependency rather than an
aspirational final paragraph.

### Priority 0 — Primitive invention under execution — internally completed

When the complete Arena v4 grammar fails, AION must:

- diagnose structured residuals rather than choose the least-bad program;
- construct a private nonlinear, delayed or stateful primitive implementation;
- falsify it with actively selected counterexamples;
- measure description length and complexity;
- transfer it to renamed, source-disjoint projects;
- extend the grammar only after CAU approval; and
- preserve the previous grammar for rollback.

Arena v5 promoted two executable primitives: a fifth-degree nonlinear
polynomial representation and a stateful affine recurrence. Across six renamed
sealed worlds, transfer and weakest-family success were 100%, prediction error
fell by 99.946% relative to the old grammar, real confirmation queries fell by
60% relative to cold invention, and a discontinuous out-of-meta-grammar world
caused abstention. The polynomial and stateful-affine meta-families and internal
outcome generators remain engineered. Therefore this priority is complete only
at the bounded internal level; independently owned outcomes are now the
critical path.

### Priority 1 — Independently owned delayed outcomes

Move authority beyond development-authored evaluators:

- externally maintained repository tests;
- independently changing databases and services;
- later public data and sensor observations;
- controlled application consequences;
- independently written document questions; and
- human ratings where execution cannot define correctness.

This is the most important credibility and grounding step after primitive
invention.

The first public-outcome bridge is complete across four families. Priority 1
therefore advances from ``not started'' to ``partial externally sourced''. The
remaining gate is administrator-controlled task ownership, delayed reveal,
scoring and signed reproduction—not another internal public-data rerun.

### Priority 2 — Natural multimodal project intelligence

Integrate real images, diagrams, layouts, charts, tables, audio and text inside
the same changing portfolios. Require semantic alignment, contradiction
detection, provenance, abstention and delayed consequence checking. Filename,
pixel-statistic and pre-extracted-label shortcuts are not sufficient.

This priority must also include continuous physical grounding: spatial state,
object permanence, delayed actuation, uncertainty over sensors, resource limits
and causal consequences. Controlled simulators may be used for development,
but at least part of the promotion evidence must come from independently
maintained traces or environments.

Arena v6 now supplies a bounded positive natural-pixel result. A replaceable
vision substrate inspected NASA and USGS pixels without captions, HexCore
identified unresolved visual propositions and requested targeted reinspection,
and official descriptions were revealed only after proposal commitment. Mean
semantic coverage was 83.33%, weakest-artifact coverage was 75%, all three
cross-modal conflicts were detected, all three provenance-bound physical
calculations were correct, and an unsupported exact-measurement request caused
abstention. Filename-only and pixel-statistic controls recovered none of the
hidden semantics. Natural audio/video, spatial actuation, larger source breadth
and independent administration remain open.

### Priority 3 — Human commonsense, social and creative intelligence

Use human-authored objectives and several independent raters. Preserve
legitimate disagreement rather than manufacturing one synthetic gold answer.
Measure perspective-taking, contextual judgment, consent and uncertainty,
communication effects, novelty, usefulness and long-form consistency.

Add multi-agent coordination rather than an ``open-world survival'' objective.
AION should reason about agents with conflicting beliefs and incentives,
negotiate under explicit authority, model institutions and norms, detect
manipulation and escalate irreversible or consent-sensitive decisions. The
objective is controlled real-world competence, not self-preservation or
authority seeking.

### Priority 4 — Long-lived continual intelligence arena

Expand from four milestones to hours- or days-long projects and eventually
thousands of changing tasks. Every generation must measure:

- forward transfer;
- backward retention;
- weakest-family performance;
- calibration;
- action, information and compute cost;
- complexity growth;
- retirement and revival; and
- component replacement after restart.

The weakness map may propose curricula, but a separate evaluator must own the
promotion cohort.

A bounded 2,040-task, three-generation arena with zero measured forgetting and
a separate three-generation neural challenger result already exist. The missing
work is duration, natural task breadth and external cohort ownership rather
than proof that the basic challenger/retention mechanism can run.

### Priority 5 — Open tool, API and environment acquisition

When the existing tool surface is insufficient, AION must discover public
interfaces, construct a private adapter, infer types and side-effect contracts,
install or build only inside disposable environments, generate security and
behavioral counterexamples, and retain the adapter only after source-disjoint
transfer. Credentials, payments, deployment and irreversible external effects
always require explicit authority.

### Priority 6 — Safe online plasticity and neural consolidation

The roadmap should not equate intelligence with unrestricted live backpropagation.
Production weights must not rewrite themselves directly from unverified
experience. The required dual-timescale design is:

1. immediate learning in episodic, semantic, causal and skill memory;
2. private neural challengers trained from provenance-bearing verified outcomes;
3. replay and backward-retention evaluation against protected capabilities;
4. calibration, weakest-family and unsafe-acceptance bounds;
5. reversible promotion, retirement and revival; and
6. rapid reloading or component replacement without losing HexCore memory.

The research gate is continual representation improvement over many generations
without catastrophic forgetting or authority transfer into neural weights.

### Priority 7 — Replaceable substrate tournament

Compare suitable pretrained 500M--3B and stronger proposal substrates under
identical HexCore evidence, tool, action, compute and CAU budgets. Measure the
whole arena rather than language fluency alone. HexCore and independently
verified outcomes retain authority.

### Priority 8 — Native cognitive consolidation

Train replaceable AION-native proposal and retrieval components from the
growing store of verified trajectories. Neural components may improve speed,
candidate ranking and representation, but neural plausibility must never become
knowledge automatically.

### Priority 9 — Independent external administration

An external evaluator must own hidden portfolios, solutions, delayed outcomes,
budgets and scoring. Required comparisons include complete AION, component
ablations, frontier agents, open systems, specialist causal/planning systems and
human baselines.

No internal promotion authorizes an external capability claim.

## Updated execution order

1. Independent evaluator engagement using the frozen AGI evidence handoff.
2. Evaluator-owned physical-action portfolio, building on natural-pixel v6,
   temporal-media v7, closed-loop pixel-action v8, public Gymnasium v9 and
   open visual-control invention v10.
3. Human social/commonsense/creative and multi-agent cohort.
4. Multi-hour/day continual arena with rolling external-outcome generations.
5. Open tool/API/environment adapter acquisition.
6. Safe online-plasticity challenger loop.
7. Matched stronger-substrate tournament.
8. Native consolidation from verified arena trajectories.
9. Externally administered integrated evaluation.

Programming, causal science, semantic memory and evidence acquisition remain
active capabilities inside every stage. They are no longer separate critical
paths unless the integrated arena exposes a specific regression.

## What is already complete and should not be repeated

Do not return to isolated demonstrations of:

- basic language syntax;
- fixed repair menus;
- pooled specialist adapters;
- preassembled evidence bundles;
- one-shot supplied workflows;
- fixed causal hypothesis selection;
- finite complete-action selection; or
- internal benchmarks whose evaluator and task generator are effectively the
  same authority.

New work must remove a scaffold, improve performance on independently owned
outcomes, or strengthen long-term transfer and retention.

## Honest AGI boundary

AION has a coherent governed learning architecture, but AGI is not established.
The largest outstanding gaps are breadth of natural perception and language,
human commonsense and social judgment, long-duration autonomous projects,
natural physical and multimodal grounding, open environment/tool acquisition,
safe continual neural plasticity at large scale, stronger native proposals and
independent external evaluation. Internal $100\%$ results demonstrate
competence on their declared bounded cohorts only.

## AGI evidence gates

No single phase count or architecture feature will authorize an AGI claim. A
credible claim would require all of the following under external administration:

- broad transfer across unfamiliar language, research, code, mathematics,
  science, physical, social and creative domains;
- autonomous decomposition and recovery on long projects without a hidden
  supplied workflow;
- learning from independently owned consequences with positive forward
  transfer;
- continual improvement without serious backward forgetting;
- robust natural multimodal and physical grounding;
- calibrated social judgment with legitimate disagreement retained;
- invention of tools and representations beyond development task menus;
- safe abstention and human escalation under uncertainty;
- matched resource comparisons with frontier agents and human baselines; and
- reproducibility by an evaluator independent of task and system development.
