\section{Phase 43: Compositional Theory Program Induction}
\label{sec:hexcore-phase43}

\subsection{Research Objective}

Phase 43 asks whether AION can determine which kind of conceptual revision is
required by predictive failure: a parameter change, a contextual exception, a
hidden intermediate predicate, or a different aggregation operator. It must
then identify the exact executable theory through active experimentation.

The promoted procedure is
\texttt{procedure\_compositional\_theory\_program\_67d49719b0b9}, descended
from the Phase 42 adaptive schema-revision champion.

\subsection{Executable Program Grammar}

Let $x\in\{0,1,2\}^{n}$ for $n\in\{3,4,5\}$. The bounded theory language
contains four program families.

\paragraph{Parameter revision.}
\begin{equation}
  f_{\tau}(x)=\mathbb{I}\!\left[\sum_i x_i\geq\tau\right].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Contextual exception.}
\begin{equation}
  f_{\tau,v}(x)=
  \mathbb{I}\!\left[
    \sum_i x_i\geq\tau \;\land\; x_v\neq 2
  \right].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Latent-predicate composition.}
\begin{align}
  z_{a,b}(x) &=
  \mathbb{I}[x_a\geq1 \land x_b\geq1],\\
  f_{\tau,a,b}(x) &=
  \mathbb{I}\!\left[
    z_{a,b}(x)=1 \;\land\!
    \sum_{i\notin\{a,b\}}x_i\geq\tau
  \right].
\end{align}

\paragraph{Range-sensitive operator.}
\begin{equation}
  f_{\tau,w}(x)=
  \mathbb{I}\!\left[
    \max_i x_i-\min_i x_i\leq w
    \;\land\;
    \sum_i x_i\geq\tau
  \right].
\end{equation}

Predictively equivalent candidates are collapsed to a single
minimum-complexity representative. Degenerate truth programs with near-constant
outputs are excluded before sealing.

\subsection{Probabilistic Criticism and Active Experimentation}

HexCore assigns a complexity-sensitive prior and maintains a posterior over all
remaining executable programs. The next intervention maximises expected
information gain per unit environmental cost:
\begin{equation}
  x^\star=\arg\max_x
  \frac{
    H[P(h)]-\mathbb{E}_{y\mid x}H[P(h\mid x,y)]
  }{c(x)}.
\end{equation}

The learner must collect at least 12 discovery observations and reach $98.5\%$
posterior confidence. The resulting program is then tested on 16 unseen
interventions; at least $87.5\%$ verification agreement is required. Otherwise
the operational result is \texttt{NO\_ADEQUATE\_THEORY}.

This separates two decisions: model-class diagnosis and exact program
identification. The truth program, revision class, and out-of-grammar status
remain unavailable to the solver.

\subsection{Sealed Evaluation}

The evaluation contained 48 sealed worlds, 16 independently named external
worlds, and 12 parity worlds intentionally outside the grammar. The in-grammar
cohort balanced all four program families and varied arity from three to five.
Observations used a deterministic $2\%$ noise process. The matched random
control used the same grammar, prior, likelihood, stopping criterion,
verification requirement, and budget; only experiment selection differed.

\subsection{Capability and Claim Boundary}

Phase 43 demonstrates bounded program induction rather than selection of one
flat rule. AION can identify when a threshold should change, when a rule needs
an exception, when two observations imply a useful hidden predicate, and when
the aggregation operator itself is wrong. It chooses discriminative
experiments, verifies the resulting executable theory on unseen interventions,
and declines to force a theory when the supplied language is inadequate.

The operator families, ternary variables, simulator, noise process, and
verification oracle remain engineered. This is not unrestricted program
synthesis, arbitrary scientific law discovery, real-world causal
identification, or AGI.

\subsection{Next Frontier}

Phase 44 should replace the fixed four-family grammar with a small typed
domain-specific language whose primitives may be composed and mutated into
previously unseen expression trees. The learner should invent reusable latent
subprograms and compare predictive evidence against description length.
Separately implemented simulators should introduce continuous or mixed-valued
state, delayed effects, and program structures absent from the curriculum.
Promotion must preserve safe timeout and abstention, external transfer,
intervention efficiency, provenance, restart retention, weakest-family
protection, and CAU authority.
