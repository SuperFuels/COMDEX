# HexCore Open-Topology Natural-Event Induction

**Date:** 30 July 2026  
**Status:** Governed promotion completed

## Objective

This track removes the fixed state-vector contract used by the earlier raw
representation learner. AION receives unfamiliar natural-language event logs
without being told:

- which tokens name entities, actions, dimensions or values;
- how many entities or state dimensions exist;
- which properties are temporary or persistent;
- how many hidden operating modes exist;
- whether interventions act independently, jointly or by fan-out.

The learner must construct the smallest predictive representation supported by
the observed transitions and abstain when no stable member of the model family
explains the stream.

## Architecture

The implemented induction path is:

1. segment natural event records into observation and intervention episodes;
2. invent anonymous entity, action, dimension and value identifiers;
3. infer entity and dimension cardinality from use rather than configuration;
4. compare immediate and settled observations to distinguish persistent from
   transient properties;
5. induce action-effect relations and cross-dimension coupling;
6. infer the number of latent regimes from distinct predictive signatures;
7. classify the resulting causal topology;
8. compile a traceable, data-only Photon IR proposal using the `⇒` operator;
9. reject streams whose effect signatures exceed the supported stable modes;
10. submit the model and evidence to HexCore and CAU for promotion.

Photon is a proposal representation only. It does not certify the inferred
model. Exact held-out transition prediction and the external sealed oracle
remain the authority.

## Evaluation design

Development and sealed cohorts use unrelated generated vocabularies. The sealed
cohort varies:

- entities from two to six;
- latent dimensions from two to six;
- hidden modes from one to three;
- independent, coupled/chain and fan-out causal structures;
- persistent and temporary properties.

A separate stochastic family deliberately violates the stable-mode assumption.
It must cause model criticism and abstention.

## What changed

The earlier learner transferred one fixed four-channel topology through renamed
symbols. This learner determines the size and structure of the state space
itself. It can form the operational distinction:

> “This stream appears to contain five entities, four changing dimensions,
> three hidden regimes, two durable properties, two temporary properties and
> a fan-out intervention structure.”

That is a significant bounded representation-learning advance because the
dimensions and topology are outputs of induction rather than inputs to the
benchmark.

## Claim boundary

The sentence templates, episode boundaries, binary value alphabets, finite
action families and sealed oracle remain engineered. This does not establish
unrestricted language understanding, ontology learning from arbitrary logs,
continuous-valued state discovery or AGI. The next representation challenge is
to remove template boundaries, support noisy coreference and continuous
variables, and ground invented structures in live project outcomes.
