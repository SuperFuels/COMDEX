\section{Arena v11: Cross-Topology Physical Invention}
\label{sec:arena-v11-cross-topology}

Arena v11 enters the articulated Acrobot environment without supplied joint or
link colours and without numerical state. Intervention-grounded component
analysis invented two joints, two links, and the topology fixed pivot $\to$
moving hinge $\to$ outer link. Six stateful controllers were composed.

The initially selected inner-link phase controller solved textbook dynamics but
collapsed under the alternate NeurIPS dynamics and was rejected. Failure-driven
worst-group calibration selected an outer-link angular-phase primitive,
\begin{equation}
 u_t=-\operatorname{sign}\!\left(
 \operatorname{wrap}(\phi^{\mathrm{outer}}_t-
 \phi^{\mathrm{outer}}_{t-1})\right).
\end{equation}
On fresh sealed seeds the revised controller solved $6/6$ textbook and $6/6$
NeurIPS-dynamics episodes, versus $0/12$ for the cold control. It recorded 1,617
pre-action commitments, used no numerical simulator observation, and executed
no unsafe action. CAU promoted
\path{procedure_cross_topology_physical_invention_v11_928b71e4fa63}.

This is bounded public-simulator topology and controller invention.  The
component primitives, controller meta-grammar, seeds and gates remain under
development control.  It is not external certification, robotics or AGI.
