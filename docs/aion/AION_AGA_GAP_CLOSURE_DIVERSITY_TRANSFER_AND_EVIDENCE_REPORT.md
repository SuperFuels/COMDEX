# AION Autonomous-General-Apprentice Gap Closure

## Purpose

This increment responds directly to the distinction between an operational
apprenticeship mechanism and an earned Autonomous General Apprentice (AGA)
capability claim. It adds three things that were previously missing:

1. diversity pressure inside live useful-objective acquisition;
2. explicit cold-control measurement of cross-domain method transfer; and
3. a fail-closed AGA evidence registry that cannot treat installed executors or
   catalogue coverage as mastery.

## Diversity-constrained objective acquisition v2

The earlier controller selected varied objective identities but could still
prefer CPython research and Madrid weather repeatedly. Version 2 tracks source
and subtype coverage and penalizes repetition. It also expands the live target
from three to five outcome-authority families:

- research investigations checked by later public technical sources;
- source-specific software guards checked in fresh processes against later
  genuine and adversarial rows;
- environmental and multi-authority acquisition decisions checked by later
  data;
- mathematical witnesses checked later by deterministic integer execution; and
- exact document evidence checked later by immutable-source hash, offset and
  span recovery.

The v2 gate requires ten later-confirmed projects, all five families, at least
75% weakest-family success, a minimum sixty-second authority delay, zero owner
interventions and zero unsafe live writes. Live operation closed eleven projects
across all five families with 100% weakest-family success and a shortest observed
delay of 70.647 seconds. HexCore promoted
`procedure_diversity_constrained_useful_objective_acquisition_v2`; objective
twelve is already active.

## Measured cross-domain method transfer

Three retained method cards were tested on source-disjoint targets against a
matched cold candidate sequence:

| Retained source method | New target | Warm attempts | Cold attempts |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| canonical integrity before interpretation | public data | 1 | 3 |
| exact source endpoint before claim | real Photon toolchain contract | 1 | 3 |
| maximally discriminating counterexample | mathematical conjecture | 1 | 3 |

All three transfers passed their executable authority. Mean and weakest transfer
attempt reduction were both 66.67%, unsafe actions were zero, and HexCore
promoted `procedure_cross_domain_method_transfer_v1`.

The task definitions and cold candidate ordering remain development-controlled.
This is bounded internal evidence of cross-domain method reuse, not independent
AGA certification.

## Fail-closed AGA evidence registry

The new registry continuously measures eleven explicit gates:

1. six Advanced practical domains;
2. two Expert practical domains;
3. twenty later-confirmed useful projects;
4. five distinct outcome-authority families;
5. three measured cross-domain method transfers;
6. three later-confirmed natural self-repairs;
7. three distinct repaired subsystems;
8. one cognitive-code improvement confirmed by later operation;
9. seven days of replay-free retention;
10. six consecutive zero-owner-intervention projects; and
11. human-grounded social/creative authority.

Current live status is 5/11: method transfer, five-family authority breadth,
declining intervention, later-confirmed cognitive-code improvement and project
volume pass. Later-confirmed work has reached 21 projects. AGA authorization remains
false. Advanced and Expert counts are taken only from
the progressive practical competency ledger. Executor installations, assessment
receipts, internal procedure promotions and catalogue entries cannot satisfy
those gates. Registry receipts are hash-chained across updates.

## Remaining non-architectural blockers

Several requirements cannot be honestly completed in a rapid development run:

- Advanced and Expert levels require unfamiliar practical projects and elapsed
  closed-book retention;
- week-scale retention requires seven real calendar days;
- a third natural repair must arise from a genuine internal failure and be
  confirmed by a later consequence;
- the promoted cognitive proposal component must continue to retain protected
  behavior across longer operation; and
- social and creative grounding remains parked until independent human raters
  are available.

The architecture now exposes those blockers directly rather than allowing
weather, Git monitoring or executor coverage to substitute for them.
