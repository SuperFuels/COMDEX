```text
timestamped natural records

→ withheld future prediction
→ independent observed outcome
→ failure diagnosis
```

The two promoted tracks are:

1. **Continuous Coreferential World Learning** — infer variable-size
   continuous state, local coreference, persistence, hidden regimes and causal
   coefficients from incomplete natural event streams.
2. **Outcome-Driven Continual World Revision** — recognize that an established
   causal graph remains structurally valid while its quantitative rules have
   changed, locate the change point, construct a private piecewise challenger
   and retain the repair over successive generations.

These results remain bounded by an engineered record grammar, finite actions,
linear/piecewise-linear effects and simulator-based outcome authority.

## Continuous coreferential world learning

### Removed scaffolding

The learner is not told:

- the number of entities;
- the number or names of state dimensions;
- which dimensions are persistent or transient;
- the number of hidden regimes;
- the causal topology;
- the intervention coefficients.

Records contain continuous measurements, missing non-critical readings,
measurement noise and local references such as “that unit” and “the same
channel.” Event grouping is recovered from timestamps and intervention order.

### Architecture

1. Parse timestamped observations and interventions.
2. Resolve local entity/dimension coreference.
3. Invent the entity and continuous-dimension vocabulary.
4. Reconstruct before, immediate and settled states.
5. Separate persistent and transient causal effects.
6. Cluster recurring action-effect signatures into hidden regimes.
7. Infer independent, coupled or fan-out topology.
8. Estimate signed continuous causal coefficients.
9. Predict effects on withheld future streams.
10. Criticize coefficient instability inconsistent with the retained model.
11. Compile a traceable Photon symbolic-numeric proposal.

## Outcome-driven continual world revision

### Research question

Can AION distinguish:

> “My representation is wrong”

from:

> “The entities and causal edges are still correct, but the world’s numerical
> rule has changed”?

The control retains the original stationary coefficients. The challenger may
use independently revealed transition outcomes to propose a single change
point and a new coefficient scale. It does not receive the true change point or
scale.

### Revision process

1. Predict each transition using the retained model.
2. Compare the prediction with a withheld simulator outcome.
3. Separate signature errors from coefficient residuals.
4. Test stationary and piecewise explanations privately.
5. Accept a change point only when it materially reduces outcome error.
6. Estimate the post-change scale from verified residual ratios.
7. Test the challenger once on withheld future episodes.
8. Replay all protected prior worlds after every generation.
9. Abstain when the process alternates too rapidly for one stable revision.

## Photon and authority boundary

Photon stores the inferred symbolic-numeric transition proposal:

```text
entity state + action + latent mode
⇒ target dimensions + signed coefficients
```

Photon does not authorize the model. The withheld outcome generator verifies
predictions; HexCore records the discrepancy and revision; CAU alone promotes
or rejects the challenger.

## Capability meaning

Within the tested family, AION can now form and revise the distinction:

> “The world still contains the same kinds of things and the same causal
> connections, but the strength of those connections changed at this point.”

It can then repair the numerical model, test the repair on future outcomes,
reject an unstable explanation and retain successful repair behavior without
forgetting earlier worlds.

## Claim boundary and next frontier

This is not unrestricted scientific learning. The following remain engineered:

- timestamped record format and local coreference scope;
- finite intervention vocabulary;
- piecewise-linear causal family;
- discrete abrupt change;
- simulator outcome authority;
- bounded generated worlds.

The next frontier is **open model-family construction** over continuous streams:
invent nonlinear operators, delayed effects and latent continuous variables
when neither the stationary nor piecewise-linear family explains the outcomes.
The subsequent external step must connect those models to real software,
document and sensor outcomes that were not produced by the development
generator.
