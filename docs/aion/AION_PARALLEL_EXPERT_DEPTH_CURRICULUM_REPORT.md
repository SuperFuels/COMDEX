# AION Parallel Expert-Depth Curriculum

## Purpose

The prior progressive curriculum delayed Expert development until every
unblocked subject had reached Advanced.  With a forty-four-subject catalogue and
many executor-acquisition gaps, this could leave proven Advanced competencies
idle for an unnecessarily long period.

## Dual-lane policy

The curriculum now operates two persistent lanes:

- a breadth lane receiving approximately seventy-five percent of new contracts;
- an expert-depth lane receiving approximately twenty-five percent.

As soon as a subject earns an overall Advanced result, its target is raised to
Expert independently of the rest of the catalogue.  Due delayed-retention tests
retain highest priority.  The depth queue persists across restart and cannot
award Expert without satisfying the normal evidence authority.

## Target-aware Expert requirements

Expert contracts require four knowledge and four practical demonstrations per
subskill, twelve projects, eight unfamiliar projects, six debugging recoveries,
four transfers, two delayed retention outcomes, eight independent outcomes,
twenty-five trials and recent scaffolding no greater than fifteen percent.
Advanced-level thresholds are no longer reused after a subject enters the depth
lane.

## Executor expansion

The verified Python project portfolio expanded from five to twelve distinct
families.  Added work covers bounded retries, default-deny authorization,
windowed event deduplication, capacity allocation, contiguous migrations,
failure-preserving batches and semantic-version selection.  Every champion
passes fresh execution and every paired incorrect mutation fails.  The debugging
portfolio can use all twelve families, and a second delayed-retention family was
added.  Algorithms practical depth now uses these unfamiliar project outcomes
rather than relabelling repeated concept demonstrations as new projects.

## Claim boundary

This establishes the control plane and executable capacity for parallel Expert
development.  No subject is marked Expert by this change.  Each must first pass
its pending Advanced retention test and then earn the additional depth evidence
through later execution and delayed outcomes.
