\section{Sovereign Brain Integration with the Existing Boardroom Business Map}
\label{sec:sovereign-boardroom-business-map}

\subsection{Architectural decision}

The Sovereign Business Brain does not introduce a replacement Business Map.
The existing Boardroom \texttt{BusinessMapContainer} remains the canonical source
of business truth.  The sovereign brain's business-map store is a registry of
bindings to that authority; it does not contain a copied collection of facts.
This prevents two maps from developing different revisions, permissions or
answers.

The surrounding Boardroom container family already models Business Identity,
Business Structure, Department Intelligence, the Financial Model, the Operating
Model, Organisation Authority, Brand Foundation, operational state and
Boardroom snapshots.  The Boardroom consumes the persisted map only after the
user confirms its exact revision and payload hash.  An earlier browser-local
map compiler is deliberately inert, so it cannot become another source of
truth.

\subsection{Sovereign binding}

First run creates a versioned binding identifying
\texttt{boardroom\_business\_containers} as the source of truth and explicitly
sets \texttt{parallel\_business\_map\_allowed} to false.  The binding contains
workspace identifiers and contract metadata, but no company facts.  New
business workspaces can be created as evidence-empty instances of the existing
Business Identity, Business Structure, Business Map and Department
Intelligence contracts.  They contain no example company, revenue, customer or
operating assumptions.

An existing Boardroom workspace is validated through the existing Pydantic
contract and registered without rewriting its Business Map.  Status reports its
revision, hash, present container types and bounded counts of facts,
assumptions, unknowns, conflicts, unanswered fields and department gaps.  This
allows the downloadable brain to adopt an established Boardroom safely rather
than forcing the business to repeat setup.

\subsection{Existing capabilities retained}

The implementation audit confirmed that Boardroom already provides:

\begin{itemize}
  \item a schema-driven voice and text foundation interview;
  \item persisted Business Map revisions and optimistic write checks;
  \item source references, confidence and verification metadata on modern facts;
  \item explicit assumptions, unknowns, conflicts and discovery gaps;
  \item founder confirmation bound to the current map revision and hash;
  \item local file, text, PDF, Word and spreadsheet knowledge extraction;
  \item owner review before extracted claims become searchable truth;
  \item department-scoped knowledge projection and Organisation Authority;
  \item existing read-side Gmail, Calendar, CRM/Sales, file and Xero foundations;
  \item Boardroom and Department Pilot context assembled from the same persisted
  containers; and
  \item evidence and execution receipts that remain separate from model output.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Governed Business Map lifecycle}

A new lifecycle service operates on the existing Business Map repository.
Every newly governed fact proposal requires a field, value, source reference,
bounded confidence and proposer.  It begins in an owner-review state and may be
approved, corrected or rejected.  Correction retains a hash of the previous
value.  Selective deletion requires an explicit deletion confirmation and emits
a durable tombstone receipt containing before and after hashes without retaining
the deleted business value in the receipt.

All mutations support an expected revision, preventing stale interfaces from
silently overwriting a newer map.  The governance ledger records action,
workspace, resulting map revision, evidence hash and time.  A hash-bound export
returns the canonical Boardroom map rather than a sovereign reconstruction.

\subsection{Knowledge-quality queues}

The same lifecycle authority produces bounded queues for proposed facts, stale
facts, legacy facts missing source or confidence, proposals without a nominated
review owner, declared unknowns, contradictions, unanswered foundation fields
and incomplete department discovery.  Expiry is calculated from explicit
effective dates; AION does not invent a stale date merely because a fact is old.

\subsection{Remaining Phase 2 work}

The existing foundation is substantial, but Phase~2 is not yet closed.  The
following work remains:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item make onboarding progress backend-authoritative and resumable across
  devices rather than dependent on browser-local state;
  \item normalize legacy relationships so every relevant edge has source,
  confidence and review state;
  \item expose map inspection, merge, correction and selective deletion through
  the non-technical interface;
  \item add entity linking to the existing provenance-preserving document claims;
  \item present existing read-only connectors in one setup flow and prove each
  connector's map projection;
  \item implement retention, legal hold and source-revocation consequences; and
  \item run a reconstruction and access-isolation exercise with representative
  organisations and restricted users.
\end{enumerate}

The design rule is therefore fixed: the downloadable AION brain adopts and
governs the Boardroom's existing Business Map; it never forks it.
