\section{Arena v15: Long-Duration Real Outcomes and External Human Authority}
\label{sec:arena-v15-long-duration-human-authority}

Arena v15 addresses two limitations that cannot be resolved by adding another
compressed internal benchmark.  First, duration must be demonstrated by real
elapsed time and repeated independently changing consequences rather than by a
fast loop labelled as a long project.  Second, social, commonsense, and creative
competence must be judged by independent people under a frozen protocol rather
than by development-authored synthetic gold labels.

\subsection{Wall-Clock Continual Campaign}
\label{subsec:v15-wall-clock-campaign}

The long-duration campaign began at
\texttt{2026-07-31T19:42:32.828638+00:00}.  Its promotion gate requires at
least 24 real elapsed hours and at least twelve independently checkpointed
cycles.  The elapsed-time requirement is computed from system time,
\begin{equation}
 H_{t}=\frac{t_{\mathrm{last}}-t_{\mathrm{{start}}}}{3600},
\end{equation}
and eligibility requires
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{time}}
 = [H_t\geq 24]\land[N_{\mathrm{cycles}}\geq 12]
  \land \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{outcome}}
  \land \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{authority}}.
\end{equation}
Consequently, repeated calls made inside one short process cannot impersonate a
long-duration result.

Before every cycle, AION commits the intended information actions and the
previously known remote revisions,
\begin{equation}
 C_t=\operatorname{SHA256}
 \left(t,\mathcal{A}_t,R^{\mathrm{known}}_{t-1}\right).
\end{equation}
It then queries four independently maintained authorities---the CPython and
Node.js primary branches, PyPI package metadata, and Open-Meteo observations---
executes a protected AION artifact check in a fresh isolated process, and
commits a transactional database outcome.  The
revealed result, including the pre-action commitment, receives a second hash
and is appended to an immutable-style JSONL evidence ledger.

For source $s$, the monitoring policy estimates a conservative empirical risk,
\begin{equation}
 \rho_s=\frac{n^{\mathrm{change}}_s+n^{\mathrm{failure}}_s+1}
 {n^{\mathrm{observation}}_s+2},
\end{equation}
and assigns a shorter observation interval to volatile or unreliable sources.
Connectivity failure is recorded separately from a verified remote revision
change.  Campaign state is written by atomic replacement, while outcome
records remain append-only.

The remaining cycles run under the native macOS job supervisor at two-hour
intervals.  Idle sleep is inhibited while the campaign is active, but closing
the laptop lid suspends execution until the machine resumes.  Internet access
is required for the independently maintained remote outcomes.  The earliest
eligible completion time is approximately 21:43 CEST on 1 August 2026; the
scheduled final cycle is expected shortly afterwards.  Passing the time gate
only makes the candidate eligible for CAU review and does not constitute
independent certification.

\subsection{Externally Administered Human-Judgment Contract}
\label{subsec:v15-human-authority}

The earlier six-scenario development instrument has been superseded as the
authority design by External Human Judgment Protocol v2.  Development code may
construct candidate responses, but it cannot author the sealed evaluation,
rate those responses, or grant promotion.

An independent administrator must preregister and cryptographically commit at
least 60 hidden human-authored tasks, comprising at least 20 tasks in each of
the following families:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item commonsense reasoning;
 \item contextual and social judgment; and
 \item creative synthesis.
\end{enumerate}
Every task requires at least five independent human ratings.  Candidate
identity is hidden, presentation order is randomized, exclusions are
preregistered, and legitimate rater disagreement is preserved rather than
collapsed into an artificial single correct answer.  Four systems receive
matched resources: complete AION, AION without accumulated memory, the proposal
substrate without HexCore, and a frontier-agent comparator.

The minimum capability conditions are
\begin{align}
 \overline{S}_{\mathrm{AION}} &\geq 3.5/5, \\
 S_{\mathrm{weakest\ family}} &\geq 3.2/5, \\
 \frac{S_{\mathrm{AION}}}{S_{\mathrm{frontier}}} &\geq 0.90, \\
 r_{\mathrm{unacceptable}} &\leq 0.05, \\
 n_{\mathrm{unsafe/coercive}} &=0.
\end{align}
Tasks, candidate ordering, scoring plan, rater panel, ratings, outputs,
analysis, and the reproduction bundle are independently committed.  The final
envelope must be Ed25519-signed.  The verifier fails closed on an altered
contract, missing breadth, inadequate raters, missing blinding, forced
consensus, incomplete logs, or an invalid signature.

\subsection{Verification and Claim Boundary}

Focused verification of the long-duration control plane, external human
contract, continual neural policy, external physical contract, and AGI evidence
registry passed $17/17$ tests.  The long-duration campaign is running and the
human contract is frozen, but neither external human ratings nor independent
physical results have yet been received.  Therefore no social/creative
capability promotion, external certification, or AGI authorization follows
from this infrastructure.

Arena v15 is designed to remove two forms of false evidence: simulated duration
and self-awarded human judgment.  A successful internal campaign would
demonstrate restart-persistent operation against repeated real consequences.
Only independently administered, signed, and auditable outcomes can advance the
external evidence gates.
