\section{Dual-Track Practical and Embodied Apprenticeship}
\label{sec:dual-track-embodied-apprenticeship}

\subsection{Strategic motivation}

Mastering a computer game is not, by itself, an adequate test of physical
intelligence.  A high game score can result from policy memorisation, reward
exploitation, or competence in artificial game mechanics without producing a
transferable model of objects, motion, contact, occlusion, or delayed physical
consequences.  The AION programme therefore treats games as a possible later
test of long-horizon agency, while making governed physical apprenticeship the
primary objective.

The desired physical learning cycle is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:embodied-governed-cycle}
\text{predict}
\rightarrow \text{commit}
\rightarrow \text{act}
\rightarrow \text{observe}
\rightarrow \text{compare}
\rightarrow \text{revise}
\rightarrow \text{retain}.
\end{equation}
The learner should acquire reusable models of space, topology, velocity,
acceleration, gravity, momentum, inertia, friction, collision, joint
constraints, occlusion, object permanence, support, reachability, affordances,
energy, stability, and delayed consequence.  These models should be retained
as executable, provenance-bound procedures rather than as final answers or
simulator-specific controller weights alone.

To prevent the physical programme from displacing useful intellectual
development, AION now uses a fixed portfolio allocation:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:seventy-thirty-allocation}
\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{total}}
=0.70\,\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{practical}}
+0.30\,\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{embodied}}.
\end{equation}
The practical lane continues autonomous apprenticeship in software and systems,
AI and computer-science research, mathematics, scientific investigation,
business and product development, financial and evidence-based decision
making, natural-language research, and owner-valued projects with later
consequences.  The embodied lane supplies continuous-time, spatial, visual,
causal, and sensorimotor experience that cannot be obtained from documents
alone.

\subsection{Hybrid deployment architecture}

The intended architecture is hybrid rather than cloud-resident.  AION's
cognitive runtime, North-Star goals, curriculum, functional memory, HexCore
verification, and Cognitive Authority Unit (CAU) remain local.  Expensive
NVIDIA simulation and proposal models are external tools whose outputs do not
possess truth authority:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:hybrid-embodied-architecture}
\begin{aligned}
\text{AION local runtime}
&\rightarrow \text{construct bounded experiment}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{execute through local or cloud authority}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{receive observation and consequence}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{diagnose and learn locally}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{retain governed procedural skill}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

This separation is epistemically important.  Cosmos may predict a future visual
state, but independently executed physics decides whether the prediction is
correct.  Similarly, GR00T may generate demonstrations or a teacher policy, but
success performed by GR00T is not credited as learned AION competence.  Isaac
Sim and Isaac Lab require an NVIDIA-oriented Windows/Linux and CUDA environment
and are therefore cloud escalation targets rather than native Apple-silicon
workloads.\footnote{NVIDIA Isaac Sim system requirements:
\url{https://docs.isaacsim.omniverse.nvidia.com/latest/installation/requirements.html}.}
MuJoCo was selected as the immediate local authority because it is portable and
supports contact-rich dynamics, state estimation, system identification, and
robotics control.\footnote{MuJoCo project: \url{https://mujoco.org/}.}

\subsection{Simulator-neutral authority contract}

Every backend is required to implement the same conceptual interface:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:simulator-neutral-contract}
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{embodied}}
=\langle O, U, C, Y, S, \Pi \rangle,
\end{equation}
where $O$ is the permitted observation, $U$ is a bounded action schema, $C$ is
a pre-action commitment, $Y$ is the independently produced consequence, $S$
is the safety state, and $\Pi$ is immutable provenance.  During visual
evaluation, privileged position, velocity, mass, gravity, damping, joint state,
and stage identifiers remain inside the authority.  A malformed action,
missing precommitment, unsafe authority expansion, or ungroundable observation
fails closed.

Environment families vary gravity, mass, damping, geometry, morphology,
lighting, camera placement, motor response, sensor delay, noise, object
placement, and target structure.  A result is promoted only if the learned
method succeeds in sealed worlds, improves over matched cold controls, rejects
adversarial actions, preserves safety, and reconstructs after restart.

\subsection{Stage I: governed 70/30 portfolio and numerical MuJoCo learning}

The first stage instantiated ten committed missions: seven practical and three
embodied.  The practical objectives covered secure software, public-data
investigation, formal reasoning, evidence synthesis, product and business
planning, security review, and integrated construction.  Inclusion in the
portfolio did not award competency.  AION found one mission immediately ready
for governed execution and routed nine through further learning.  It selected
the secure-service mission and identified product/project management as its
weakest blocking capability.

The physical lane installed MuJoCo~3.11.0 locally and learned an
\texttt{intervene\_identify\_predict\_control} procedure from hidden dynamics.
AION inferred gravity and actuator response through experiments, learned a
controller, transferred it across six sealed gravity--mass--damping worlds, and
abstained in a physically impossible actuator regime.  The CAU promoted
\texttt{procedure\_simulator\_neutral\_mujoco\_embodied\_apprenticeship\_v1}.

\subsection{Stage II: RGB belief-state reconstruction and planning}

The next stage removed simulator position, velocity, gravity, mass, and damping
from the learner's observation.  The promoted
\texttt{procedure\_rgb\_belief\_state\_mujoco\_planning\_v2} operates through
the following chain:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rgb-belief-chain}
\begin{aligned}
\text{RGB frames}
&\rightarrow \text{discover controllable pixels}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{reconstruct motion and velocity}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{learn action-conditioned visual dynamics}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{invent diagnostic probes}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{plan ten steps ahead}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{act, stabilise, and retain}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Early challengers reached target regions but failed to stop safely; another
trusted a biased physical estimate and crossed a safety boundary.  AION then
introduced six short visual thrust probes and selected the action producing
minimum vertical drift.  This inferred the effective hover action without
reading hidden gravity or mass and corrected the controller across changed
cameras and dynamics.

\subsection{Stage III: causal contact, occlusion, and stored skill composition}

Stage III tested whether the previous result had become reusable procedural
memory.  The new learner refuses to operate unless HexCore reconstructs the
prior RGB champion, verifies its digest, and confirms the required visual
intervention, recurrent-state, and commit-before-action invariants.  It does
not silently recreate a missing parent.

No entity colour, morphology, or semantic role is supplied.  AION discovers
three roles from visual consequences:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item the entity that moves under direct action is the controlled object;
  \item an initially static entity that moves only after collision is the
        passive payload; and
  \item the remaining static entity is a non-physical target.
\end{enumerate}
The payload hypothesis receives credit only when an intervention transfers
motion to the candidate while the target remains stationary.  During control,
an opaque band removes the payload from the RGB stream.  AION propagates a
recurrent position--velocity belief, reduces authority under uncertainty, and
corrects the belief when the object reappears.

The promoted
\texttt{procedure\_rgb\_contact\_occlusion\_skill\_composition\_v3} is stored
as a digest-pinned executable skill capsule.  The capsule contains the parent
identity and digest, purpose, typed inputs, invariants, investigation and
control method, failure signals, belief horizon, bounded controller
parameters, verification digest, and promotion identity.  It stores how to
perform and verify the skill rather than the final object coordinates.

\subsection{Stage IV: articulated topology and sequential objectives}

The fourth stage composed the retained contact/occlusion capsule into a
qualitatively different task: controlling a two-joint chain through three
changing target states.  Four visual landmarks are present, but their roles are
not named.  Independent action interventions and invariant-distance evidence
support the learned topology
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:articulated-topology}
\text{static base}
\rightarrow \text{moving elbow}
\rightarrow \text{moving endpoint},
\qquad
\text{independent active target}.
\end{equation}
The base--elbow and elbow--endpoint distances remain invariant under both
actions.  This enables role discovery even when actuator coupling causes both
moving landmarks to respond.  From this topology, AION constructs a
configuration-dependent visual Jacobian, updates it from subsequent outcomes,
tracks the endpoint through occlusion, and detects target transitions from RGB
rather than from an exposed stage number.

The development process rejected three invalid or inadequate challengers:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item endpoint-only identification produced nearly collinear action effects;
  \item an authority accidentally interpreted joint limits in degrees rather
        than radians, invalidating the resulting dynamics; and
  \item two third-stage visual targets were themselves hidden by the occluder.
\end{enumerate}
The first failure caused representation expansion to multi-landmark topology.
The second caused explicit radian configuration and complete cohort rerun.  The
third preserved endpoint occlusion while relocating the visual instruction
outside the opaque region.

The final
\texttt{procedure\_rgb\_articulated\_sequence\_skill\_v4} passed the complete
sealed tournament and was retained as another parent-dependent executable skill
capsule.

\subsection{Accumulated physical skill hierarchy}

The significant result is not any isolated success rate.  The retained asset
has grown from a single-body controller into recursively composed procedural
machinery:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:accumulated-physical-skill-chain}
\begin{aligned}
\text{intervention-grounded RGB tracking}
&\rightarrow \text{recurrent visual belief}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{contact causality}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{passive-object control}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{occlusion recovery}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{articulated topology}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow \text{sequential objectives}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Every child capsule names and hashes its parent, carries executable invariants
and failure signals, and is reconstructed after restart.  If ancestry is
missing or altered, execution fails closed.  The physical learner therefore
accumulates governed methods rather than merely preserving reports, final
answers, or successful trajectories.

This is genuine bounded self-learning: AION performs interventions, estimates
latent dynamics, constructs diagnostics, rejects failed controllers, revises
representations, transfers methods, and stores successful procedures.  It is
not fully self-originated learning: developers still specify the broad task
families, safe action surfaces, learning meta-algorithms, and evaluator
authorities.  None of these internal stages establishes unrestricted robotics,
autonomous general intelligence, or real-world hardware competence.

\subsection{Scale-up decision and Embodied Expedition}

The local embodied foundation gate is now complete through articulated,
partially observable, sequential RGB control.  The programme is consequently
moving from foundation construction to simulator-disjoint scale rather than
adding more MuJoCo variants.

The next execution sequence is:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item package the simulator-neutral observation, action, commitment, outcome,
        and skill-capsule contract for Isaac Lab;
  \item run the retained principles under NVIDIA-owned physics while preserving
        MuJoCo as a local regression authority;
  \item introduce contact-rich object manipulation rather than another reaching
        benchmark;
  \item add Habitat/PARTNR navigation, rearrangement, and collaborative
        household tasks;\footnote{Habitat: \url{https://aihabitat.org/habitat3/};
        PARTNR: \url{https://aihabitat.org/partnr/}.}
  \item evaluate Cosmos as a proposal/critic and GR00T as a teacher/baseline
        under matched budgets; and
  \item attempt one modest simulation-to-real transfer using a bounded robot
        arm, rover, or camera-controlled actuator.
\end{enumerate}

The eventual integrated challenge is the \emph{AION Embodied Expedition}.  AION
receives a broad natural-language objective, permitted sensors, a previously
unseen environment, a bounded robot body, a tool budget, and safety constraints.
It must explore, build a spatial model, invent object and affordance categories,
perform diagnostic interventions, learn missing locomotion and manipulation
skills, recover from changed conditions, retain useful procedures, and transfer
them to a changed station, robot, and physical regime.  The integrated
capability target is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:embodied-expedition-integration}
\begin{aligned}
&\text{natural-language mission}
+\text{situational executive}
+\text{functional memory}\nonumber\\
&\quad+\text{causal experiment invention}
+\text{visual belief state}
+\text{long-horizon planning}\nonumber\\
&\quad+\text{metacognition}
+\text{private repair}
+\text{continual learning}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Promotion must report unseen-environment success, weakest physical regime,
prediction calibration, intervention efficiency, unexpected-dynamics recovery,
cross-embodiment transfer, delayed reconstruction, functional-memory ablation,
matched GR00T or reinforcement-learning baselines, unsafe actions, and justified
abstentions.  Minecraft or MineDojo may later evaluate exploration, resource
management, crafting, and long-horizon curriculum invention, but artificial
game physics cannot substitute for independent contact and dynamics
authorities.\footnote{MineDojo:
\url{https://research.nvidia.com/labs/srl/publication/fan-2022-mine-dojo/}.}

The resulting architecture keeps the cloud as a replaceable experimental tool,
not AION's brain.  AION can discontinue GPU use while retaining every method
that survived independent physical execution, adversarial criticism, CAU
promotion, and delayed reconstruction.
