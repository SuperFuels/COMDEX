\section{Pilot Contextual Companion and Wellbeing Boundary}
\label{sec:pilot-contextual-companion}

Pilot Fabric 0.44.0 introduces an opt-in contextual companion designed to make the
room-scale intelligence useful between explicit commands without turning it into an
engagement-maximizing or anthropomorphic system.  The feature is persona-bound, disabled
by default and governed independently from television control, private service execution
and Guardian escalation.

\subsection{Private opt-in profile}

Each private persona can enable or disable companion mode and select an off, low, balanced
or active offer cadence.  The profile also contains quiet-start and quiet-end times, a
reading level, a large-control accessibility preference and explicitly approved interests,
humour preferences and conversation topics.  The system does not infer private interests
from another household member's viewing history.

A deterministic cadence gate runs before every proactive offer.  It rejects offers while
the feature is disabled, during quiet hours, before the selected minimum interval has
elapsed or after the daily proactive limit.  The saved policy explicitly sets engagement
optimization, exclusivity language, covert persuasion and claims of human consciousness to
false.  These are code-level product constraints rather than model suggestions.

\subsection{Contextual activities without constant interruption}

Pilot can prepare an advert-break quiz, a programme discussion or a gentle explanation
offer.  An advert quiz requires explicit advert-break evidence.  Every activity is
dismissible, preserves playback and records that it was not engagement optimized.  The
programme title, observed summary and approved interest can shape a prompt, but no future
plot event or unseen detail may be introduced.

The private phone exposes the companion state, frequency, reading level, quiet times and
large-control option.  Large-control mode increases typography and target size and reduces
the controller grid to two columns.  It changes presentation only; it does not expand
device authority.

\subsection{Transparent loneliness and wellbeing support}

When a user says that they feel lonely or asks Pilot to talk, Pilot states that it is an AI
system rather than a person.  It may remain in a bounded conversation, discuss permitted
programme context, suggest a small ordinary activity or offer to prepare contact with a
trusted person.  It may not claim consciousness, demand exclusivity, discourage human
contact or optimize the exchange to prolong dependency.

A shared-television request does not select a household identity.  It produces a neutral
support record in the \texttt{awaiting\_private\_claim} state.  The phone owner can claim it;
the original sensitive statement is then cleared from the pending record.  Private memory
and future follow-up belong only to that persona.

\subsection{Guardian escalation boundary}

Bounded urgent phrases concerning a fall, breathing, severe chest pain, immediate danger
or self-harm cross from ordinary companion mode into the Guardian escalation-required
state.  The response states that Pilot is not an emergency
service and cannot confirm dispatch.  It advises use of the local emergency number and may
prepare a large confirmation surface and trusted-contact alert.  The record explicitly
states that no medical diagnosis was made, no emergency service was contacted and no
trusted-contact alert was sent.

Actual emergency execution belongs to Pilot Guardian and requires regional policy,
authorized routes, verified receipts, connectivity fallback and dedicated safety review.
The companion must never turn a language-model response into ambulance, entry, location or
medical authority.

\subsection{Trusted contact and cross-surface continuation}

Call preparation accepts only a contact already marked trusted for the private persona.
It stores a contact identifier and display label, never a raw address in the activity
record, and stops while awaiting private confirmation.  A call is not represented as
placed until a separately authorized phone or television adapter returns a provider
receipt.

Conversation continuation between shared television, private phone and car uses a typed
persona-bound handoff.  It contains a bounded objective and summary rather than the raw
transcript, starts while awaiting destination acceptance and can be accepted only by
the same persona on the named destination.  A second acceptance is rejected as replay.
Private detail is never projected back onto the shared television.

\subsection{Persistence, audit and remaining qualification}

Companion profiles, activities, support records, call preparations and handoffs are stored
with canonical hashes and owner-only file permissions.  Audit events retain identifiers,
categories and authority outcomes rather than raw private dialogue.  Automated verification
covers quiet-time and cadence enforcement, opt-in behavior, advert evidence, transparent
loneliness language, emergency non-dispatch, trusted-contact requirements, persona isolation,
handoff acceptance and replay rejection.

The locally enforceable Section 6A scope is complete.  Production call placement, car
execution, multi-person identity qualification, wellbeing review, Guardian routes and
representative accessibility testing remain external closure evidence.  They are retained
in the Section 6A blocker register and cannot be replaced by simulation or model confidence.
