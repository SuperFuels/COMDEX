\section{Evidence-Based Capacity and Whole-Brain Migration}

\subsection{Requirements rather than business-size labels}

AION does not ask whether a customer is ``small'', ``medium'' or ``large''. Its capacity planner
uses observed days, peak concurrency, verified actions, model-token and visual workload, private
data volume, measured latency, availability target, offline requirement, regulatory controls and
multi-location operation. It recommends an existing machine, AION Box, single-GPU rack or private
cluster/customer cloud from those requirements.

Evidence shorter than thirty days is labelled provisional. The output states its assumptions and
reasons, does not silently choose a hardware vendor and contains no invented saving or return-on-
investment claim. Offline requirements keep the proposed deployment on customer-controlled
premises. Model family, size and quantisation remain a separate selection because hardware class
alone does not prove workload quality.

\subsection{Migration of the same learned brain}

Moving from Level~1 to a Box, rack or private cloud does not create a new brain. The migration
orchestrator first proves the source is healthy, creates the existing encrypted complete-brain
export, restores it into an empty staged destination, verifies target health and requires the
logical brain identity to remain unchanged. A mismatch removes the failed staging result and
cannot activate it.

Successful preparation leaves the original installation active and recoverable. A second,
explicit activation receipt is required before the target is marked active. Even after activation,
the source is retained for controlled recovery until a later, separately governed retirement
policy exists. Rollback records the actor and a hash of the reason; it does not delete either brain
or reveal the reason in the portable receipt.

Five focused tests cover requirements-based scaling, provisional evidence, offline placement,
identity-preserving encrypted migration, source retention, activation, rollback and refusal to
overwrite a non-empty destination. Production qualification on each appliance, rack and private-
cloud target remains necessary before promising service levels.
