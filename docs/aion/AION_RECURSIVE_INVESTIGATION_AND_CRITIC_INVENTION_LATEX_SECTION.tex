\section{Recursive Investigation, Runtime Extension, and Critic Invention}
\label{sec:recursive-investigation-invention}

This stage investigated whether AION could progress beyond retrieving answers
with pre-supplied tools and instead retain increasingly general forms of
executable investigative machinery.  The central experimental sequence was

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:recursive-invention-chain}
\begin{aligned}
\text{institutional objective}
&\rightarrow \text{dependency discovery}
\rightarrow \text{action-operator invention} \\
&\rightarrow \text{runtime-language expansion}
\rightarrow \text{micro-operation invention} \\
&\rightarrow \text{critic failure detection}
\rightarrow \text{property-language invention}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Each promotion used the same governance discipline.  Candidate machinery was
constructed and executed privately; semantic, adversarial, and resource
properties were evaluated before promotion; development success was
insufficient for runtime installation; exact candidate digests were committed
before delayed evaluation; and CAU authority remained responsible for
promotion.  The proposal pathway acquired no ambient network, filesystem,
process, credential, or live-write authority.

\subsection{Multi-step institutional intelligence}
\label{subsec:multistep-institutional-intelligence}

The first experiment removed the assumption that official documentation would
expose an immediately executable final endpoint.  Given an institutional
mission but no identifier or final collection URL, AION had to construct and
precommit a dependent action graph:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:institutional-chain}
\text{mission}
\rightarrow \text{official documentation}
\rightarrow \text{identifier resolution}
\rightarrow \text{collection query}
\rightarrow \text{record selection}
\rightarrow \text{detail retrieval}
\rightarrow \text{cross-hop verification}.
\end{equation}

In the SEC cohort, AION resolved Apple's Central Index Key (CIK), used the
resolved identity to access the submissions collection, selected the latest
Form~10-K, and verified identity continuity across the dependency chain.  The
initial public-search route was throttled and was rejected rather than treated
as negative evidence.  AION recovered by traversing the SEC's own public link
graph and discovering the resolver and submissions contract without receiving
a hard-coded terminal endpoint.  In the Federal Register cohort, it resolved
the Environmental Protection Agency identifier, queried the agency collection,
selected a rule, recovered its document number, and retrieved the detailed
record while preserving agency identity across hops.

Both action graphs were committed before their terminal requests.  After real
five-minute boundaries, fresh observations reproduced the committed identities
and semantics.  The response hashes remained stable, so the result establishes
delayed reobservation and retained execution, not environmental change.  These
experiments promoted
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_multistep_institutional_intelligence_v1}} and
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_multistep_institutional_later_retention_v1}}.

\subsection{Open information-action invention}
\label{subsec:open-information-action-invention}

Multi-step composition exposed cases that the retained action grammar could not
express.  AION therefore diagnosed the residual, synthesized typed executable
abstract syntax trees, criticised alternatives, and retained two new
investigation operators:

\begin{description}
    \item[\texttt{BOUNDED\_PAGINATED\_GET}.]
    Follows authority-supplied continuations, supports both URL and page-counter
    protocols, detects cycles, enforces an eight-page ceiling, removes duplicate
    inflation, rejects cross-authority redirection, and rejects incomplete
    truncation.  Fixed two-page retrieval, cycle-unsafe traversal, and trusting
    an unwitnessed declared total were rejected.

    \item[\texttt{SAFE\_LOCALNAME\_XML\_EVIDENCE}.]
    Parses bounded XML without executing external entities, rejects DTD and
    entity declarations, resolves namespaces through local-name matching,
    normalizes bounded textual evidence, and abstains on binary or unsupported
    input.  Regex extraction and exact case-sensitive tag matching were
    rejected.
\end{description}

The pagination operator enumerated 29 Federal Register records and transferred
unchanged to 66 World Bank observations despite incompatible continuation
representations.  The XML operator recovered agency and subject evidence from
Federal Register XML, then transferred to RFC~9110 XML to recover title and
abstract evidence across different roots, namespaces, cases, and document
scales.  Seven candidate programs were criticised and all ten malicious action
programs were rejected.  Four delayed public executions passed, promoting
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_open_information_action_invention_v1}} and
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_open_information_action_later_retention_v1}}.

\subsection{Recursive action-language expansion}
\label{subsec:recursive-action-language-expansion}

The remaining limitation was the interpreter's atomic vocabulary.  AION was
asked to transform unfamiliar JSON or CSV evidence into a canonical temporal
numeric series without receiving a record-container path, time-field name, or
measurement-field name.  No composition of the existing atoms could express
the required semantic projection.  AION privately constructed
\texttt{TEMPORAL\_NUMERIC\_PROJECTION}, an atom that:

\begin{itemize}
    \item discovers candidate record containers;
    \item profiles field names and observed values to infer temporal fields;
    \item ranks numeric measurements using mission semantics and coverage;
    \item requires a confidence margin over competing interpretations;
    \item normalizes finite decimal values while preserving row and digest
          provenance; and
    \item abstains on ambiguous schemas, binary evidence, formula-like cells,
          and requested capability escalation.
\end{itemize}

The first challenger was rejected because missing observations produced
excessive uncertainty, formula-like CSV cells failed the property gate, and a
legitimate NOAA institutional subdomain was incorrectly rejected.  The revised
challenger preserved ambiguity abstention while correcting missing-value,
formula, and verified-subdomain behaviour.  It passed six executable
metamorphic properties: field-order invariance, missing-not-zero semantics,
ambiguous-measure abstention, formula non-execution, binary abstention, and
capability-escalation rejection.

\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_recursive_action_language_expansion_v1}} and

A fresh runtime verified this digest and executed the atom without private

it possesses no network, filesystem, process, or arbitrary-code authority.

\subsection{Open compiler micro-operation invention}
\label{subsec:open-micro-operation-invention}

Once temporal projection was available, the next residual occurred inside the
meta-compiler: its micro-operation vocabulary could not express robust trend
estimation.  AION synthesized three private implementations and executed them
in isolated Python processes using a restricted AST.  Imports, filesystem
access, shell execution, reflection, dynamic execution, and unapproved calls
were unavailable.

The retained implementation was the median pairwise trend estimator

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:median-pairwise-trend}
\widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{pair}}
=
\operatorname*{median}_{1\leq i<j\leq n}
\left(\frac{x_j-x_i}{j-i}\right).
\end{equation}

The rejection chain was material to the result.  An endpoint-slope candidate
initially survived an insufficient critic.  A boundary-outlier test exposed its
fragility.  An ordinary least-squares candidate was rejected by the restricted
source validator, while
\texttt{ROBUST\_MEDIAN\_PAIRWISE\_TREND} passed six semantic and resource
properties.  The new operation matched an independently implemented oracle
with zero error on World Bank GDP and transferred to FRED GDP and NOAA
temperature.

The exact source digest
\texttt{\detokenize{78efd13d0873be5aff46cf008afbb5728390d1478265a7cb58d47134a775cda3}}
was precommitted while the compiler registry remained unchanged.  Following
three later authority confirmations, CAU promoted
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_open_micro_operation_invention_v1}} and
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_delayed_micro_operation_compiler_promotion_v1}}.
A cold runtime reconstructed the operation from the registry and verified its
digest without development replay.

\subsection{Failure-driven property-language invention}
\label{subsec:property-language-invention}

The endpoint candidate revealed a deeper problem: improving implementation
synthesis was insufficient if the critic itself could approve a wrong program.
The inherited critic checked translation invariance, constant-series behaviour,
insufficient-data abstention, and deterministic execution.  Endpoint slope
passed those tests but failed the withheld transformation

\begin{equation}
(1,2,3,4,5,6,7)
\longrightarrow
(1,2,3,4,5,6,1000),
\end{equation}

returning $166.5$ when the expected robust trend remained $1$.  AION treated
this as a critic false acceptance rather than merely an implementation defect.
It analysed the changed indices and residual, inferred a sparse boundary
contamination pattern, and rejected three unsuitable criticism strategies:
exact replay because it was instance-specific, global translation because it
did not explain the residual, and permutation invariance because it erased
temporal semantics.

AION then compiled the typed executable property
\texttt{SPARSE\_BOUNDARY\_CONTAMINATION\_STABILITY}.  For candidate program
$f$, sequence $x$, bounded contamination transformation $T$, and robust adjacent
scale $s(x)$, the property requires

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sparse-contamination-property}
\frac{\left|f(T(x))-f(x)\right|}
{\max\!\left\{s(x),\,0.05|f(x)|,\,10^{-12}\right\}}
\leq 0.25.
\end{equation}

The new critic rejected endpoint slope while preserving the median pairwise
trend on World Bank and FRED series.  It then transferred across operator
family: on NOAA temperature and Treasury exchange-rate series, it rejected the
arithmetic mean while preserving a robust median.  This matters because the
retained object is an executable failure-detection method rather than a stored
expected answer for the original trend example.

The declarative critic language is capability classified.  Eight hostile
property programs requesting network, filesystem, process, shell, external
oracle, unbounded-perturbation, invalid-tolerance, or embedded-code behaviour
were rejected.  The property digest
\texttt{\detokenize{bd108fd178547e9087146b53cea7c3d14e02ec9186ca17b5d9dbe8e9ec8cda30}}
was committed while the runtime critic registry remained unchanged.  Four later
public re-executions reproduced both fragile-strategy rejection and
robust-strategy preservation.  CAU consequently promoted
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_open_property_language_invention_v1}} and
\texttt{\detokenize{procedure_delayed_property_critic_runtime_promotion_v1}}.

A fresh critic runtime made all four expected decisions without private
synthesis state: endpoint slope was rejected with normalized instability
$165.5$; pairwise trend was accepted with instability $0$; arithmetic mean was
rejected with instability $141.857143$; and robust median was accepted with
instability $0$.

\subsection{Interpretation and remaining boundary}
\label{subsec:recursive-invention-boundary}

The principal advance is a shift in what AION retains.  Earlier stages could
retain answers, endpoints, or successful methods.  The present sequence retains
dependency graphs, investigation operators, runtime atoms, compiler
micro-operations, and executable critics.  In compact form,

\begin{equation}
\text{use machinery}
\rightarrow \text{compose machinery}
\rightarrow \text{invent machinery}
\rightarrow \text{extend machinery}
\rightarrow \text{invent criticism of machinery}.
\end{equation}

These results remain bounded.  The institutional missions, authority adapters,
typed AST interpreter, restricted source grammar, public cohorts, independent
oracles, and property meta-grammar retain engineered components.  The evidence
does not establish unrestricted research agency, arbitrary self-programming,
autonomous general apprenticeship, or AGI.  The next removal target is
\emph{open critic-objective invention}: when neither a failure category nor an
expected invariant is supplied, AION must determine which behavioural risk
deserves falsification, construct the corresponding diagnostic objective, and
retain it only after source-disjoint and delayed consequences.
