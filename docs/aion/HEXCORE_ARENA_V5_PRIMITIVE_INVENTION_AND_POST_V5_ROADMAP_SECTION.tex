\section{Primitive Invention and the Post-v5 Roadmap}
\label{sec:arena-v5-and-roadmap}

Arena v5 addresses the principal remaining limitation of Arena v4.  Arena v4
could criticise its complete program grammar and abstain, but it could not
construct a new executable primitive when every program in that grammar failed.
Arena v5 introduces residual-driven primitive construction while retaining the
same separation between proposal and authority.

The internally promoted procedure is
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize
\texttt{procedure\_residual\_driven\_primitive\_invention\_v5\_35b31ad85c20}}.
\end{center}
Its parent is
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize
\texttt{procedure\_open\_experiment\_program\_arena\_v4\_7b66f2843ca9}}.
\end{center}


\subsection{Research question and authority contract}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-question}

The experiment asks whether AION can detect systematic residual structure
outside its retained action grammar, construct a private executable
representation, actively falsify that representation, limit its complexity,
transfer it to renamed projects, and abstain when the enlarged meta-grammar is
still inadequate.

The governed pipeline is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{retained Arena v4 grammar exhausted}
&\rightarrow \text{structured residual detected} \\
&\rightarrow \text{private model-family challengers} \\
&\rightarrow \text{maximum-disagreement queries} \\
&\rightarrow \text{parameter fitting and complexity pressure} \\
&\rightarrow \text{held-out validation} \\
&\rightarrow \text{security and complexity scan} \\
&\rightarrow \text{independent-process execution} \\
&\rightarrow \text{source-disjoint transfer} \\
&\rightarrow \text{CAU promotion, rollback, or abstention}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v5-pipeline}
\end{equation}

The previously retained grammar contained raw, mean, median, clipping, and lag
operations.  It could not express either the nonlinear development response or
the state-carrying temporal response.  Neural or language proposals could
suggest a representation, but could not accept it, execute forbidden effects,
or extend the retained primitive library.


\subsection{Invented executable primitives}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-primitives}

\subsubsection{Nonlinear polynomial primitive}

AION selected a fifth-degree polynomial implementation with identifier
\begin{center}
\texttt{primitive\_da3806a71ff8561d}.
\end{center}
The learned approximation was
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
y(x)={}&-3.40\times 10^{-17}
       +0.997718x
       +1.67\times 10^{-16}x^2 \\
     &-0.162831x^3
       -3.81\times 10^{-17}x^4
       +0.006727x^5.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v5-polynomial}
\end{equation}
The near-zero even coefficients were learned rather than imposed.  The
implementation remained within the declared eight-parameter ceiling.


\subsubsection{State-carrying affine primitive}

A second primitive, identified as
\begin{center}
\texttt{primitive\_169b31b28601ccaf},
\end{center}
learned the recursive transition
\begin{equation}
y_t=0.15+0.55x_t+0.65y_{t-1}.
\label{eq:arena-v5-stateful}
\end{equation}
This is not a static input--output curve: the primitive explicitly stores and
updates a continuous internal state.


\subsection{Active falsification and model selection}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-falsification}

Private challengers included polynomial models of degrees one, three, five,
and seven, together with a stateful affine model.  At each investigation round,
AION selected a query on which the three strongest candidates disagreed most.
Incorrect candidates were recorded as concrete counterexamples rather than
collapsed into a generic failure label.

Model selection minimized
\begin{equation}
J(h)=\operatorname{MSE}_{\mathrm{train}}(h)
     +10^{-6}C(h),
\label{eq:arena-v5-mdl}
\end{equation}
where $C(h)$ is the number of executable parameters.  This minimum-description
pressure prevented a higher-degree polynomial from winning for a negligible
training-only improvement.

Only two confirmation queries were allowed for each sealed transfer world.
The remaining cases were revealed to the independent executable verifier only
after selection.


\subsection{Independent executable verification}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-execution}

Accepted representations were serialized as data and passed to a fresh,
isolated Python interpreter that did not import the fitting implementation.
The interpreter admitted only two executable schemas:
\begin{itemize}
  \item polynomial evaluation; and
  \item stateful affine recurrence.
\end{itemize}

Filesystem access, network access, dynamic execution, and authority writes were
absent from the executable schema.  A valid fitted representation without a
passing security schema could not reach the interpreter.

\subsection{Meta-grammar criticism, security, and persistence}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-criticism}

A discontinuous sign-response world was deliberately outside the polynomial
acceptance envelope and the stateful-affine explanation.  The best challenger
produced validation RMSE $0.206$ against a maximum permitted value of $0.015$.
Arena v5 rejected the challenger and abstained.  Primitive invention therefore
did not become permission to retain the least-bad available approximation.

Four malicious proposals requested dynamic Python execution, network access,
filesystem writing, or authority override.  All four failed the schema and
security gate before execution.  The primitive implementations, hashes,
authority records, generation record, and champion survived full runtime
reconstruction with zero relearning.


\subsection{Capability progression and interpretation}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-progression}

The operational progression across the three latest arenas is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{Arena v3:}&\quad \text{learn when a complete repair is required},\\
\text{Arena v4:}&\quad \text{compose a new action from known primitives},\\
\text{Arena v5:}&\quad \text{construct a new primitive when known primitives fail}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:arena-v3-v5-progression}
\end{equation}

This is a bounded representation-learning advance.  The system is no longer
restricted to recombining the Arena v4 vocabulary for the tested continuous
families.  It does not establish unrestricted representation invention: the
polynomial and stateful-affine meta-families, query pool, controlled outcomes,
parameter ceilings, and evaluator remained engineered.

\label{subsec:post-v5-agi-assessment}

An external review correctly identified several important AGI gaps but assessed
the older Arena v2 system.  Arena v3--v5 change the current position as follows.

\begin{center}

A numerical placement on a ``100-step AGI ladder'' is not a reproducible
scientific measurement and is not adopted.  Progress is instead measured using
declared capability gates, component ablations, independent outcomes, transfer,
retention, and external evaluation.


\subsection{Refreshed remaining research programme}
\label{subsec:post-v5-priorities}

Programming, causal reasoning, semantic memory, evidence acquisition, changing
projects, program composition, and bounded primitive invention now operate as
capabilities within the integrated arena.  They are not separate critical paths
unless a broad arena exposes a specific regression.

The remaining priorities are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Independently owned delayed outcomes.} Connect projects to
        externally maintained repository tests, independently changing
        databases or services, later public observations, independently
        authored document questions, and human judgments where execution
        cannot define correctness.

  \item \textbf{Natural multimodal and physical grounding.} Integrate real
        images, diagrams, layouts, charts, tables, audio, spatial state,
        uncertain sensors, delayed actuation, object persistence, and resource
        constraints.  Filename and pixel-statistic shortcuts are insufficient.

  \item \textbf{Human commonsense, social, creative, and multi-agent
        intelligence.} Use human-authored situations and several independent
        raters.  Retain legitimate disagreement; measure perspective,
        incentives, norms, consent, manipulation, negotiation, novelty,
        usefulness, and consistency.  The objective is controlled competence,
        not self-preservation or authority seeking.

  \item \textbf{Long-lived continual intelligence.} Expand from four
        milestones to hours- or days-long projects and eventually thousands of
        changing tasks.  Measure forward transfer, backward retention,
        calibration, weakest-family performance, cost, complexity, retirement,
        revival, and component replacement.

  \item \textbf{Open tool, API, and environment acquisition.} Discover public
        interfaces, infer types and side effects, construct private adapters in
        disposable environments, and retain them only after security,
        behavioral, and source-disjoint transfer gates.  Credentials, payments,
        deployment, and irreversible effects remain approval-gated.

  \item \textbf{Safe online plasticity.} Immediate learning occurs in episodic,
        semantic, causal, and skill memory.  Private neural challengers learn
        only from provenance-bearing verified outcomes and must pass replay,
        forgetting, calibration, weakest-family, and unsafe-acceptance gates
        before reversible promotion.

  \item \textbf{Replaceable substrate tournament.} Compare suitable
        $500$M--$3$B and stronger proposal substrates under identical HexCore
        evidence, tool, action, compute, outcome, and CAU budgets.

  \item \textbf{Native cognitive consolidation.} Train replaceable AION-native
        proposal and retrieval components from verified trajectories without
        allowing neural plausibility to become accepted knowledge.

  \item \textbf{Independent external administration.} An external evaluator
        must own hidden portfolios, solutions, outcomes, budgets, and scoring,
        including matched AION ablations, frontier agents, open systems,
        specialist learners, and human baselines.
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{Dual-timescale plasticity contract}
\label{subsec:dual-timescale-plasticity}

The roadmap does not equate intelligence with unrestricted live
backpropagation.  Production weights must not rewrite themselves from
unverified experience.  The required design is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{observed experience}
&\rightarrow \text{immediate structured memory} \\
&\rightarrow \text{verified outcome and provenance} \\
&\rightarrow \text{private neural challenger} \\
&\rightarrow \text{replay and backward-retention audit} \\
&\rightarrow \text{calibration, safety, and weakest-family gates} \\
&\rightarrow \text{reversible CAU promotion or rejection}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:dual-timescale-plasticity}
\end{equation}

This preserves rapid experience-dependent learning without allowing a single
unverified event to rotate or corrupt the production representation.


\subsection{Evidence required for an AGI claim}
\label{subsec:agi-evidence-gates}

No phase count, internal score, or architecture feature can authorize an AGI
claim.  Credible evidence would require all of the following under independent
administration:
\begin{itemize}
  \item broad transfer across unfamiliar language, research, code,
        mathematics, science, physical, social, and creative domains;
  \item autonomous decomposition and recovery on long projects without a
        hidden supplied workflow;
  \item learning from independently owned consequences with positive forward
        transfer;
  \item continual improvement without serious backward forgetting;
  \item robust natural multimodal and physical grounding;
  \item calibrated social judgment with legitimate disagreement retained;
  \item invention of tools and representations beyond development task menus;
  \item safe abstention and human escalation under uncertainty;
  \item matched resource comparisons with frontier agents and human baselines;
        and
  \item reproducibility by an evaluator independent of system and task
        development.
\end{itemize}

Arena v5 supplies evidence for bounded primitive invention and transfer only.
It does not satisfy these broader AGI gates.  The immediate critical path is
independent outcome authority rather than additional internally generated
$100\%$ cohorts.

\section{AGI Evidence Authority Registry and External Certification Pathway}
\label{sec:agi-evidence-authority-registry}

The post-v5 roadmap identifies independent evidence authority as the principal
remaining credibility constraint.  AION therefore implements an executable
registry that maps retained capability artifacts to the ten AGI evidence gates
while preserving the distinction among internal sealed evaluation,
independently maintained public outcomes, and independently administered
certification.  The promoted control-plane procedure is
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize
\texttt{procedure\_agi\_evidence\_authority\_registry\_v1\_9b6e63c112a4}}.
\end{center}
Promotion applies to the evidence-control mechanism only.  The AGI claim flag
remains false.


\subsection{Evidence and authority architecture}
\label{subsec:agi-registry-architecture}

The registry executes the following chain:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{retained capability artifacts}
&\rightarrow \text{SHA-256 commitments and field contracts} \\
&\rightarrow \text{authority classification} \\
&\rightarrow \text{ten explicit AGI evidence gates} \\
&\rightarrow \text{frozen external-evaluator handoff} \\
&\rightarrow \text{signed result verification} \\
&\rightarrow \text{CAU acceptance or continued denial}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:agi-registry-pipeline}
\end{equation}

Twelve source artifacts are committed.  Each registry row stores the artifact
path, cryptographic digest, authority class, required result fields, declared
claim boundary, and integrity result.  The authority taxonomy is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}=\{
  \text{internal sealed},
  \text{externally sourced public},
  \text{externally administered}
\}.
\label{eq:agi-authority-classes}
\end{equation}
Evidence may move between classes only when the corresponding authority
requirements are actually satisfied.  Public data are therefore not promoted
to external certification merely because AION did not create the underlying
source.

\subsection{Frozen evaluator contract}
\label{subsec:agi-evaluator-contract}

The external handoff requires eight portfolio families:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item unfamiliar language and long-document research;
  \item mathematics and formal reasoning;
  \item source-disjoint software engineering;
  \item scientific model invention and prediction;
  \item natural multimodal and physical grounding;
  \item long-horizon changing projects;
  \item social, commonsense, and multi-agent judgment; and
  \item creative synthesis with independent raters.
\end{enumerate}

Every portfolio must be evaluated using matched information, tool, compute,
and time budgets.  Required systems are complete AION; AION without accumulated
memory; AION without its causal world model; AION without neural proposals;
AION without HexCore verification where that ablation can be run safely; a
frontier agent; a strong open agent; and qualified humans.

Before AION receives a task, the evaluator must freeze commitments to the
portfolio, delayed outcomes, human-rater panel, matched budgets, and answer
reveals.  The audit then reports mean and weakest-family performance, forward
transfer, backward retention, unsafe acceptance, abstention, human escalation,
restart recovery, component replacement, source overlap, and complete execution
logs.  The development process is explicitly forbidden from self-certifying
this contract.


\subsection{Cryptographic result return}
\label{subsec:agi-signed-return}

External results are returned in a signed
\texttt{aion.external.agi\_evidence\_attestation.v1} envelope.  Let $M$ denote
the canonical JSON encoding of the complete envelope excluding its signature,
and let $sk_E$ and $pk_E$ be the evaluator's Ed25519 private and public keys.
The evaluator publishes
\begin{equation}
\sigma_E=\operatorname{Ed25519.Sign}_{sk_E}(M),
\qquad
\operatorname{Ed25519.Verify}_{pk_E}(M,\sigma_E)=\mathrm{true}.
\label{eq:agi-ed25519-attestation}
\end{equation}

The verifier rejects the envelope if any result changes after signing, if a
required portfolio or comparison is absent, if any audit flag fails, if any of
the ten evidence gates is missing, or if reproduction artifacts and logs are
incomplete.  A signature authenticates the submitted statement; it cannot by
itself prove that the evaluator is institutionally independent or that its
measurements are scientifically valid.  Those properties still require public
audit and reproduction.

\subsection{Resulting execution order and claim boundary}
\label{subsec:agi-registry-next-work}

The remaining programme is now ordered by missing evidence rather than phase
count:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item independently administered hidden evaluation;
  \item independent human social, commonsense, and creative assessment;
  \item natural multimodal and physical interaction beyond engineered files;
  \item multi-hour and multi-day projects against externally changing systems;
  \item open acquisition of unfamiliar tools, APIs, and environments;
  \item longer continual generations with externally owned replay cohorts;
  \item matched whole-system comparison with frontier agents and qualified
        humans; and
  \item published independent reproduction.
\end{enumerate}

This stage establishes a persistent evidence and authority control plane.  It
shows that AION can consolidate its evidence, preserve provenance and authority
classes, prepare a machine-verifiable external handoff, and continue denying an
unsupported AGI claim.  It does not establish AGI, human-level generality,
contamination-free superiority, independent reproducibility, or external human
acceptance.  Those claims remain closed by construction until the corresponding
outside evidence exists.
