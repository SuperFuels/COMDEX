# AION Open Objective-Family Invention

## Breakthrough

AION has moved beyond choosing among the five development-defined useful-work
families. From two later-confirmed situated projects it detected a residual
structure that none of the original labels represented adequately and retained
a new family: `situated_operational_resilience`.

Promoted procedure: `procedure_open_objective_family_invention_v1`.

The invented family means: maintain useful operation under changing
independently observed authorities by appraising context, composing retained
capabilities, diagnosing origin and revising only the affected model.

## Retained machinery

```text
appraise situation
  -> bind evidenced capabilities
  -> precommit expected envelope
  -> observe later multi-authority outcome
  -> diagnose change origin
  -> rank the response
  -> return fresh evidence to participating domains
```

Its outcome authority is a later multi-authority public consequence. The
development proof used software authorities (CPython, Node and NumPy), while the
source-disjoint transfer used the Madrid environmental authority.

The tournament rejected a single-source monitor that could not transfer and an
always-repair policy that blamed AION when the external world changed. The
situated-resilience executor was the unique safe winner.

available public outcomes remain engineered. Future evidence must show repeated
