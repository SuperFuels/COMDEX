\section{Minimum Remaining Work for Production-Complete Sales}
\label{sec:tessaris-sales-minimum-remaining-work}

\subsection{Current Position}

The core Tessaris Sales architecture is substantially complete.  The canonical
customer relationship management ledger, opportunity pipeline, consent model,
qualification system, governed Retell voice agent, appointment preparation,
quotations, Finance and Operations hand-offs, email drafting, exact approvals,
outcome evidence and Home Fixed intake routes have been implemented.

The remaining work is therefore not another fundamental Sales redesign.  It is
a bounded production-completion and real-world validation sprint.  Sales must
not be described as fully autonomous or production-complete until the external
communication loops and the minimum evidence in this section have been closed.

\subsection{Minimum Production-Completion Requirements}

\subsubsection{Gmail Reply Correlation and Continued Qualification}

Incoming Gmail replies must be correlated to the existing canonical contact,
opportunity, prior message and frozen call-goal contract.  A reply must not
create a duplicate lead merely because the customer changed the email subject
or replied from the same thread after a delay.

The connector must:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item use authenticated Gmail thread and message identifiers where available;
  \item match the sender to the canonical contact without trusting the display name;
  \item preserve the original opportunity and call-goal hash;
  \item extract only fields permitted by the frozen qualification contract;
  \item retain earlier confirmed facts and ask only the next missing questions;
  \item store the original message as source evidence without altering Gmail; and
  \item route ambiguity, conflicting facts, attachments and identity uncertainty to a person.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} a real reply to an approved Home Fixed
draft must update the same opportunity, preserve the goal-contract hash, avoid
duplicate creation and prepare the correct next question without sending it.

\subsubsection{Approved Email Sending Policy}

The current governed path can create a Gmail draft but does not automatically
send it.  Production completion requires an explicit per-business communication
policy supporting at least the following modes:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{draft only}: a person reviews and sends in Gmail;
  \item \textbf{exact approval}: Tessaris sends only the exact payload separately approved by an authorised person; and
  \item \textbf{bounded automation}: only pre-approved templates and low-risk purposes may send automatically within configured limits.
\end{enumerate}

Automatic or exact-approved execution must record the approving identity,
payload hash, provider message identifier, delivery state and failure state.
Replies, complaints, unsubscribe requests and delivery failures must return to
the same canonical opportunity.

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} one exact-approved email must be sent to
an owner-controlled address, read back from the provider, received successfully
and recorded with the correct immutable payload hash.  No altered or stale draft
may execute.

\subsubsection{WhatsApp Business Conversation and Evidence Intake}

WhatsApp is a high-value channel for photographs, short videos, locations and
customer conversation, particularly for trade and field-service businesses.
The implementation must use an official WhatsApp Business provider or API and
must not automate a personal WhatsApp account.

The connector must support:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item business-specific credentials and telephone identity;
  \item opt-in and permitted conversation-window evidence;
  \item approved templates where required by the provider;
  \item inbound and outbound message correlation to the canonical opportunity;
  \item protected storage of images and documents with provenance;
  \item delivery and failure receipts;
  \item human escalation for unsafe, abusive, ambiguous or unsupported content; and
  \item the same prohibition on invented price, booking, payment and dispatch commitments.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} an owner-controlled WhatsApp exchange
must attach an image and reply to the correct Sales opportunity, preserve the
goal contract and create no unapproved commercial consequence.

\subsubsection{Real Voice Robustness Evaluation}

The successful owner-controlled Retell call established that the voice quality
and basic conversation path are credible.  Production completion requires a
broader frozen examination rather than relying on one excellent call.

The minimum evaluation cohort should include:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item different English and Spanish accents relevant to the target market;
  \item interruptions, silence, talking over the agent and corrections;
  \item background noise and weak telephone connections;
  \item confused, impatient and objection-raising customers;
  \item unsupported price, availability and guarantee questions;
  \item requests for a person and urgent safety concerns;
  \item voicemail, busy, no-answer and dropped-call outcomes; and
  \item deliberate attempts to move the agent outside its business scope.
\end{itemize}

Each call must be scored for disclosure, accuracy, goal completion, repetition,
latency, interruption recovery, prohibited commitments, escalation quality and
customer experience.  Provider transcripts are evidence, not self-issued proof
of success.

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} a precommitted cohort of controlled calls
must meet the agreed quality and safety thresholds with no severe unsupported
commitment and with every failed case documented for correction.

\subsubsection{Complete Real Customer Journey}

At least one genuine, consented Home Fixed enquiry must traverse the complete
commercial path:

\begin{quote}
enquiry $\rightarrow$ qualification $\rightarrow$ call or written continuation
$\rightarrow$ human-reviewed booking $\rightarrow$ quotation
$\rightarrow$ accepted, won or independently recorded lost outcome.
\end{quote}

The test must preserve source attribution, contact permission, conversation
evidence, appointment authority, quotation approval and the final outcome
reference.  A favourable result is not required; a genuine lost or no-response
outcome is valid evidence if it is recorded honestly.

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} one complete real-world trace must be
reproducible from canonical records without relying on an internal narrative or
manually reconstructed chronology.

\subsubsection{Controlled Outbound Prospecting}

Outbound prospect lists and campaigns remain a separate authority class from
responding to inbound enquiries.  Production completion requires:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item lawful source and permission evidence for every contact;
  \item suppression, do-not-contact and unsubscribe enforcement;
  \item per-business and per-channel volume limits;
  \item approved audience, message, script and calling window;
  \item exact separation between cold, warm and existing-customer treatment;
  \item campaign pause, complaint and human-takeover controls;
  \item recorded provider outcomes and conversion evidence; and
  \item control-versus-challenger comparison under matched budgets.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} an owner-controlled pilot campaign must
pass preflight, exclude deliberately ineligible contacts, execute only the
approved audience and record delivery or call outcomes without contacting a
suppressed person.

\subsubsection{External CRM Adapters}

Tessaris remains the canonical Sales operating model.  HubSpot and later CRM
connectors are adapters for businesses that retain an existing system; they are
not prerequisites for the Home Fixed proof deployment.

The first HubSpot adapter should support contact, company, opportunity and stage
mapping; external identifier storage; idempotent imports; bounded exports;
conflict detection; and an explicit direction-of-authority policy.  It must not
silently overwrite a newer Tessaris or provider record.

\textbf{Minimum acceptance evidence:} a sandbox or owner-controlled CRM must
round-trip a representative contact and opportunity without duplication,
authority loss or silent field conflict.  Additional CRM vendors should reuse
the same adapter contract rather than introduce vendor-specific business logic.

\subsection{Definition of Production-Complete}

Sales may be labelled production-complete for a defined business only when all
of the following statements are supported by external or provider-backed evidence:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item genuine enquiries enter the canonical opportunity ledger without duplication;
  \item telephone and written conversations continue the same frozen goals;
  \item an authorised communication can be delivered and independently read back;
  \item incoming replies and evidence return to the correct opportunity;
  \item a real journey can reach a human-reviewed booking, quotation and final outcome;
  \item the voice agent remains safe and useful across the agreed evaluation cohort;
  \item failures, complaints and human requests reliably stop or redirect automation;
  \item every external consequence identifies its authority, exact payload and provider receipt; and
  \item no dashboard claim depends solely on self-scoring or an internal counter.
\end{enumerate}

This definition is business-scoped.  Home Fixed can reach production completion
without first implementing every CRM provider, language, country or outbound
sales strategy.  Wider connectors and markets expand product coverage after the
reference workflow is proven.

\subsection{Recommended Programme Decision}

The appropriate immediate decision is to move primary development attention to
the next business department while retaining Sales as a controlled operational
foundation.  When genuine Home Fixed enquiries arrive, a focused Sales
production-validation sprint should execute the work packages above in priority
order.

The honest summary is:

\begin{quote}
The Sales brain, CRM, voice, workflow and safety system are built.  What remains
is production-channel closure and real-world validation, not another major
architectural build.
\end{quote}
