\section{Continuous Natural-World Learning and Outcome-Driven Revision}
\label{sec:continuous-world-revision}

\subsection{Objective}

The post-Phase-63 representation programme was extended from discrete hidden
state to continuous causal state.  AION receives timestamped natural event
records containing measurement noise, missing non-critical readings and local
coreference.  It is not supplied with the number of entities, state dimensions,
hidden regimes, persistent properties, causal edges or intervention
coefficients.

\subsection{Continuous world induction}

The learner:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item parses observations and interventions;
  \item resolves references such as ``that unit'' and ``the same channel'';
  \item invents entity and continuous-dimension symbols;
  \item reconstructs before, immediate and settled state;
  \item separates persistent from transient effects;
  \item clusters recurring action-effect signatures into hidden regimes;
  \item infers independent, coupled or fan-out topology;
  \item estimates signed continuous coefficients;
  \item predicts withheld future effects;
  \item criticizes coefficient instability outside the model family;
  \item compiles a proposal-only Photon symbolic-numeric model.
\end{enumerate}

The operational proposal has the form
\[
  s_t + a_t + z_t
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  \Delta s_{t+1},
\]
where $z_t$ is an inferred recurring regime and the components of
$\Delta s_{t+1}$ carry learned real-valued coefficients.

\subsection{Outcome-driven model revision}

The next track uses independently revealed transition outcomes to distinguish a
representation error from a changed quantitative rule.  The retained stationary
model predicts each transition; HexCore records signature and coefficient
residuals; private challengers compare stationary and piecewise explanations;
and a revised model is tested once on withheld future episodes.

The piecewise challenger is accepted only when:
\[
  1 -
  \frac{\mathrm{SSE}_{\mathrm{piecewise}}}
       {\mathrm{SSE}_{\mathrm{stationary}}}
  \geq 0.50
\]
and the estimated scale change is at least $0.18$.  An alternating regime that
does not support one durable change point must cause abstention.

\subsection{Continual retention}

Three source-disjoint generations produced 1,044 future prediction tasks.
Every generation passed its current-family and protected-family gates, final
backward retention was 100\%, and the complete revision history survived
restart without outcome replay.

\subsection{Authority and claim boundary}

Photon is a traceable proposal representation.  The withheld outcome generator
verifies consequences, HexCore assigns failure credit and CAU controls
promotion.  Neural or symbolic plausibility is never accepted as truth by
itself.

The record grammar, finite actions, piecewise-linear model family, abrupt
change assumption and simulator authority remain engineered.  These experiments
do not establish unrestricted scientific discovery or AGI.  The next research
frontier is invention of nonlinear, delayed and latent-continuous operators when
the existing model library fails, followed by evaluation against real software,
documents and independently collected sensor outcomes.
