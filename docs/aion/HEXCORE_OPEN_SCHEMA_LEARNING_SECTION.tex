\section{Phase 41: Open-Schema Learning and Explanatory Graphs}
\label{sec:phase41-open-schema}

\subsection{Objective}

Phase 41 tests whether AION can construct a relation schema rather than receive
a fixed ontology.  The solver observes unfamiliar documents, symbols,
relation wording, and decision contracts.  Evaluator-only data includes the
expected schemas, goal operators, missing relation, and final answer; none is
available to the solver.

\subsection{Structural Schema Invention}

Each conditional relation is represented by an induced structural signature
\[
\sigma(r)=
\left(
o_{\mathrm{antecedent}},
n_{\mathrm{antecedent}},
n_{\mathrm{consequence}},
d_{\mathrm{temporal}}
\right).
\]
The canonical hash of \(\sigma(r)\) becomes a persistent invented schema
identifier.  No supplied label such as ``enables'' or ``requires'' is needed.
Three schemas emerged: unary conjunctive implication, binary all-required
implication, and binary either-sufficient implication.  All three transferred
from three development domains into six source-disjoint sealed domains.

\subsection{Goal and Explanation Induction}

Decision prose is independently converted into all-of, any-of,
\(k\)-of-\(n\), or ordered goal structures.  Forward inference begins with
cited facts at depth zero.  Each applied relation stores its schema,
antecedents, consequence, proof depth, source, and provenance.  Inference
continues until a fixed point is reached.  Ordered goals additionally require
strictly increasing proof depths.

\subsection{Missing Knowledge and Epistemic Control}

For an unsupported requirement, AION recursively searches for producing
relations and unresolved antecedents.  A leaf with no producer becomes a
specific missing-knowledge query.  Conversely, a direct observation of the
same entity in a conflicting state establishes falsity rather than absence.
Thus the operational classification is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{complete proof path} &\Rightarrow \text{supported},\\
\text{verified conflicting state} &\Rightarrow \text{unsupported},\\
\text{no evidence path} &\Rightarrow \text{unknown and clarify}.
\end{aligned}
\]

\subsection{Promotion and Boundary}

All capability, weakest-domain, missing-knowledge, provenance, CAU, and
restart gates passed.  The promoted procedure is
\begin{center}
\small\texttt{procedure\_open\_schema\_learning\_1d0456fd3c9f}
\end{center}

This remains bounded open-schema learning.  The prose grammar, binary
propositions, conditional operator family, goal operators, documents, and
oracle remain engineered.  AION invents and validates structural signatures;
it does not yet perform unrestricted ontology invention, arbitrary scientific
discovery, general language understanding, or AGI.
