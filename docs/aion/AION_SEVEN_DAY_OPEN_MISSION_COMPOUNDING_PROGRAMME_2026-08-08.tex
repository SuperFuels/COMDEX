\section{Purpose and Research Question}
\label{sec:purpose}

The seven-day open-mission programme marks a deliberate change in AION's
development strategy.  Technical AGA v1 is frozen as a completed technical
baseline.  The programme no longer treats additional internal project counts,
curriculum receipts, or repeated passes in the same task family as evidence of
increasing generality.  Its purpose is to test whether AION's governed memory,
repair, retention, authority separation, and accumulated experience produce a
measurable advantage over the same underlying proposer without those
mechanisms.

The primary research question is:
\begin{quote}
After controlling the proposer, tools, and action budget, does full AION solve
unfamiliar consequential missions more successfully or more efficiently than
proposer-only, cold, no-memory, and no-repair controls; and are any gains
retained after sources close and confirmed by later independent outcomes?
\end{quote}

The intended compounding chain is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{unfamiliar mission}
&\rightarrow \text{frozen success contract}
\rightarrow \text{matched five-arm execution} \\
&\rightarrow \text{independent later consequence}
\rightarrow \text{failure attribution} \\
&\rightarrow \text{governed repair or abstention}
\rightarrow \text{source closure} \\
&\rightarrow \text{seven-day protected transfer}
\rightarrow \text{harder next mission}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:compounding-chain}
\end{equation}

This is a path-to-evidence experiment, not an automatic AGI declaration.  The
campaign contract explicitly fixes \texttt{agi\_claim\_authorized} to false.

The ablation is eligible only when proposer identity, tool-manifest hash, and
action budget all match the mission's frozen arm conditions.  A stronger model
cannot be substituted into one arm.  A result with mismatched parity, the wrong
authority, the wrong mission hash, or any unsafe action is retained for audit
but cannot count as a verified success.

For outcome receipt $r$, eligibility is
\begin{equation}
E(r)=P(r)\land A(r)\land C(r)\land S(r),
\label{eq:eligibility}
\end{equation}
where $P$ is cohort parity, $A$ is evaluator-authority validity, $C$ is
precommitment validity, and $S$ means zero unsafe actions.  Verified success is
therefore
\begin{equation}
V(r)=\operatorname{success}(r)\land E(r).
\label{eq:verified-success}
\end{equation}

\section{Mission Contract and Lifecycle}
\label{sec:mission-lifecycle}

Every mission must declare, before execution:
\begin{itemize}
    \item a unique mission identifier and authorized lane;
    \item an explicit objective;
    \item an evaluator that is not AION, the proposal component, or self;
    \item a success contract frozen before execution;
    \item a source policy that permits a genuine closed-source retention test;
    \item a risk class and bounded execution environment;
    \item the fixed proposer and matched arm conditions; and
    \item a mission hash binding the above fields.
\end{itemize}

The lifecycle is
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Register and freeze.}  The governor validates the mission and
          freezes the success criteria before AION sees an outcome.
    \item \textbf{Execute matched arms.}  The executor runs each arm under its
          identical budget and tool boundary.
    \item \textbf{Reveal independent authority.}  Hidden integration tests,
          an append-only later ledger row, or another declared authority
          produces the consequence.
    \item \textbf{Record receipts.}  The governor verifies parity, authority,
          precommitment, safety, success, work, actions, and intervention.
    \item \textbf{Close sources.}  Training or solution sources become
          unavailable and the seven-day retention clock begins.
    \item \textbf{Confirm repair later.}  A repair is credited only after a
          separate later authority confirms its consequence.
    \item \textbf{Run protected retention.}  After seven elapsed days, AION
          must transfer the method without reopening the original source.
    \item \textbf{Escalate.}  The next mission should remove scaffolding,
          change the schema or environment, increase horizon, or introduce a
          new authority family.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Implemented Mission Authorities}
\label{sec:authorities}

\subsection{Answer-hidden polyglot systems fault}

The first completed software/systems cohort uses a disposable transactional
event service.  A malformed JSON event stops a Node consumer.  The mission
requires valid events to continue, invalid evidence to remain available for
audit, the active path not to encounter the same invalid event repeatedly, and
no network or dynamic execution.

The candidate strategies are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item accept the event and continue;
    \item discard the unreadable event; or
    \item validate, quarantine, preserve, remove from the active path, and
          continue safely.
\end{itemize}

The evaluator, not the proposer, owns the SQLite state, quarantine evidence,
continuity checks, and Node-consumer result.  Only the third method satisfies
both safe continuation and evidence preservation.  Full AION reached the
verified method in two total investigation actions; the no-memory arm required
four.  Proposer-only and cold AION failed this cohort.

This cohort establishes real matched-execution plumbing and a measurable
memory effect.  It is still one induced, answer-hidden systems family and does
not by itself establish broad open-world intelligence.

\subsection{Rolling later-public-change authority}

The open-research loop observes an append-only Arena v15 ledger containing
changes in monitored public sources.  At cursor $t$, each arm sees only history
up to $t$ and freezes a prediction for row $t+1$.  The next row is not visible
when the prediction commitment is hashed.

For prediction set $P$ and later observed change set $O$, the independent
score is Jaccard similarity,
\begin{equation}
J(P,O)=\frac{|P\cap O|}{|P\cup O|},
\label{eq:jaccard}
\end{equation}
with the empty-union case scored as one.  The present success threshold is
$J\geq 0.5$, and the revealed Arena row must also report safe outcomes,
successful execution, and a successful transaction.

The first completed public cohort froze cursor 105 before row 106 existed.
Row 106 later revealed \codepath{open_meteo_madrid}; its outcome hash was:

Full AION and the no-memory/no-repair arms scored $0.5$; proposer-only and cold
AION scored $1/3$ and failed the $0.5$ contract.  Immediately after scoring,
the executor froze a new commitment at cursor 106:

The no-gap commitment rule matters.  After a row is revealed and scored, the
executor creates the next commitment before another future row is available.
This prevents retrospective prediction and keeps the later row genuinely
unseen.

\subsection{Precommitted repository-repair mission}

The campaign also registers a repository mission requiring diagnosis and

At the document snapshot this mission is precommitted but not yet a completed

\section{Retention Protocol}
\label{sec:retention}

Source closure is a distinct state transition.  When a mission's learning
source closes, the governor records
\begin{equation}
t_{\mathrm{due}}=t_{\mathrm{close}}+7\ \mathrm{days}.
\end{equation}
An early pass cannot mature.  A pass that accesses the original source cannot
mature.  A retention receipt is verified only if
\begin{equation}
R=\mathbf{1}\!\left[
t\geq t_{\mathrm{due}}
\land A_{\mathrm{retention}}
\land \mathrm{pass}
\land \neg\mathrm{sourceAccessed}
\right].
\label{eq:retention}
\end{equation}

The first protected test is due on 15 August 2026 at approximately 21:49 CEST,
about 23 minutes after the nominal campaign end.  The first public-change
cohort's retention is due at approximately 22:44 CEST.  Therefore the final
honest campaign verdict must be issued after the applicable protected tests,
not exactly when the seven-day execution timer ends.

The retained challenge should be renamed or structurally altered, use no
training replay, and record relearning actions explicitly.  Passing the same
literal example from memory is weaker evidence than reconstructing and applying
the method to a source-disjoint transfer.

\section{Repair Protocol}
\label{sec:repair}

The campaign accepts two fault classes:
\begin{description}
    \item[Natural:] a real dependency, CI, API, schema, protocol, or operational
          change not created to provide an answer;
    \item[Induced but answer-hidden:] a fault transparently committed before
          solver execution whose answer is not placed in proposer context.
\end{description}

An induced fault is eligible only if it was committed before solver start.
Every repair additionally requires a later independent confirmation and a
confirmation authority that is not AION, self, or the proposal component:
\begin{equation}
Q=F_{\mathrm{precommitted}}\land C_{\mathrm{later}}
\land A_{\mathrm{external}}.
\label{eq:repair}
\end{equation}

At the document snapshot, campaign-level confirmed repairs are zero.  This is
not a curriculum regression: older Technical AGA evidence contains confirmed
natural repairs, but the new open-mission programme intentionally starts its
own repair count at zero.  Repair advantage remains untested because full AION
and AION-without-repair have so far produced the same success count.  A future
mission must force a meaningful repair opportunity; otherwise this ablation
cannot answer its research question.

\section{Metrics and Claim Gates}
\label{sec:metrics}

For arm $a$ with $n_a$ eligible outcomes, success rate is
\begin{equation}
S_a=\frac{1}{n_a}\sum_{i=1}^{n_a}V(r_i).
\end{equation}
Because autonomous arms often require zero human intervention, intervention
ratios may be undefined.  The current primary efficiency measure is verified
work per investigation action:
\begin{equation}
\eta_a=\frac{\sum_i W_i}{\sum_i A_i},
\end{equation}
where $W_i$ is evaluator-verified work and $A_i$ is investigation actions.
The matched advantage is
\begin{equation}
G=\frac{\eta_{\mathrm{full}}}{\eta_{\mathrm{proposer}}}.
\label{eq:gain}
\end{equation}

The narrow numerical 10$\times$ gate opens only if
\begin{equation}
G\geq 10
\quad\land\quad
S_{\mathrm{full}}\geq S_{\mathrm{proposer}}.
\label{eq:tenx}
\end{equation}
Even that gate is not sufficient for a strong intelligence claim.  A credible
10$\times$ result must also survive multiple unfamiliar mission families,
retention, repair, safety, and external reproduction.  One ratio from one
repeated pattern is not an order-of-magnitude general-intelligence result.

\section{Authority Separation and Safety}
\label{sec:safety}

The programme separates five roles:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item the proposer suggests an action or prediction;
    \item the executor performs bounded work;
    \item the evaluator owns the success consequence;
    \item the governor validates and records eligibility; and
    \item the dashboard reports but cannot promote.
\end{enumerate}

No proposal can self-grade.  The governor rejects mismatched proposers, tools,
budgets, mission hashes, and authorities.  Unsafe results cannot become
verified successes.  Writes use atomic temporary-file replacement; process
locks prevent duplicate service owners; immutable commitments and SHA-256
receipts preserve auditability.

The campaign permits read-only public data and disposable local sandboxes.  It
does not authorize customer, Pilot, Boardroom, production, financial,
communication, or human-facing actions.  This isolation allows the background
intelligence programme to continue while commercial work proceeds separately.

\section{End-State Verdicts}
\label{sec:verdicts}

\subsection{Minimum operational pass}

The programme is operationally successful if it completes without stale
services or corrupted evidence, every receipt remains authority-valid, no
unsafe or false promotion occurs, and due retention challenges execute at the
correct time.  This validates the measurement system, not necessarily AION's
intelligence advantage.

\subsection{``OK'' intelligence result}

An acceptable substantive result would contain approximately 10--20
independently scored matched cohorts across several mission families, with:
\begin{itemize}
    \item full AION consistently above proposer-only and cold AION;
    \item a repeatable memory advantage on unfamiliar missions;
    \item at least one independently confirmed repair advantage;
    \item one or more mature source-closed transfers;
    \item no success regression and no unsafe or false promotion;
    \item an efficiency advantage that remains approximately 2--5$\times$
          after more evidence; and
    \item enough mission diversity to rule out a single-template trick.
\end{itemize}

This would support the statement that AION contributes persistent,
independently measurable value beyond its underlying proposer.  It would be a
credible architecture result, but not an AGI claim.

\subsection{``Fucking wow'' result}

An exceptional result requires convergence of several forms of evidence:
\begin{itemize}
    \item full AION repeatedly succeeds where proposer-only, cold AION, and the
          relevant ablation fail;
    \item the effect survives software, systems, changing public data, and at
          least one genuinely different authority family;
    \item the matched efficiency advantage reaches or exceeds 10$\times$
          without sacrificing success or safety;
    \item no-memory falls progressively behind, demonstrating that experience
          is accumulating rather than merely wrapping the proposer;
    \item a natural or honestly induced answer-hidden failure triggers a
          repair whose later consequence passes while no-repair fails;
    \item after seven days and source closure, AION reconstructs and applies the
          method to a renamed or structurally changed challenge with no replay;
    \item later missions become longer and harder while human intervention and
          investigation cost decline;
    \item generation three decisively exceeds generation one on AION's initial
          weak area without regression elsewhere; and
    \item an independent administrator can reproduce the frozen comparison.
\end{itemize}

That package would justify the claim that AION is not merely wrapping a
language model: it is accumulating persistent competence, using it to
outperform the same model without its architecture, repairing itself after
failure, and retaining improvements across time and changed situations.

\subsection{Flat or negative result}

The programme must also preserve a scientifically honest failure verdict.  If
full AION does not separate from the controls, if the gain disappears under
novel missions, if no-memory is equivalent, if repair remains untested, or if
retention fails, the correct conclusion is that the current implementation is
a sophisticated curriculum or orchestration engine whose compounding advantage
has not yet been demonstrated.  That result should change the limiting
mechanism, not trigger more farming of easy internal receipts.

\section{Known Limitations and Required Corrections}
\label{sec:limitations}

The following limitations are authoritative at this snapshot:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Small sample.}  Two completed cohorts are insufficient for a
          robust performance claim.
    \item \textbf{Narrow rolling loop.}  Repeated public-source forecasting is
          a real delayed-outcome experiment but remains one mission family.
          Repetition alone cannot demonstrate generality.
    \item \textbf{Repair not separated.}  Full and no-repair are currently 2/2;
          confirmed campaign repairs remain zero.
    \item \textbf{Retention immature.}  No new campaign retention receipt can
          honestly pass before its seven-day boundary.
    \item \textbf{Human-grounded lane absent.}  Full AGA remains 10/11 because
          independent multi-rater social/creative authority is unavailable.
    \item \textbf{Internal operation.}  The current cohort evidence is locally
          reproducible but not yet externally administered.
    \item \textbf{Fixed small proposer.}  The fixed local proposer is valuable
          for clean attribution, but conclusions apply first to this cohort and
          should later be repeated with another frozen proposer.
    \item \textbf{Ratio sensitivity.}  Early efficiency ratios can change
          sharply with a small denominator.  The 4.5$\times$ value must not be
          presented without its sample size.
\end{enumerate}

The highest-priority technical correction is mission diversity.  The generator
must produce new repository, CI, dependency, schema, protocol, and public-data
problems, not merely new cursors in the same forecasting template.  Difficulty
should increase by removing help, changing structure, lengthening the horizon,
and requiring transfer---not just by increasing the number of nearly identical
examples.

\section{Final Epistemic Boundary}
\label{sec:boundary}

Technical AGA 10/10 remains an important frozen result: AION has demonstrated
the technical apprenticeship machinery required by that protocol.  Full AGA
remains 10/11 because independent human-grounded social/creative authority has
not been supplied.  Neither result is equivalent to demonstrated AGI.

The seven-day programme asks a harder and more useful question: does the full
architecture compound under unfamiliar work, real consequences, matched
controls, source closure, and time?  The current 2/2 versus 0/2 separation and
4.5$\times$ work-per-action ratio are promising first evidence.  They remain
an early baseline until breadth, repair, retention, longer horizons, and
external reproduction are present.

The governing publication rule is therefore:
\begin{quote}
Report exactly what the independent receipts establish.  Keep every larger
claim closed until diverse, delayed, source-disjoint, safe, and reproducible
evidence forces it open.
\end{quote}
