\section{Arena v8: Closed-Loop Physical Intelligence}
\label{sec:arena-v8-physical}

Arena v8 closes the loop from pixel-grounded perception to intervention,
prediction, delayed consequence, model revision and transfer.  A separately
implemented authority owns hidden actuation gain, drag, delay, structural
change and target dynamics.  AION receives rendered pixels, a noisy velocity
channel and bounded control actions; it receives no latent parameter labels or
object coordinates.

\subsection{Perception, identification and action}

Object position is recovered from the rendered red-pixel support.  AION
maintains a posterior over candidate dynamics $h=(g,d,\delta)$, where $g$ is
actuation gain, $d$ is drag and $\delta$ is action delay.  For state
$(x_t,v_t)$ and bounded action $u_t$, a candidate predicts
\begin{align}
 \hat v_{t+1} &= (1-d\Delta t)v_t + g\tilde u_t\Delta t,\\
 \hat x_{t+1} &= x_t + \hat v_{t+1}\Delta t,
\end{align}
where $\tilde u_t=u_{t-1}$ for delayed actuation and $u_t$ otherwise.  The next
diagnostic action maximises posterior predictive disagreement subject to a
small action cost.  A SHA--256 commitment to $\hat x_{t+1}$ is recorded before
the authority executes the action and reveals the next observation.

Accepted observations update the model posterior.  Short action sequences are
then evaluated with model-predictive control, only the first action is executed,
and planning repeats after each observed consequence.  Residual surprise
criticises the current dynamics and can trigger private model revision.  If
repeated bounded actions cannot produce the predicted controllability, the
system abstains rather than forcing an in-family explanation.

\subsection{Outcome-induced transfer prior}

The experience prior is not development encoded.  It is induced from 32
source-disjoint development worlds using outcome-only identification.  A
Dirichlet pseudocount of one retains support for every model.  The learned and
cold systems then receive identical sealed worlds, actions, targets, budgets
and stopping rules; the cold control alone begins from a uniform prior.

The initial design reached every target but produced no probe reduction, no
change diagnosis and no out-of-family abstention, and was rejected.  The final
design distinguishes exact parameter certainty from operational certainty: it
may act when the gain/delay family is resolved even if short-horizon evidence
cannot distinguish two nearly equivalent drag values.

\subsection{Authority and claim boundary}

The simulator is separated from AION's proposal and promotion path but remains
development authored.  Dynamics are one-dimensional, actions and targets are
bounded, and the model family is finite.  This is not unrestricted embodiment,
robotic deployment, independently administered physical evaluation or AGI.
The corresponding external gate requires evaluator-owned devices or simulators
whose dynamics, tasks and outcomes were unavailable during development.
