\section{North-Star Objective Acquisition and AGA Gap Closure}
\label{sec:aion-objective-acquisition-aga-gap-closure}

This development increment replaced a fixed three-contract useful-work loop
with a persistent controller that derives projects from AION's constitutional
North Star, its current evidence, and the availability of an outcome authority.
It then widened that controller from three operational families to five
qualitatively different families, introduced measured cross-domain method
transfer against cold controls, and installed a fail-closed evidence registry
for the Autonomous General Apprentice (AGA) claim.

The resulting operating cycle is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{North Star and current evidence}
&\rightarrow \text{candidate opportunity induction} \\
&\rightarrow \text{value, novelty, cost, and authority ranking} \\
&\rightarrow \text{one selected objective and explicit success contract} \\
&\rightarrow \text{private work artifact and immutable commitment} \\
&\rightarrow \text{strictly later outcome authority} \\
&\rightarrow \text{retain, reject, or revise} \\
&\rightarrow \text{executive-method update and automatic continuation}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:aion-varied-objective-loop}
\end{equation}

The central epistemic restriction is that producing an artifact does not count
as completing an objective.  Credit is granted only when the authority declared
before execution evaluates a later consequence.  Immediate replay, internal
plausibility, and the artifact's own tests cannot close the objective.

\subsection{Varied Useful-Objective Acquisition v1}
\label{subsec:aion-varied-objective-v1}

The first controller, promoted as
\texttt{procedure\_varied\_useful\_objective\_acquisition\_v1}, established
that AION could replace a fixed work bundle with selective, evidence-derived
projects.  For a candidate objective $o$, the controller calculated
\begin{equation}
P(o)=
\frac{
0.45V_o+0.25U_o+0.15R_o+0.15L_o
}{\max(0.1,C_o)}Q_o,
\label{eq:aion-objective-priority}
\end{equation}
where $V_o$ is owner-visible value, $U_o$ is urgency, $R_o$ is risk reduction,
$L_o$ is learning value, $C_o$ is expected cost, and $Q_o$ is confidence that a
valid outcome authority is available.  Dependency readiness and a diversity
bonus were applied before the highest-ranked eligible objective was selected.

For every selection, AION retained the candidate-set size, the rejected
alternatives, the North-Star relevance, the selected work system, the success
criterion, and the artifact commitment.  It produced one artifact rather than
blindly emitting the former three-artifact bundle.  This reduced artifact
actions by $66.67\%$ while retaining research, software, and data coverage.

\subsubsection{Outcome authorities}

Three bounded work families were supported initially:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Research investigation.} A technical-source assessment was
          checked against a later independently collected Git or package row.
    \item \textbf{Software tool construction.} A source-specific integrity
          guard was generated from the observed evidence contract.  A fresh
          process had to accept the later genuine row and reject an adversarial
          mutation.
    \item \textbf{Data decision.} A bounded environmental monitoring decision
          was committed before the next public sensor observation.
\end{enumerate}

The timing condition for objective $o$ and authority observation $a$ was
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Eligible}(a,o)=
\mathbf{1}\!\left[
  c_a>c_o
  \land t_a-t_o\geq 60\ \mathrm{s}
  \land h_a\neq\varnothing
\right],
\label{eq:aion-delayed-authority-eligibility}
\end{equation}
where $c$ denotes the public-outcome cycle, $t$ denotes observation time, and
$h_a$ is the committed outcome hash.  A dedicated timing test demonstrated
that an observation arriving only thirty seconds later could not award credit.

\subsection{Diversity-Constrained Objective Acquisition v2}
\label{subsec:aion-diversity-objective-v2}

The v1 result was positive but exposed a residual optimization risk: unique
objective identifiers could still conceal repeated preference for CPython
research and Madrid weather.  Version 2 therefore added source-level and
subtype-level coverage counts.  If $N(f)$ is the number of prior objectives in
family or source class $f$, eligible candidates are first restricted to
under-covered classes,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{O}^{\star}=
\left\{o\in\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{eligible}}:
N(f_o)=\min_{f\in\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{available}}}N(f)
\right\},
\label{eq:aion-diversity-filter}
\end{equation}
and the priority score in Equation~\eqref{eq:aion-objective-priority} is applied
inside $\mathcal{O}^{\star}$.  This makes repetition visible and prevents a
high-scoring familiar source from permanently excluding a missing family.

Version 2 also added two new authority families:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Machine-checked mathematics.} AION constructs a finite
          witness, such as the sum of the first $n$ odd integers, and a delayed
          deterministic checker independently recomputes both the witness and
          its closed form.
    \item \textbf{Exact-provenance document evidence.} AION commits the source
          hash, query, byte offsets, exact span, and epistemic status.  A delayed
          immutable-source reread must recover the same span and hash.
\end{itemize}

The v2 promotion predicate was
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
G_{\mathrm{v2}}={}&
\mathbf{1}\!\left[N_{\mathrm{closed}}\geq 10\right]
\mathbf{1}\!\left[|\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{closed}}|=5\right]
\mathbf{1}\!\left[\min_f A_f\geq 0.75\right] \\
&\times
\mathbf{1}\!\left[\min_o(t_{a(o)}-t_o)\geq 60\ \mathrm{s}\right]
\mathbf{1}\!\left[I_{\mathrm{owner}}=0\right]
\mathbf{1}\!\left[W_{\mathrm{unsafe}}=0\right].
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:aion-v2-promotion-gate}
\end{equation}

Live operation closed eleven objectives across all five families.  HexCore
therefore promoted
\texttt{procedure\_diversity\_constrained\_useful\_objective\_acquisition\_v2}
and automatically began objective twelve.

\subsection{Measured Cross-Domain Method Transfer}
\label{subsec:aion-measured-method-transfer}

Architectural reuse alone does not establish method transfer.  A separate
campaign therefore compared a retained method card against a cold candidate
sequence on three source-disjoint targets.  For transfer task $i$, the measured
attempt reduction was
\begin{equation}
\Delta_i=1-\frac{A_i^{\mathrm{warm}}}{A_i^{\mathrm{cold}}}.
\label{eq:aion-transfer-attempt-reduction}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Fail-Closed Autonomous-General-Apprentice Evidence Registry}
\label{subsec:aion-aga-evidence-registry}

A new registry prevents capability scaffolding from being mistaken for earned
generality.  In particular,
\begin{equation}
\text{executor installed}\not\Rightarrow\text{Advanced},\qquad
\text{catalogue covered}\not\Rightarrow\text{general apprentice}.
\label{eq:aion-aga-nonimplication}
\end{equation}
Only the progressive practical ledger can award Advanced or Expert status, and
only the eleven evidence gates in Table~\ref{tab:aion-aga-gates} can authorize
the AGA claim.  Registry snapshots are SHA-256 committed and chained to the
previous receipt so that later progress cannot silently rewrite earlier denial.

\subsection{Verification and Persistent Operation}
\label{subsec:aion-aga-gap-verification}

The focused integrated cohort passed $13/13$ tests.  It covered objective
diversity, source rotation, delayed-authority enforcement, adversarial mutation
rejection, restart deduplication, all three method transfers, registry
fail-closure, and hash-chained registry history.  The mastery curriculum,
canonical cognitive runtime, and real-outcome learner remained active during
the work.  Progressive evidence continued to increase independently of the
objective campaign.

\subsection{Claim Boundary and Remaining Work}
\label{subsec:aion-aga-gap-boundary}

This increment closes important architectural gaps but does not establish AGA.
The following conditions require further operation or unavailable authority:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item the 20-project gate is complete, but diverse project operation must
          continue without backward regression;
    \item six Advanced and two Expert practical domains require unfamiliar
          projects, debugging recovery, transfer, and elapsed retention;
    \item a third natural internal failure must occur and its private repair
          must be confirmed by a later operational consequence;
    \item the promoted cognitive proposal component must retain protected
          behavior over longer operation;
    \item replay-free retention must reach seven calendar days; and
    \item social and creative competence requires human-grounded multi-rater
          authority or must remain explicitly outside the claimed scope.
\end{itemize}

The accurate current statement is therefore that AION possesses an operational,
governed apprenticeship organism with live five-family objective acquisition,
measured bounded method transfer, persistent learning, and explicit AGA evidence
accounting.  Autonomous General Apprentice status is being earned rather than
inferred from architecture or procedure count.
