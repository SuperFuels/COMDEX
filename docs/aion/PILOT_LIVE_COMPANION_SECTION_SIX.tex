\section{Pilot Live Companion: Evidence, Context, Accessibility and Live Events}
\label{sec:pilot-live-companion-complete}

Pilot Live Companion is the playback-preserving intelligence layer that can answer
questions about a programme, scene, live claim or event without treating a language
model as a source of truth or as device authority.  The locally enforceable scope was
completed in Pilot Fabric 0.43.0.  Capabilities that require licensed feeds, native
television permissions, content rights or hardware audio controls remain explicitly
fail-closed and are listed in the external qualification register.

\subsection{Evidence model and operating boundary}

Every result separates three evidence classes: owner-permitted observation, authenticated
provider fact and Pilot interpretation.  A camera observation can establish visible text,
a scoreboard or a bounded object label; it cannot establish a person's identity, account
entitlement or official event status.  An authenticated provider can establish only the
fields present in its signed response.  Pilot may explain those facts, but its explanation
cannot manufacture a missing source, permission or playback state.

The operating sequence is:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item acquire explicit, bounded live context while preserving playback;
  \item normalize observations and provider data independently;
  \item reconcile agreement, conflict, staleness and missing evidence;
  \item answer with provenance, confidence and a stated limitation;
  \item route private evidence or approval to the phone when identity is required;
  \item retain structured evidence hashes and audit receipts, not raw programme media.
\end{enumerate}

Raw microphone audio and source photographs are not retained.  Face recognition and
visible-person guessing are not used.  Protected programme audio or video is never copied
into a Pilot Moment.

\subsection{Programme people, credits, origins and sources}

After multi-frame perception has established one stable programme identity, the programme
companion can retrieve the public main cast, resolve an exact character name and retrieve
broader credits for an exactly matched current cast member.  Requests such as ``what else
has this cast member been in?'' are accepted only when the named person matches one current
cast entry exactly.  ``Who is that actor?'' is deliberately refused because programme
membership does not prove that a person is visible in the present scene.

Programme-origin evidence uses strict exact-title matching against Wikidata and extracts
only structured properties such as filming locations, narrative locations, original
sources, countries of origin, original languages and original broadcasters.  Multiple
exact candidates fail closed.  A programme filming location is never represented as the
location of the current shot.  Public catalogue responses are bounded, cached for twenty
four hours and stripped of account information, pixels and protected media.

\subsection{Contextual explanation and selectable audience modes}

The explanation engine supports scene recap, dialogue, joke, reference and cultural
context.  It also supports child, simple, normal and detailed response modes.  The prompt
and deterministic fallback both impose an observed-evidence-only spoiler boundary: Pilot
may explain what has been heard or captured, but may not retrieve or reveal future plot
events to complete an explanation.

Accessibility mode produces a descriptive scene summary without identifying unknown
people.  Learning mode converts permitted scene evidence into an age-appropriate science,
history or language activity.  The child mode uses short language, avoids mature invented
detail and does not silently weaken the evidence threshold.  Reading level changes the
presentation, not the underlying facts.

\subsection{Products, advertising, cooking and visible objects}

Screen fusion recognizes a bounded allowlist of ingredient and cooking-technique terms and
retains only structured object labels above a confidence threshold.  It does not infer a
brand, exact product or ingredient that was not visibly evidenced.  Advertising and
product claims route through the same fact-check pipeline as live news: the claim is
decomposed, HTTPS evidence is retained with source quality, contradictory verdicts are
detected outside the model and an unverifiable result remains unverifiable.

\subsection{Authenticated sports and live-event intelligence}

The live-event adapter keeps authenticated provider data separate from an owner-captured
scoreboard.  It can report scores, goals, bookings, substitutions and supplied lineups,
and can build a chronological incident timeline.  If the feed omits player-performance
statistics, Pilot says so instead of estimating them from the screen.  Screen/provider
score conflicts are retained and displayed rather than silently merged.

Historical comparisons require an explicit range of one to 365 days.  Pilot identifies
one team from the authenticated results, rejects an ambiguous match and calculates played,
won, drawn, lost, goals for and goals against from completed scored matches only.  The
selected range and provider provenance remain part of the result.

\subsection{Private alerts, commentary and household participation}

``Notify me when this match becomes close'' creates an unassigned threshold request on the
shared television.  A private phone must claim it before activation.  Evaluation occurs
only against an authenticated event update and emits a notification only when the score
first crosses into the selected absolute-margin threshold.  Repeated identical updates do
not create duplicate alerts.  The initial adapter delivers to the local private-phone
inbox; external push remains disabled until a signed native phone adapter is qualified.

Commentary preferences are persona-bound and allow standard, child-friendly, tactical,
quiet and accessibility styles.  They generate local explanations and do not replace the
programme audio.  Household predictions are opt-in, persona-bound and explicitly disable
money and betting.  Fantasy updates and richer competitions require an authenticated
licensed provider before they may be represented as current.

\subsection{Fact-check benchmark and private delivery}

The repeatable benchmark fixes public claims and expected verdicts before execution.  It
records verdict agreement, HTTPS evidence coverage, mean and 95th-percentile latency and
provider errors.  Network and provider failures count against the run; the model does not
grade itself.  The initial public case set includes stable claims with official WHO and
NASA references and can be executed in air-gapped, public-evidence and optional-provider
modes.

The first AION Native public-evidence smoke run exposed irrelevant search results and
matched one of three expected verdicts.  After adding deterministic token-overlap
rejection and a bounded public encyclopaedia fallback, a fresh run matched all three fixed
verdicts, retained HTTPS evidence for all three and completed with an 11.12-second mean
latency.  This is recorded as a smoke result rather than a claim of general accuracy; a
larger dated, adversarial and multilingual qualification set remains open.

A fact-check Moment binds the statement, verdict, summary, sanitized source links and
evidence hash into a signed, expiring card.  Recipient choice and the final Send action
occur on the private phone.  The built-in local private-phone adapter writes the card to an
identity-bound inbox and returns a verified receipt.  Email, messaging and Pilot-contact
delivery remain adapter-gated and cannot claim success without a provider receipt.

\subsection{Platform, rights and hardware authority}

The \texttt{LiveMediaAuthority} gate treats permission as structured evidence rather than
model output.  Synchronized subtitle projection requires native overlay authority, caption
projection authority and explicit subtitle projection rights.  Local dubbing requires an
authorized audio-output route and explicit content rights.  A compact playback overlay
requires native dismissible-overlay authority.  Dialogue enhancement or background-sound
reduction requires an exposed, signed hardware DSP preset.

``Show every goal'' returns only HTTPS routes marked both provider-verified and
rights-authorized by an event feed.  Pilot does not scrape, record or download protected
video.  When any required grant is absent, the request produces a bounded explanation of
the missing authority and no media operation is attempted.

\subsection{Persistence, security and verification}

All persistent files use canonical structured serialization and owner-only permissions.
Caches are bounded; provider secrets remain on the mother brain; raw provider responses
are not retained; shared-room speech cannot silently select a private identity.  Audit
events record hashes, scopes and outcomes without copying raw private content.  Automated
tests cover exact person matching, ambiguity rejection, scene modes, programme origins,
screen-object bounds, incident normalization, historical ranges, private threshold claim,
notification deduplication, no-money predictions, benchmark measurement, private Moment
delivery and fail-closed media authority.

Section 6 is therefore locally complete.  Field-complete claims remain conditional on the
documented provider, platform, rights and hardware evidence.  This distinction allows work
to continue into contextual companionship without disguising an unavailable third-party
permission as finished functionality.
