\section{Open Evidence Acquisition and Iterative Investigation}
\label{sec:open-evidence-acquisition-arena-v2}

Arena v2 removed the preassembled evidence bundle from the open-world project
contract.  Instead of receiving documents selected by the benchmark, AION
received only a broad unfamiliar objective and had to determine what it needed
to learn, invent search queries, acquire current public evidence, criticise
source sufficiency, execute a second investigation round, and synthesize a
provenance-bearing answer after process reconstruction.

The promoted procedure was
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize\texttt{procedure\_open\_evidence\_acquisition\_arena\_v2\_32d79e5c6f41}}.
\end{center}
Its parent was
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize\texttt{procedure\_open\_world\_outcome\_execution\_arena\_v1\_5bc6e92f19ae}}.
\end{center}

The promotion tests open evidence acquisition and iterative investigation under
governed authority.  It does not transfer epistemic authority to the language
model, search engine, source-ranking policy, or URL cache.


\subsection{Research question and openness contract}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-research-question}

The primary question was whether AION could determine which information was
missing and acquire sufficiently authoritative and independent evidence without
receiving a source list.  Every episode withheld:
\begin{itemize}
  \item documents and URLs;
  \item entity and relation schemas;
  \item workflows and expected subgoals;
  \item search queries;
  \item expected answers; and
  \item delayed scoring terms.
\end{itemize}

The proposal substrate was required to produce an objective interpretation,
at least three success criteria, an uncertainty inventory, two to four search
queries, a source-selection policy, and initial information actions.  It was
explicitly prohibited from answering from prior model knowledge before the
acquisition stage.

The complete investigation sequence was
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{broad unfamiliar objective}
&\rightarrow \text{criteria, unknowns, and queries} \\
&\rightarrow \text{governed read-only search} \\
&\rightarrow \text{authority/relevance/independence ranking} \\
&\rightarrow \text{live retrieval and hash freezing} \\
&\rightarrow \text{evidence criticism} \\
&\rightarrow \text{typed second-round actions} \\
&\rightarrow \text{pre-answer commitment and restart} \\
&\rightarrow \text{exact-evidence synthesis} \\
&\rightarrow \text{quarantine, CAU promotion, or rejection}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v2-investigation-cycle}
\end{equation}


\subsection{Governed network and source boundary}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-network-boundary}

The acquisition layer was read-only and admitted only public HTTPS targets.
Before retrieval, the runtime resolved the destination and rejected private,
loopback, link-local, reserved, and multicast addresses.  Redirect destinations
were checked again.  Responses were capped at $3{,}000{,}000$ bytes and active
HTML elements were excluded from text extraction.

For each selected document $d$, the evidence capsule stored
\begin{equation}
C(d)=\{u,\,p,\,t,\,m,\,b,\,h(d),\,x(d)\},
\label{eq:arena-v2-source-capsule}
\end{equation}
where $u$ is the final URL, $p$ is the publisher domain, $t$ is retrieval time,
$m$ is content type, $b$ is byte count, $h(d)$ is the SHA-256 digest of the
retrieved bytes, and $x(d)$ is the extracted text.  A URL or search result did
not become evidence merely by being ranked or cached.

Candidate selection used a bounded score
\begin{equation}
S(d\mid g)=A(d)+R(d,g)-0.03\,\rho(d),
\label{eq:arena-v2-source-score}
\end{equation}
where $A(d)$ is a publisher-authority feature, $R(d,g)$ is lexical relevance
to goal $g$, and $\rho(d)$ is search rank.  Source diversity was enforced by
publisher domain before the requested independence count was satisfied.

The failover cache stored query-to-URL candidates, not article content.  A
cached URL still had to pass the public-network policy, be retrieved live, and
receive a fresh content hash.  Thus,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{CachedURL}(u)
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{AcceptedEvidence}(u).
\label{eq:arena-v2-cache-no-authority}
\end{equation}


\subsection{Investigation portfolio}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-portfolio}

Six broad objectives covered six unrelated capability families:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Earth seasons and the distance-from-the-Sun misconception;
  \item HTTP validation using ETag, If-None-Match, and status 304;
  \item earthquake magnitude and released-energy scaling;
  \item antibiotic resistance and unnecessary antibiotic use;
  \item serializable database transactions and retry behavior; and
  \item the United States constitutional amendment process.
\end{enumerate}

AION invented 24 queries.  Twelve live pages with twelve distinct content
hashes were selected from sources including NOAA, MDN, HTTPWG, USGS, WHO,
NCBI, PostgreSQL, and the United States National Archives.  These documents
were not present in the planning prompt.


\subsection{Evidence criticism and typed second-round actions}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-actions}

After acquisition, a separate proposal pass received only the frozen evidence
capsules and was required to identify contradictions, missing information, and
useful follow-up actions.  The permitted action grammar was
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}=\{\texttt{calculator},\texttt{compare\_sources},
\texttt{request\_more\_evidence}\}.
\label{eq:arena-v2-action-set}
\end{equation}

The calculator accepted only numeric constants, parentheses, and bounded
arithmetic operators in a restricted abstract syntax tree.  Source comparison
could reference only identifiers already present in the acquisition ledger.
Further search used the same network boundary and query hashing as the first
round.

The learned router retained the following policy:
\begin{quote}
Fetch the minimum number of independently governed sources required by the
objective; add a source only to resolve contradiction or missing evidence.
\end{quote}
Consequently, a proposed additional search was skipped when the currently
frozen evidence already satisfied the authority and independence gates.  The
abstention itself was recorded as a verified cost-aware action.


\subsection{Three governed learning generations}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-generations}

\subsubsection{Generation 1: open-acquisition negative result}

The first generation invented all 24 queries, acquired 11 unique live sources,
executed without an unsafe action, survived restart, and reduced sealed
information cost by $26.09\%$.  However, only two of six tasks passed.  Exact
evidence binding failed on three tasks, no usable earthquake evidence was
acquired, and the quantitative critic did not instantiate a calculator action.
CAU rejected the challenger.

This failure was decomposed into separate queues for perception, authority,
source independence, knowledge, and quantitative reasoning rather than being
represented by one undifferentiated failure bit.

\subsubsection{Generation 2: information-channel failure}

The live search provider then rate-limited every acquisition request.  The
second generation acquired zero sources, accepted no unsupported claim, and
correctly achieved zero promoted tasks.  The failure was classified as an
unavailable information channel rather than evidence that the retained
knowledge or reasoning procedure had regressed.

A governed search-failover manifest was constructed from externally resolved
primary-source search results.  It contained no source text and no answer.  It
allowed the same AION-authored query to be resolved through a replaceable
provider while retaining live retrieval and hashing as the evidence authority.

\subsubsection{Generation 3: recovery and promotion}

The third generation acquired two live sources for each task.  The combined
six-task final-synthesis call then exceeded its provider timeout.  Because the
plans, sources, criticisms, action inputs, outcomes, and hashes had already been
committed, AION resumed the six synthesis tasks independently.  The completed
outputs were recombined under the unchanged evaluator without repeating search
or relearning the action policy.

The remaining failure ledger produced two transferable repairs:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item when a quantitative objective contains two quantities and acquired
        evidence provides a multiplicative factor but no calculator proposal,
        construct and execute the missing arithmetic action; and
  \item when a project has complete evidence and success criteria but omits
        only a verification field, attach the retained generic verification
        procedure without modifying claims or factual content.
\end{enumerate}

For the earthquake task, live USGS evidence supplied an approximate factor of
32 in released energy for each whole magnitude unit.  The difference between
magnitudes 7 and 5 is two units, so the recovered action executed
\begin{equation}
32^{\lvert 7-5\rvert}=32^2=1024\approx 1{,}000.
\label{eq:arena-v2-earthquake-recovery}
\end{equation}
The result remained labelled approximate.  A subsequent evaluator defect that
treated \texttt{1,000} and \texttt{1000} as different numerical answers was
corrected without changing the plan, source, action, or final answer.


\subsection{Evidence quarantine and authority accounting}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-authority}

Final synthesis could cite only exact text recovered from a frozen source.
Every proposed claim $c$ was checked by
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Accept}(c)
\Longleftrightarrow
q(c)\subseteq x(d)
\land s(c)=s(d)
\land e(c)\in\mathcal{E},
\label{eq:arena-v2-claim-gate}
\end{equation}
where $q(c)$ is the normalized quotation, $s(c)$ is its source identifier,
$x(d)$ is the frozen source text, and $\mathcal{E}$ is the allowed epistemic
label set.  Invalid quotations were quarantined rather than committed.

Source independence and claim authority were measured separately.  The former
tested whether the acquisition procedure considered the required number of
independent domains.  The latter tested whether each retained claim could be
recovered exactly.  Search results, URL-cache entries, language-model output,
and executed calculations remained proposal or outcome channels rather than
source authority.

\subsection{Interpretation and claim boundary}
\label{subsec:arena-v2-boundary}

Arena v2 demonstrates a material increase in openness.  AION no longer merely
reasons over a prepared reading packet; it identifies information needs,
constructs searches, acquires current documents, tests evidence sufficiency,
performs follow-up actions, recovers from information-channel and provider
failure, and retains a cheaper verified investigation policy.

The result remains bounded.  Objectives, permitted action grammar, ranking
features, network policy, and delayed scoring terms were engineered.  The
failover manifest was externally maintained.  Publisher independence was
approximated by source domain.  The experiment did not include unrestricted
browser interaction, natural image/audio understanding, human social judgment,
or independently administered evaluation.  It therefore supports a claim of
governed open evidence acquisition and continual investigation, not AGI,
unrestricted web autonomy, medical authority, or external certification.


\subsection{Arena v3 acceleration contract}
\label{subsec:arena-v3-contract}

The next stage should embed open investigation inside long-running, changing
projects.  The promotion contract should require:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item dozens of unfamiliar portfolios lasting across multiple interruptions;
  \item independently delayed code, database, document, and sensor outcomes;
  \item experiment and tool-parameter invention beyond arithmetic;
  \item repeated action--observation loops governed by expected
        value-of-information, cost, and risk;
  \item matched no-memory, no-router, no-causal, and substrate-only controls;
  \item several continual generations with backward-retention and
        weakest-family gates;
  \item natural multimodal portfolios with semantic image and diagram
        grounding;
  \item human-authored commonsense, social, and creative objectives evaluated
        by multiple independent raters with disagreement retained;
  \item replaceable stronger proposal substrates under identical evidence,
        action, and authority budgets; and
  \item externally administered portfolios, delayed outcomes, and scoring.
\end{enumerate}

The decisive next question is whether open investigations can compound across
weeks of changing projects without forgetting earlier competence, overusing
tools, or allowing neural and language proposals to acquire epistemic
authority.
