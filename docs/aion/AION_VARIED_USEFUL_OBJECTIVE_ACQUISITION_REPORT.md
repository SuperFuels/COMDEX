# AION Varied Useful-Objective Acquisition

## Result

AION now has a persistent controller that derives individual research,
software and data objectives from the constitutional North Star and the latest
independently collected evidence. It replaces the earlier requirement to emit
the same three work contracts on every generation.

The live controller completed six varied objectives across research, software
and data. All six were confirmed by later public observations, with 100%
weakest-family success and a shortest authority delay of 70.647 seconds. The
seventh objective is open and operation continues automatically.

## Control loop

1. Read the immutable North-Star purpose and current executive attention.
2. Inspect the most recent committed public-outcome row.
3. Induce candidate opportunities from the actual sources, schema and measured
   environmental values present in that row.
4. Score value, urgency, risk reduction, learning value, cost and authority
   availability.
5. Apply an explicit diversity pressure so one objective family cannot crowd
   out the others.
6. Select one objective and declare a measurable success criterion.
7. Compose an evidence-backed executive work system.
8. Produce a private artifact and persist its hash and commitment.
9. Wait for a strictly later independently collected outcome.
10. Verify, retain or reject the work and update the executive method ledger.

## Induced objective families

### Research investigation

The controller creates a question about a technical source that actually
appears in the live evidence. Its brief records the authority, observed value,
source cycle, North-Star relevance and provisional conclusion. A later source
row owns the score.

### Software tool construction

The controller discovers the observed public-outcome contract and constructs a
private executable integrity guard from those fields. The guard must later
accept a genuine outcome in a fresh process and reject an adversarially mutated
copy. It cannot modify the live repository.

### Data decision

The controller derives a bounded environmental decision from the measured
temperature and wind rather than from a fixed answer. The operating envelope is
committed before the next sensor row and is rejected when the later observation
falls outside it.

## Governance and promotion gate

The promotion gate requires at least six later-scored objectives, all three
families, unique objective identities, at least 75% weakest-family success,
zero owner interventions and zero unsafe live writes. Restarting while an
objective is awaiting authority does not create a duplicate objective.

The focused test cohort passed 3/3 tests. Across the simulated later-authority
sequence it produced more than six unique objectives, exercised all three
families, obtained 100% weakest-family success, rejected the integrity mutation
and preserved the same open commitment across restart. A separate timing test
proved that a row arriving only thirty seconds later cannot close credit.

## Live status

The live system closed 6/6 required objectives across all three families and

software guards accepted genuine later rows and rejected adversarial mutations.
Seven unique objectives have been created, six executive outcomes recorded and
fourteen artifact actions avoided relative to the previous fixed three-lane

Status is shown on the development dashboard under
`VARIED NORTH-STAR OBJECTIVE ACQUISITION`.

## Claim boundary

Objective instances, source choice, parameters and success contracts are
derived from live North-Star state and evidence. The safe artifact builders and
later-outcome evaluator adapters remain engineered. This is varied autonomous
objective acquisition under bounded authorities, not unrestricted agency, broad
economic autonomy or AGI.

repository failures, document contradictions, active competency gaps and owner

valuable objectives across materially different authority families while owner
