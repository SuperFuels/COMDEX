\section{Situated Compounding Intelligence}
\label{sec:aion-situated-compounding-open-methods}

This development sequence replaced several fixed orchestration assumptions with
retained, consequence-tested machinery.  AION now appraises its operational
situation, binds multiple evidenced competencies into one project, diagnoses
disagreement between authorities, invents a new project family when its current
taxonomy is inadequate, compiles experience into reusable verification methods,
constructs hierarchical goal programmes, and extends its own method language
when no retained method can verify a new project structure.

The resulting control path is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{situation}
&\rightarrow \text{need and constraint appraisal}
\rightarrow \text{hierarchical mission graph} \\
&\rightarrow \text{capability binding or prerequisite learning}
\rightarrow \text{prospective method selection} \\
&\rightarrow \text{action and later independent consequence}
\rightarrow \text{disagreement diagnosis} \\
&\rightarrow \text{recovery, family invention, or retention}
\rightarrow \text{harder successor objective}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:situated-compounding-loop}
\end{equation}

This is an architectural move away from selecting a task from a static queue.
It does not grant AION new terminal desires or unrestricted authority.  The
owner-defined North Star remains immutable, HexCore retains execution authority,
and every promoted method remains subordinate to executable outcomes and the
Cognitive Authority Unit (CAU).

\subsection{Situation-Driven Executive Cognition}
\label{subsec:situation-driven-executive}

The Situation Engine was upgraded from an event summary into an upstream
executive appraisal.  Its state is represented as
\begin{equation}
S_t =
\left(E_t, C_t, O_t, R_t, K_t, F_t, P_t\right),
\label{eq:situation-state}
\end{equation}
where $E_t$ denotes the observed environment, $C_t$ the current condition,
$O_t$ active obligations, $R_t$ constraints and authority limits, $K_t$
available competencies, $F_t$ unresolved failures, and $P_t$ constitutional
instrumental preferences.  The executive derives a current driver
\begin{equation}
d_t = \arg\max_{d \in \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{authorised}}}
\left[V(d,S_t)-\lambda_C C(d)-\lambda_R R(d)-\lambda_U U(d)\right],
\label{eq:situational-driver}
\end{equation}
where value, cost, risk, and uncertainty are evaluated only over authorised
objectives.

The appraisal influences portfolio construction and canonical goal attention.
It can identify that the current bottleneck is a missing executor, authority,
competency, or diagnostic capability.  It cannot create a terminal objective,
expand ambient authority, or alter the constitutional North Star.

\subsection{Open Authority-Disagreement Diagnosis}
\label{subsec:open-authority-disagreement}

When credible authorities disagree, selecting the convenient answer would
corrupt both the world model and the repair system.  AION therefore constructs
a diagnostic experiment over four competing origins:
\begin{equation}
H = \{h_{\mathrm{evidence}}, h_{\mathrm{environment}},
h_{\mathrm{adapter}}, h_{\mathrm{method}}\}.
\label{eq:disagreement-hypotheses}
\end{equation}
It chooses a bounded probe that maximises expected discrimination between these
hypotheses, precommits the probe, executes it against the relevant authorities,
and routes the result to world revision, adapter repair, method criticism, or
abstention.  Self-repair is authorised only when evidence supports an internal
origin.

\subsection{Open Objective-Family Invention}

residual and proposed
\texttt{situated\_operational\_resilience}, together with the reusable executor
contract
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}

\subsection{Experience-Compiled Project Intelligence}
\label{subsec:experience-compiled-project-intelligence}

Retaining one executor for every project family scales poorly.  The experience
compiler instead induces compact method prototypes from chronological verified
history:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{verified}}
\rightarrow \{m_1,\ldots,m_k\}
\rightarrow \Pi(x,m_i)
\rightarrow y_{\mathrm{independent}},
\label{eq:experience-compiler}
\end{equation}
where $m_i$ is a reusable verification method and $\Pi$ compiles the method
into a prospective project program from observable affordances.  Development

\subsection{Tier-Six Prospective Evidence and Integrity Corrections}
\label{subsec:tier-six-prospective-evidence}

Tier six adds prospective method choice to the previous evidence roles.  A
useful-work goal is not complete until it obtains
\begin{enumerate}
    \item a later specialist consequence;
    \item cross-domain competency evidence;
    \item an independent authority receipt;
    \item transferred evidence from an invented objective family; and
    \item a verification program committed before the outcome was visible.
\end{enumerate}

The first learned-method tier-six completion selected
\texttt{fresh\_subprocess\_plus\_later\_public\_row}, committed it before the
specialist result, and closed only after all five receipt roles were present.
The transition also exposed three integrity defects: cross-tier goal identity
collisions, reuse of one specialist consequence by two goals, and disappearance
of a completed compiler receipt during portfolio rotation.  Corrections bound
goal identity to difficulty, enforced one-project--one-goal assignment, and
recovered historical receipts only through immutable digest references.  No
affected goal received premature credit.

The autonomous useful-work portfolio now contains five learned-method contracts
and zero fixed-family contracts.  The executive therefore binds goals to
observable authority semantics rather than to the original five family labels.

\subsection{Hierarchical, Capability-Gated Mission Graphs}
\label{subsec:hierarchical-mission-graphs}

Broad objectives such as ``create a profitable website business'' cannot be
executed as one atomic goal.  The hierarchical mission compiler expands such an
objective into a persistent directed acyclic graph (DAG) containing strategic
milestones, work nodes, measurable success criteria, recovery branches, and
capability prerequisites.

For every work node $w$, the compiler queries the progressive competency
ledger.  If a required capability $c$ is below Intermediate, it inserts a
learning node $\ell_c$ and rewrites the dependency relation as
\begin{equation}
c < \textsc{Intermediate}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\ell_c \prec w.
\label{eq:capability-gated-work}
\end{equation}
A verified learning outcome releases the affected branch; unrelated branches
remain available.  Failure invokes a narrow recovery edge---diagnose the
origin, replan the affected branch, then abstain or escalate---rather than
restarting or corrupting the whole mission.

For a website venture, representative milestones include demand validation,
secure product construction, launch readiness, customer acquisition, and
economic verification.  Public deployment, spending, customer contact, and
revenue transactions remain explicit human-approval nodes.

\subsection{Open Method-Language Expansion}

six retained methods could express.  AION diagnosed the residual and invented
\texttt{dependency\_gated\_mission\_graph\_audit}.  The method contains six
typed, read-only atoms:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \texttt{ASSERT\_ACYCLIC};
    \item \texttt{ASSERT\_HIERARCHY};
    \item \texttt{ASSERT\_CAPABILITY\_PREREQUISITES};
    \item \texttt{ASSERT\_MEASURABLE\_OUTCOMES};

\subsection{Operational Integration and Safety}
\label{subsec:situated-compounding-integration}

The situational executive, project engine, authority diagnosis, family
invention, experience compiler, hierarchical mission compiler, and method
expander run through the persistent cognitive and curriculum services.  The
terminal dashboard reports their live status, method-driven portfolio,
prospective receipts, graph coverage, and autonomous consequence closure.

The architecture preserves four separations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item proposals are distinct from execution authority;
    \item internal failure is distinct from external world change;
    \item historical reconstruction is distinct from prospective evidence; and
    \item capability acquisition is distinct from permission to act externally.
\end{enumerate}
Focused integration verification passed 36/36 tests for the final hierarchical
mission and open-method stage.  Earlier consecutive stacks passed 41/41,
44/44, 45/45, and 46/46 tests as each preceding capability was integrated.

\subsection{Technical Interpretation and Remaining Boundary}
\label{subsec:situated-compounding-boundary}

The defensible technical claim is that AION can now:
\begin{itemize}
    \item appraise its current operational situation and select an authorised
          need rather than merely consume a queue;
    \item compose evidenced competencies inside later-confirmed projects;
    \item diagnose disagreement instead of assigning an unsupported failure
          label;
    \item invent a new reusable objective family;
    \item compile verified experience into reusable methods with substantial
          reduction in investigation attempts;
    \item convert broad objectives into capability-gated, recoverable mission
          programmes; and
    \item grow its governed verification language when existing retained
          methods cannot express the required success contract.
\end{itemize}

These results do not establish unrestricted agency, general self-programming,
commercial success, autonomous general apprenticeship, or AGI.  Situation
features, safe atom semantics, selected authority interfaces, and CAU promotion
remain engineered.  The strongest next boundary is recursive interpreter
expansion: when no existing atom can implement the newly required verifier,
AION must privately invent the missing safe atom, construct its semantic and
resource counterexamples, transfer it to unrelated real missions, and retain it
only after a later independent consequence.  In parallel, the mission system
must repeatedly complete unfamiliar, owner-valued, long-horizon projects with
declining intervention and increasing practical competency.
