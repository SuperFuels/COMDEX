# AION Deep Apprenticeship Curriculum and Mission-Capability Action Switch

## Outcome

AION now retains a hierarchy of 15 top-level apprenticeship missions. Each
mission expands into assessed leaf subjects, and every leaf subject expands
into its complete declared subskill set. Top-level selection therefore cannot
be satisfied by one lesson or one broad assessment receipt.

The curriculum is joined to a mission-capability action harness. Given a broad
natural-language objective, the harness:

1. infers the required capability portfolio;
2. reads actual knowledge and practical levels from the progressive ledger;
3. retrieves missing subskills;
4. proposes an appropriate technology stack;
5. selects independent execution, strongly verified bounded execution, or
   learning before execution;
6. places the weakest required capability into the live curriculum when an
   authorized mission exposes a gap; and
7. returns an eight-stage action contract ending in independent verification
   and post-outcome reflection.

## Top-level missions

The hierarchy contains:

- systems and software engineering;
- data and transactional integrity;
- empirical and scientific modelling;
- document and evidence work;
- tool and environment acquisition;
- mathematics and formal reasoning;
- planning and long-horizon projects;
- physical and sensor-actuator grounding;
- causal investigation;
- security and adversarial robustness;
- natural-language instruction and dialogue;
- commonsense and social judgment;
- creative synthesis under constraints;
- multi-agent and institutional reasoning; and
- research and meta-learning.

Subjects may contribute to several missions. This overlap is intentional and
allows verified methods to transfer rather than creating disconnected copies.

## Action Switch integration

The original resonant and reflex Action Switch remains intact. A conservative
competency gate now runs before StrategyEngine execution:

\[
\text{natural mission}
\rightarrow
\text{required capability portfolio}
\rightarrow
\text{competency lookup}
\rightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{execute}, & \text{all required capabilities advanced},\\
\text{execute with strong verification}, & \text{floor intermediate},\\
\text{learn then execute}, & \text{any required capability below intermediate},\\
\text{clarify}, & \text{no grounded mapping}.
\end{cases}
\]

The harness never treats a technology recommendation, memory retrieval, or
teacher proposal as successful execution. Real outcomes remain the authority.

## Concrete mission test

For the objective "Build a secure browser weather app with an API and
persistent user settings," AION identified software engineering, testing,
security, product management, the browser platform, TypeScript, UI design and
secure engineering. It proposed TypeScript and HTML/CSS for the browser-facing
stack and correctly selected `learn_then_execute` because several required
capabilities remain below Intermediate.

## Live status

The progressive service is active. Delayed Python Core and Testing/Debugging
retention assessments are parked until their clocks mature while Advanced
Python continues through executable CPython, sealed concurrency, transfer and
security outcomes.

## Claim boundary

This establishes a deep curriculum hierarchy and a working mission-to-
competence-to-action control plane. It does not establish mastery of the 15
missions. Every subject still requires repeated subskill evidence, unfamiliar
projects, debugging, transfer, delayed retention and declining scaffolding
before its target level is reached.
