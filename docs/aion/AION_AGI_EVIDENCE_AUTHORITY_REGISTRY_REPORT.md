# AION AGI Evidence Authority Registry v1

## Purpose

This stage converts AION's scattered capability results into one executable,
fail-closed evidence registry. It does **not** claim AGI. Its purpose is to make
the remaining distance measurable and to prevent internally generated results,
public-source transfer, and independently administered certification from being
treated as equivalent forms of evidence.

Promoted control-plane procedure:

`procedure_agi_evidence_authority_registry_v1_9b6e63c112a4`

Promotion applies only to the evidence-control mechanism. The AGI claim flag is
hard-set to false until an independent evaluator owns the task portfolios,
delayed outcomes, budgets, scoring and reproduction.

## Architecture

The registry performs the following governed chain:

```text
existing capability artifacts
        |
        v
SHA-256 commitments + declared field contracts
        |
        v
authority classification
  internal | public external source | external administrator
        |
        v
ten AGI evidence gates + explicit remaining work
        |
        v
frozen independent-evaluator handoff package
        |
        v
CAU promotes the registry runtime, never an AGI claim
```

Twelve source artifacts are committed. Every artifact records its path, SHA-256
digest, authority class, required fields and claim boundary. A restart rebuilds
the runtime from persisted state and confirms that both the registry and the
denial of an AGI claim remain intact without replaying source artifacts.

## Frozen external handoff contract

The generated handoff package requires an independent evaluator to commit,
before system access, to eight portfolio families:

1. unfamiliar language and long-document research;
2. mathematics and formal reasoning;
3. source-disjoint software engineering;
4. scientific model invention and prediction;
5. natural multimodal and physical grounding;
6. long-horizon changing projects;
7. social, commonsense and multi-agent judgment; and
8. creative synthesis with independent raters.

It also requires matched evaluation of complete AION, four component ablations,
a frontier agent, a strong open agent and qualified humans under identical
information, tool, compute and time budgets. Task hashes, delayed outcomes,
human-panel membership, budgets and answer reveals must be committed before the
run. Mean and weakest-family results, forward transfer, backward retention,
unsafe acceptance, abstention, escalation, restart recovery and reproducibility
must all be reported.

The developer is explicitly prohibited from filling the external certification
role. Blank evaluator and signature fields are therefore a required current
state rather than missing implementation.

### Cryptographic return path

The handoff now has a corresponding Ed25519 attestation verifier. An evaluator
must sign the complete result envelope, including the original registry and
artifact commitments, five preregistration commitments, the full portfolio and
matched-system coverage, all ten audit flags, all ten gate verdicts and the
reproduction record. Any post-signature result change invalidates the whole
envelope. A correctly signed but incomplete scope is also rejected. The
verifier does not pretend that a signature proves scientific independence; the
evaluator identity, measurements and published logs must still be audited.

## Remaining critical programme

The registry changes the execution order. More isolated internal perfect scores
are no longer the highest-value work. The remaining development and authority
work is:

1. build natural multimodal and physical portfolios with independently
   maintained outcomes;
2. run multi-hour and multi-day projects against changing external systems;
3. collect independent human social, commonsense and creative judgments while
   retaining legitimate disagreement;
4. acquire unfamiliar APIs and execution environments through governed private
   adapters;
5. extend the continual arena over many generations with external replay and
   held-out task ownership;
6. compare stronger proposal substrates under identical whole-system budgets;
7. consolidate only verified trajectories into replaceable native components;
8. hand the frozen package to an independent evaluator for matched AION,
   ablation, frontier-agent and human-baseline execution.

## Claim boundary

This stage demonstrates an executable evidence and authority control plane. It
proves that AION can consolidate its existing evidence without granting itself
an AGI label and can prepare a reproducible external handoff. It does not prove
AGI, general human-level competence, contamination-free superiority, external
reproducibility or independent human acceptance. Those claims remain closed by
construction until the corresponding outside evidence exists.
