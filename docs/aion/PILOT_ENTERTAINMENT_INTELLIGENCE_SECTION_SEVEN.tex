\section{Pilot Entertainment Intelligence and Private Personalization}
\label{sec:pilot-entertainment-intelligence}

Pilot Fabric 0.45.0 completes the locally enforceable entertainment layer by joining
evidence-labelled discovery, strict provider execution, signed playback facts and private
personalization.  The design never equates a search result with availability, opening an
application with playback, or household presence with permission to use another person's
history.

\subsection{Discovery, official routes and verified execution}

Cross-service discovery labels each result with its source, region and current-evidence
limitation.  Execution accepts only narrowly validated, credential-free HTTPS title or
video routes for supported providers.  Search pages, catalogue pages, embedded credentials,
fragments and unrecognized hosts fail closed.  The canonical route is bound to one private
persona and requires a separate phone confirmation before any device attempt.

A device receipt can establish that a provider or application opened; it cannot establish
playback.  Playback requires explicit evidence of both the expected title and a playing
state.  Signed provider telemetry is additionally bound to provider, persona, scope,
timestamp and one-use nonce before it may establish content identity, position or
entitlement.  Catalogue identity and household subscription remain separate facts.

\subsection{Private history and watchlists}

Viewing feedback spoken in a shared room creates an unassigned preference.  It remains in
an awaiting-private-claim state until a phone persona accepts it.  The claimant may keep it
private or deliberately share it with the household.  A private history entry cannot
influence another persona or a shared recommendation.

Watchlists follow the same policy.  Each item is owned by one persona and marked private or
explicitly household-shared.  Snapshot queries return the active persona's private items
plus deliberately shared items, never another person's private collection.  Duplicate
title entries for one persona are replaced rather than multiplied.

\subsection{Contextual recommendations without paid AI}

The deterministic recommendation layer accepts provider-supplied candidates and explicit
criteria: mood, maximum duration, time of day and who is present.  It can score a matching
mood, late-evening duration and a provider-supplied family-safety label.  Watched,
disliked and avoided titles are excluded only from the active persona's history or from
history explicitly shared with the household.  Every candidate retains
\emph{entitlement unknown until authenticated provider confirmation}.

This ranking path requires no paid model.  Local or optional cloud intelligence may improve
the explanation or candidate acquisition, but it cannot weaken privacy, evidence or
entitlement rules.

\subsection{Continue watching, reminders and room handoff}

Continue watching uses the last provider route verified for the same persona and creates a
new private confirmation.  Signed playback telemetry can restore title, state and position
after restart only when its identity and scope match the execution.  Without that adapter,
Pilot prepares the official route but does not claim the previous position was resumed.

Episode reminders require exact provider, provider-content and episode metadata plus a
future timestamp.  The initial reminder is stored in the persona's local private inbox and
states that external push is not scheduled.  Native push requires a separately signed phone
adapter and delivery receipt.

Cross-room continuation reuses the television room and playback handoff contract.  It
prepares a persona-bound destination action and requires destination verification.  It
does not claim synchronized playback because two applications opened.

\subsection{Provider health, sign-in recovery and sharing}

Provider health records distinguish available, degraded, outage, sign-in-required and
unknown states with source, authentication status and observation time.  Sign-in recovery
opens an official provider surface for owner action.  Pilot does not inspect, copy or retain
raw credentials.  Degraded and outage states suggest retry or another verified provider
rather than performing blind navigation.

Programme sending uses signed Pilot Moments and exact official links.  Protected programme
media is not copied.  Provider-native sharing and recipient opening receipts remain
adapter-gated.

\subsection{Verification and remaining provider evidence}

Automated tests cover strict route classification, confirmation and playback boundaries,
signed telemetry reconciliation, persona isolation, private/shared watchlists, unassigned
shared-room history, deterministic ranking, exact-metadata reminders and credential-free
health recovery.  The locally enforceable Section 7 scope is complete.

Real provider account adapters, native push, provider-native sharing, synchronized watch
together and per-service model/region field matrices remain external closure evidence.
They are recorded in the Section 7 blocker register and cannot be replaced by a successful
search result, application launch or simulated provider response.
