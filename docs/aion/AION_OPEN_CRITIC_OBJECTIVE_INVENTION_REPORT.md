# AION Open Critic-Objective Invention

## Result

AION selected what behavioural risk deserved falsification without receiving a
failure category or expected invariant. It searched safe behavioural probes,
measured unlabelled disagreement between candidate strategies, rejected
semantically invalid objectives, compiled the selected risk into an executable
critic contract, and transferred it across three computation families.

Private procedure:

`procedure_open_critic_objective_invention_v1`

Promoted delayed runtime procedure:

`procedure_delayed_critic_objective_runtime_promotion_v1`

## Objective discovery

The engine received competing candidate programs and observations but no label
identifying robustness, contamination, trend, location, derivative failure or
the expected invariant. It generated four bounded probes:

1. global translation;
2. global positive scaling;
3. temporal reversal; and
4. sparse boundary replacement.

Global scaling and temporal reversal were rejected as attractive but invalid
critic objectives because they change the semantics of the target computation.
Global translation produced no material disagreement. Sparse replacement
produced repeated normalized candidate disagreement of 167.667 and was the only
operationalizable repeated residual.

AION inferred and compiled:

`LOCALIZED_INFLUENCE_CONCENTRATION_FALSIFICATION`

with the risk hypothesis:

> one local observation has disproportionate global influence.

The objective was selected before any independent success label was revealed.
Outcome authority was applied only after the objective had been committed.

After runtime promotion, a fresh objective runtime transferred without private
state or repeated objective search to a fourth family: it rejected full-range
spread and selected robust interquartile spread on an unfamiliar numerical
sequence.

The retained objective therefore transferred across trend, central-location and
change-estimation problems. A retained objective uses one selected diagnostic
probe instead of repeating the four-probe cold search, reducing diagnostic
probe cost by 75%.

## Abstention and security

When both competing candidates exhibited identical behaviour under every safe
probe, AION abstained with `no_repeated_operationalizable_risk`. It did not
invent a criticism merely to force separation.

Eight adversarial objective programs were rejected. They attempted to acquire
network, filesystem or process authority; perform destructive probing; use a
hidden oracle as selection authority; generalize from one context; remove the
delayed gate; or embed executable code. Unsafe execution, live writes and
ambient-authority expansion remained zero.

## Delayed promotion

The private objective passed development, source-disjoint trend transfer and
two cross-family transfers. Its exact digest
`05dd1c2980195a22270b1a280cea285f569acec2ab40614f3f5561e621ccf935`
was precommitted while the runtime objective registry remained absent. After the
real five-minute boundary, all four public authorities were reacquired and
reproduced the committed discrimination. CAU promoted
`procedure_delayed_critic_objective_runtime_promotion_v1` and installed the
exact objective. Public payloads were stable, establishing delayed retained
execution rather than external change.

## Boundary and next target

This is bounded open critic-objective invention. The safe-probe vocabulary,
candidate programs, authority cohort and later success labels remain engineered.
It is not unrestricted autonomous scientific criticism, AGA or AGI.

The next removal target is open diagnostic experiment invention: after choosing
a risk, AION must construct a new information-producing intervention when none
of its existing safe probes can resolve whether the apparent defect belongs to
the candidate, evidence, environment or critic.
