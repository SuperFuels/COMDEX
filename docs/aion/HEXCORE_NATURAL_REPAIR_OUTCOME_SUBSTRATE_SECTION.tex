\subsection{Authority and Claim Boundary}

The failures and repair revisions are authentic Git history. However, commit
selection, five repair-strategy families, feature extraction, reflection contract,
three-case portfolio, and hidden evaluator remain engineered. This establishes
bounded blind repair, outcome-grounded failure-state invention, and verified
neural--symbolic reflection. It does not establish AGI, unrestricted software
engineering, or autonomous deployment.

\subsection{Next Gate}

The next evaluation must be administered independently. It should provide
read-only failing source and logs without repository history, retain human
repairs and hidden tests outside AION's environment, include unrelated
repositories and languages, and compare AION under matched budgets against
frontier agents, open models, and human baselines. No internally scored result
may authorize that promotion.
