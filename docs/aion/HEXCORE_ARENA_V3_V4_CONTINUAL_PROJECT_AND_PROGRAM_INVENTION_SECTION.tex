\section{Continual Project Adaptation and Open Experiment-Program Invention}
\label{sec:arena-v3-v4}

Arena v3 and Arena v4 extend the open-world control plane from evidence
acquisition into persistent, consequence-bearing action.  Arena v3 tests
whether verified experience can improve behaviour across changing projects.
Arena v4 removes the supplied menu of complete actions and requires AION to
compose typed experiment programs from smaller safe primitives.  Both phases
retain the central authority invariant:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{proposal or synthesized program}
&\not\Rightarrow \text{accepted capability}, \\
\text{execution, withheld outcomes, and CAU}
&\Rightarrow \text{promotion authority}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:arena-v3-v4-authority}
\end{equation}

The internally promoted procedures are
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize\texttt{procedure\_long\_running\_changing\_project\_arena\_v3\_f948c2272b31}},
\end{center}
and
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize\texttt{procedure\_open\_experiment\_program\_arena\_v4\_7b66f2843ca9}}.
\end{center}
Their respective parents are the promoted Arena v2 evidence-acquisition
procedure and the promoted Arena v3 changing-project procedure.


\subsection{Arena v3: long-running changing projects}
\label{subsec:arena-v3}

\subsubsection{Research question}

Arena v3 asks whether AION can retain and improve an investigation policy when
outcomes arrive only after action commitment, the governing rule changes during
the project, process reconstruction occurs after every milestone, accumulated
memory may help or mislead, actions have explicit costs, and some regimes may
lie outside every available model.

The control loop is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{broad project objective}
&\rightarrow \text{typed parameter proposal} \\
&\rightarrow \text{pre-execution safety gate} \\
&\rightarrow \text{isolated experiment} \\
&\rightarrow \text{delayed independent outcome} \\
&\rightarrow \text{failure signature or model criticism} \\
&\rightarrow \text{retained repair memory} \\
&\rightarrow \text{costed stability monitor} \\
&\rightarrow \text{revision or continuation} \\
&\rightarrow \text{checkpoint and process reconstruction} \\
&\rightarrow \text{retention, ablation, and CAU gates}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v3-loop}
\end{equation}

The controller does not receive the hidden regime label.  Before an outcome is
revealed, each executed action records a cryptographic commitment to that
regime.  This separates action selection from retrospective knowledge of the
answer.


\subsubsection{Executed outcome surfaces}

Four distinct project surfaces were exercised:
\begin{description}
  \item[Software execution.] An isolated Python subprocess transforms event
  records.  The withheld interface changes from first-occurrence
  deduplication to canonical latest-value serialization.

  \item[Database execution.] A SQLite database is constructed inside a
  disposable workspace.  The independently checked integrity requirement
  changes from unique event identifiers to referential integrity.  Invalid
  writes must be rejected transactionally without erasing committed records.

  \item[Sensor reasoning.] A CSV observation stream is evaluated against a
  withheld continuous reference.  The noise process changes from distributed
  low-amplitude noise to impulsive outliers, requiring a transition from mean
  filtering to robust median filtering under an absolute error ceiling.

  \item[Document world modelling.] A disk-backed dependency manifest records
  evidence, analysis, report, and verification artifacts.  The required stale
  set changes from direct propagation to transitive propagation after an
  upstream revision.
\end{description}

Every subprocess, database, trace, and dependency graph is confined to a
temporary workspace.  The live repository is read-only for this arena.


\subsubsection{Continual generations and outcome memory}

Development outcomes constructed a provenance-bearing mapping from project
family and raw failure signature to a verified repair.  The mapping was tested
on renamed sealed projects rather than the development instances.  Three
governed generations separated memory and routing effects:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{|c|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{5.6cm}|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Generation} & \textbf{Policy} & \textbf{Project success} & \textbf{Mean action cost} \\
\hline
0 & No accumulated outcome memory & $100\%$ & $11.00$ \\
\hline
1 & Outcome memory without conditional routing & $100\%$ & $8.50$ \\
\hline
2 & Outcome memory plus costed stability routing & $100\%$ & $2.75$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The relative reductions were
\begin{equation}
1-\frac{2.75}{11.00}=75.00\%,
\qquad
1-\frac{2.75}{8.50}=67.65\%.
\label{eq:arena-v3-cost-reductions}
\end{equation}
These gains were reproduced across three independent execution seeds.  The
costed monitor preserves a currently verified policy while outcomes remain
stable, detects a change when the monitor fails, records the new failure
signature, and activates a previously verified repair only when needed.

\subsubsection{Falsification, safety, and unknown-model criticism}

Four malicious candidates requested dynamic execution, network access,
destructive SQL, or live-path traversal.  All four were blocked before
execution.  No unsafe action ran and no live repository file changed.

An additional out-of-distribution sensor regime was not expressible by any
retained filter model.  After all candidates failed independent verification,
AION recorded
\begin{center}
\texttt{EVENT\_OUTSIDE\_AVAILABLE\_ACTION\_MODEL\_FAMILY}
\end{center}
and abstained instead of selecting the least-bad known model.

\subsection{Arena v4: open experiment-program composition}
\label{subsec:arena-v4}

Arena v3 still selected complete parameters from a finite action menu.  Arena
v4 removes that menu.  It asks whether verified experience can teach AION how
to construct a new typed action program while preserving outcome authority,
active falsification, intervention efficiency, transfer, security, persistence,
and explicit criticism of the available representation.


\subsubsection{Synthesis and verification architecture}

The Arena v4 pipeline is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{broad outcome contract}
&\rightarrow \text{safe typed primitive grammar} \\
&\rightarrow \text{single- and multi-primitive candidates} \\
&\rightarrow \text{maximum-disagreement experiment} \\
&\rightarrow \text{outcome reveal after query commitment} \\
&\rightarrow \text{candidate elimination by counterexample} \\
&\rightarrow \text{minimum-complexity survivor} \\
&\rightarrow \text{sealed post-selection verification} \\
&\rightarrow \text{governed adapter execution} \\
&\rightarrow \text{motif retention or abstention} \\
&\rightarrow \text{CAU promotion or rejection}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v4-synthesis}
\end{equation}

For survivor set $\mathcal{H}$ and candidate experiment $x$, the active
falsification policy selects
\begin{equation}
x^{*}
=
\arg\min_{x}
\max_{y}
\left|
\left\{h\in\mathcal{H}:h(x)=y\right\}
\right|,
\label{eq:arena-v4-disagreement}
\end{equation}
thereby minimizing the largest remaining outcome partition.  The environment,
not the proposed program, supplies the outcome.


\subsubsection{Typed primitive grammars}

The compiler operated over four typed grammars:

\begin{center}

\hline

\hline
Sensor & raw, mean window, median window, clipping, lag & robust observation pipeline \\
\hline
Documents & direct dependents, transitive closure, global invalidation & revision-propagation procedure \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Complete solutions were not supplied as named actions.  Programs were composed
from the primitives, including the two-stage software program
\begin{equation}
\texttt{last\_by\_key}
\rightarrow
\texttt{sort\_key}.
\label{eq:arena-v4-compound-program}
\end{equation}
A synthesized program could execute only if a verified lowering contract
connected it to a governed Python, SQLite, sensor, or document adapter.


\subsubsection{Negative result and dual accounting}

The first Arena v4 challenger recovered and executed every correct program but
required exactly as many environmental queries as cold synthesis.  It therefore
failed the transfer-efficiency gate and was rejected.

The corrected design introduced dual accounting:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item a retained motif receives one real environmental confirmation;
  \item the program is selected before a source-disjoint property suite is
        revealed;
  \item post-selection verification is reported separately and cannot be
        described as a free environmental intervention; and
  \item failed confirmation or failed sealed verification triggers fallback or
        rejection.
\end{enumerate}

This preserves the authority of independent verification while allowing prior
experience to reduce real-world intervention cost.

\subsubsection{Complete-grammar criticism and security}

The OOD sensor world used a nonlinear sine response not expressible by the raw,
smoothing, clipping, or lag primitives.  The retained median-filter motif failed
confirmation, synthesis failed the withheld property suite, and no program was
retained.  This demonstrates criticism of the complete available grammar; it
does not demonstrate unrestricted primitive invention.

Programs requesting dynamic execution, network access, filesystem writing, or
authority override were outside every permitted grammar.  All four were
rejected before execution.  Neural or language proposals cannot add a primitive
to the authority grammar, decide an outcome, reveal sealed properties, lower a
program through an unverified adapter, modify the live repository, or approve
their own promotion.


\subsection{Combined capability interpretation}
\label{subsec:arena-v3-v4-interpretation}

Together, the two promotions establish the following bounded chain:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{verified project outcomes}
&\rightarrow \text{retained repair and primitive motifs} \\
&\rightarrow \text{newly composed experiment program} \\
&\rightarrow \text{active counterexample search} \\
&\rightarrow \text{fewer real interventions} \\
&\rightarrow \text{governed execution} \\
&\rightarrow \text{change-sensitive adaptation} \\
&\rightarrow \text{persistent transferable skill}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v3-v4-combined-chain}
\end{equation}

Arena v3 shows that verified experience changes future project behaviour and
reduces repeated investigation.  Arena v4 moves one level deeper: verified
experience changes how AION constructs the action itself.  The result is a
bounded form of continual project adaptation and experiment-program invention,
not evidence of unrestricted general intelligence.


\subsection{Authority and claim boundary}
\label{subsec:arena-v3-v4-boundary}

The following components remain engineered:
\begin{itemize}
  \item objectives and project families;
  \item primitive vocabularies and type systems;
  \item candidate-program depth;
  \item interpreters and adapter lowerings;
  \item outcome cases and sealed evaluators;
  \item controlled sensor traces; and
  \item internal benchmark administration.
\end{itemize}

Several gates remain explicitly unpassed: natural multimodal semantic
grounding; independently judged social and creative work; substrate
comparison; long projects against independently changing systems; and external
administration.  The reported $100\%$ internal results therefore describe only
the declared sealed cohorts.  They do not establish AGI, unrestricted
autonomy, general software engineering, or independent certification.


\subsection{Next primitive-invention and external-outcome contract}
\label{subsec:arena-v5-contract}

The next decisive stage must remove further scaffolding rather than add another
closed grammar exercise:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item detect systematic residuals that no retained primitive grammar can
        express;
  \item synthesize a private primitive implementation in a sandbox;
  \item generate algebraic, behavioral, security, and adversarial
        counterexamples;
  \item admit the primitive only after positive transfer to source-disjoint
        projects and independent outcomes;
  \item connect long-running projects to externally maintained repository
        tests, databases, delayed public observations, and controlled
        applications;
  \item introduce natural images, diagrams, tables, and text with deliberate
        cross-modal contradiction;
  \item use several independent human judges for commonsense, social, and
        creative objectives while retaining disagreement;
  \item run many continual generations with forward-transfer, forgetting,
        calibration, complexity, and retirement/revival measurements;
  \item compare replaceable proposal substrates under identical evidence,
        action, compute, outcome, and CAU budgets; and
  \item submit final portfolios, outcomes, and scoring to an external
        administrator.
\end{enumerate}

Future internal work should be rejected unless it directly removes one of
these boundaries or produces independently reproducible transfer.
