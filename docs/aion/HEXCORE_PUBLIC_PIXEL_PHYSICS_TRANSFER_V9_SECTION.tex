\section{Arena v9: Public Pixel-Physics Transfer}
\label{sec:arena-v9-public-physics}

Arena v9 transfers AION's physical learning loop into transition and reward
authority implemented by the third-party Farama Gymnasium project. Gymnasium
1.2.3 owns MountainCar-v0 and CartPole-v1 dynamics, termination and reward.
The AION path discards numerical simulator observations and derives control
only from rendered RGB frames.

\subsection{Outcome-selected visual feedback}

MountainCar perception recovers wheel position and estimates motion by frame
difference. Four bounded feedback programs competed on three development
seeds; outcomes selected a momentum program. CartPole perception grounds the
axle and pole in pixels. Four candidate controllers competed, selecting
\begin{equation}
 u_t=\mathbf{1}\!\left[\theta_t+2(\theta_t-\theta_{t-1})>0\right].
\end{equation}
Both programs were frozen before evaluation on six new seeds per environment.
A matched zero-experience control received identical pixels, actions and
budgets but used the first unevaluated program. Every action was SHA--256
committed before the simulator returned its consequence.
