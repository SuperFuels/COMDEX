\section{Return Register: Mobile Delivery, Native Phones, Calls and Wave Transport}
\label{sec:pilot-mobile-delivery-calls-wave-return-register}

This section is the authoritative return register for the work that could not be
closed during the Mobile Delivery, Responsive Qualification and Sovereign Transport
Hardening stage.  The underlying software foundations are implemented and tested;
the following items require external accounts, signed native applications,
representative physical devices, specialist review or radio hardware.  None of these
items may be marked complete using a mock response, an interface demonstration or a
locally reconstructed packet.

\subsection{Real provider qualification}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Task, calendar and communication providers
  (\texttt{MOB-0514}).} Connect persona-specific, user-authorized accounts for at
  least one real task service, one real calendar and one real communication channel.
  Exercise creation, modification or delivery only after the trusted phone displays
  the exact account, recipient, content and scope.  Completion requires provider
  receipts, idempotency evidence and confirmation that shared-TV speech cannot choose
  another person's account.

  \item \textbf{SMS field delivery.} Connect an authorized SMS provider account to
  the implemented adapter.  Send to a controlled real recipient, authenticate the
  provider's status callback and record provider acceptance and carrier delivery as
  separate states.  Human reading or agreement must not be inferred from either
  state.

  \item \textbf{Official WhatsApp route (\texttt{MOB-0703}).} Obtain an officially
  authorized WhatsApp Business Platform sender or another explicitly permitted
  provider route.  Bind it to the correct persona, complete exact private approval,
  verify the provider receipt and test revocation.  Browser automation, copied login
  sessions and unofficial protocol clients are prohibited substitutes.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Signed native applications and representative devices}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Signed iOS application (\texttt{MOB-1101}).} Build the existing
  native iOS source with the full Apple toolchain, bind the approved application
  identifier and entitlements, sign it with an authorized development identity and
  install it on representative physical iPhones.

  \item \textbf{Signed Android application (\texttt{MOB-1102}).} Build the existing
  native Android source with the production Android toolchain, sign it with the
  controlled release key and install it on representative physical Android phones.

  \item \textbf{Physical accessibility and family qualification
  (\texttt{MOB-1110}).} Test VoiceOver, TalkBack, dynamic type, large controls,
  reduced motion, camera and microphone permission flows, guardian-controlled child
  journeys, notification privacy, phone locking, application suspension, background
  delivery, network changes and biometric approval on representative devices.

  \item \textbf{Store and privacy approval (\texttt{MOB-1111}).} Complete Apple and
  Google signing, privacy manifests and declarations, permission explanations,
  export/compliance declarations, release-key custody and store review.  Store
  acceptance must not weaken customer ownership or move mother-brain secrets into a
  Tessaris-operated service.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Independent call-encryption assurance}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Audited end-to-end media encryption (\texttt{MOB-1201}).} Submit the
  native WebRTC media construction, participant authentication, reconnect epochs,
  group-key rotation, dependency inventory and threat model to an independent
  cryptographic/security reviewer.  Resolve material findings and publish a bounded
  assurance statement identifying precisely what was reviewed.

  \item Repeat real-device calls under packet loss, jitter, network switching,
  suspension and reconnection.  Confirm that removed participants and revoked or
  compromised devices cannot regain media or group authority.  These field results
  supplement the completed automated adverse-network tests; they do not replace the
  independent audit.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Physical Wave and direct-radio completion}

Wave remains hidden and optional until every applicable item below is proven.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Genuine direct adapters (\texttt{MOB-1307}).} Implement actual BLE
  and Wi-Fi Direct transports only against supported operating-system and hardware
  interfaces.  The adapters must preserve signed enrollment, capability leases,
  fragmentation, integrity, TTL, priority, retry, deduplication and truthful delivery
  states.  Merely labelling an IP or mock transport as BLE or Wi-Fi Direct is not
  acceptable.

  \item \textbf{Hardware qualification (\texttt{MOB-1308}).} Select supported
  ESP32, Raspberry Pi or dedicated radio/bridge hardware and record model, firmware,
  antenna, power source, range, throughput, duty cycle, thermal behavior, reconnect
  behavior and failure modes.  Test bridge revocation and automatic mock-driver
  exclusion on the real device.

  \item \textbf{Regional compliance (\texttt{MOB-1309}).} For every intended
  market and radio profile, obtain competent confirmation of permitted frequency,
  transmit power, bandwidth, duty cycle, equipment certification, labeling and
  operator obligations.  A laboratory success is not authorization to transmit.

  \item \textbf{Internet-disconnected field proof (\texttt{MOB-1310}).} Place two
  independently controlled AION mothers on qualified hardware, physically remove
  internet connectivity and exchange both text and push-to-talk traffic.  Completion
  requires authenticated sender and recipient receipts, complete payload integrity,
  duplicate suppression, store-and-forward recovery after interruption and proof that
  local reconstruction was not misreported as over-the-air delivery.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Product and governance field closure}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Representative field pilots (\texttt{MOB-1409}).} Run consented,
  documented trials with a household, freelancer, employee and Boardroom user.
  Exercise identity separation, invitations, tasks, approvals, organization expiry,
  shared-TV logout and recovery.  Record failures, corrections and participant
  acceptance without collecting unnecessary private content.

  \item \textbf{Independent product review (\texttt{MOB-1410}).} Complete security,
  privacy, data-protection, consumer, employment, child-safety, communications and
  relevant regulatory review before general release.  Track findings to verified
  remediation or a clearly accepted residual risk.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Final closure rule}

An item leaves this register only when it has a dated implementation or field
reference, reproducible evidence, an accountable owner and a verified outcome.  The
canonical mobile checklist must be updated at the same time.  Until then Pilot must
continue to label unavailable providers, unaudited media, unqualified hardware and
unverified delivery honestly, while keeping Wave and unfinished native distribution
outside ordinary consumer claims.
