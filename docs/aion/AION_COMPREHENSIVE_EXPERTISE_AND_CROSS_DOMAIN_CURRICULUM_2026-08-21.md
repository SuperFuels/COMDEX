# AION Comprehensive Expertise and Cross-Domain Curriculum

**Date:** 21 August 2026  
**Phase status:** Curriculum architecture built and machine-validated  
**Validation:** PASS  
**Important boundary:** This phase defines what AION must learn and how it must
be tested. It does not claim that AION already possesses the listed expertise.

## 1. Objective

The programme is designed to turn isolated problem solving into cumulative,
cross-domain expertise. AION should be able to encounter an unfamiliar problem,
identify the knowledge it lacks, acquire the necessary subject foundations,
develop broad expertise, connect that expertise to other disciplines, pass
independent examinations, retain the capability and continue extending the
knowledge graph as the world changes.

The intended compounding cycle is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{unfamiliar problem}
&\longrightarrow \text{knowledge-gap diagnosis}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{subject and prerequisite map}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{source acquisition and structured learning}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{practice, failure and repair}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{source-disjoint examination}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{functional memory and delayed retention}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{cross-domain synthesis}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{real project and measured outcome}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{new questions and deeper expertise}.
\end{aligned}
\]

There is deliberately no finite “curriculum complete” state. New problems,
unknown terminology, failed transfer, contradictory evidence, discoveries and
stale knowledge all reopen learning.

Each ability must be tested through an unfamiliar problem, source-disjoint

does not pass it.

## 3. Mastery ladder

Every subject is tracked through six levels:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Unassessed | No reliable evidence |
| Aware | Knows the map, terminology and important unknowns |
| Functional | Solves ordinary bounded problems with verification |
| Proficient | Handles difficult unfamiliar transfer and repairs failures |
| Expert | Completes multiple real projects, retains competence and can teach it |
| Research-capable | Maps the frontier and produces novel, falsifiable work that survives replication and external scrutiny |

Expert status requires, at minimum, a source-disjoint knowledge examination,
three practical projects, two unfamiliar transfer projects, two verified
failure repairs, independent authority, teaching evidence, and delayed tests
after 1, 7, 30 and 90 days.

## 5. The cross-domain intelligence graph

Subject expertise is insufficient if every discipline remains isolated. The
curriculum therefore includes an explicit bridge graph.

Knowledge nodes may represent concepts, claims, equations, procedures, models,
components, constraints, measurements, failures, questions, sources and
authorities. Cross-domain edges may represent prerequisite, part-of, causal,
constraint, implementation, measurement, optimisation, analogy,
contradiction, support, regulation, manufacture or transfer relationships.

Every retained node must preserve:

- provenance;
- confidence;
- valid scope;
- known counterexamples;
- last verification time; and
- retention status.

Unknowns and contradictions remain first-class graph nodes. AION must not
silently fill them with plausible text.

The first graph contains 29 verified bridge targets. Examples include:

\[
\text{battery chemistry}
\rightarrow \text{energy density and degradation}
\rightarrow \text{drone mass and endurance},
\]

\[
\text{control theory}
\rightarrow \text{flight stability}
\rightarrow \text{embedded implementation}
\rightarrow \text{energy consumption},
\]

\[
\text{CAD and materials}
\rightarrow \text{frame strength and drag}
\rightarrow \text{manufacturing process}
\rightarrow \text{yield and unit cost},
\]

\[
\text{agronomy}
\rightarrow \text{crop-stress objective}
\rightarrow \text{sensor selection}
\rightarrow \text{drone field route}
\rightarrow \text{economic outcome},
\]

and

\[
\text{scientific method}
\rightarrow \text{business experiment}
\rightarrow \text{causal measurement}
\rightarrow \text{operational decision}.
\]

Each proposed bridge must be derived, attacked with counterexamples and applied
in an unfamiliar project before it can be treated as learned.

## 6. Parallel learning lanes

AION should not choose between solving today’s problem and developing broad
expertise. It should run five governed lanes:

1. **Immediate problem lane:** acquire what is required to solve the present
   mission safely.
2. **Broad subject academy:** continue building the wider foundations of every
   discovered subject.
3. **Cross-domain synthesis:** search for useful relationships and shared
   variables across subjects.
4. **Retention and repair:** re-examine old capabilities and repair forgetting
   or contradictions.
5. **Frontier questions:** investigate unresolved, high-value questions after
   the required foundations and authorities exist.

During bootstrapping, the planned allocation is 30% learning abilities, 35%
foundations, 15% subject depth, 10% cross-domain projects and 10% retention and
repair. In the mature programme, the allocation shifts to 30% subject depth,
25% cross-domain projects, 15% frontier research, 20% retention and repair and
10% continuing breadth discovery.

## 10. Assessment and honesty controls

- Teachers and source models propose curricula but cannot award competence.
- Examinations remain source-disjoint.
- Self-scoring cannot promote a capability.
- Full AION is compared with cold, no-memory, no-repair and subject-isolated
  controls.
- Competence is retested after 1, 7, 30 and 90 days.
- Expert claims require unfamiliar real projects and independent outcomes.
- Research claims require literature review, adversarial attack, replication
  and external scrutiny.
- A curriculum entry, document download or completed reading task is never
  evidence of expertise by itself.

## 11. Safety and high-stakes governance

Military and cybersecurity knowledge is dual-use. The curriculum permits
benign, defensive, safety, resilience and compliance applications. It does not
authorise weapon construction, targeting or harmful operational enablement.

Medical, legal and financial actions require current authoritative sources and
qualified human authority. Physical execution requires authorised hardware,
sandboxing, a safety case and human approval.

These restrictions constrain actions, not legitimate high-level understanding.
AION may learn history, strategy, risk, protection and system safety without
being authorised to cause harm.

## 17. Governed Prediction-Market Intelligence Arena

On 22 August 2026 a real-world prediction-market pathway was added. The live
curriculum now contains 90 subjects, 68 cross-domain bridges and ten capstones.
Five new academies cover probabilistic forecasting and calibration;
prediction-market microstructure; event evidence and resolution research;
elections, polling and public opinion; and prediction-market design, integrity
and compliance. A new `governed_prediction_market_fund` capstone joins those
subjects to risk management, psychology, geopolitics, regulation, data science
and software engineering.

The first live read-only run accessed public Polymarket and Kalshi endpoints,
observed 100 markets from each platform and froze a 200-market snapshot with
SHA-256
`13072ef24e728b8fdd2861e69a16833db1cf72d3e46e919e8f46f59fa2909ac0`.
Polymarket's official geoblock endpoint reported that the observed Spanish IP
was not blocked. No API credentials were loaded, no orders or market proposals
were submitted, no wallet was funded and no live capital was placed at risk.

The arena distinguishes a research candidate from a trading edge. Liquidity,
spread and resolution-source quality can prioritize a market for investigation,
but they cannot establish expected profit. A paper position is permitted only
after AION commits an independently derived probability and uncertainty bound.
Its conservative edge must remain positive after uncertainty, fees and
slippage. Paper positions are capped at one per cent of the paper bankroll and
are scored only against later platform settlements using Brier score, net
return, calibration and drawdown.

The live-capital gate is deliberately separate. Before AION may even prepare a
live order proposal, it must have at least 100 settled forecasts, beat the
market's Brier score by at least one percentage point, show positive paper
returns after all costs and keep paper drawdown below five per cent. The owner
must verify account ownership and jurisdiction, sign a mandate limiting each
order to one per cent of bankroll, daily loss to two per cent and total exposure
to five per cent, and approve every individual order during qualification.
Passing this gate still does not enable execution; it only permits an exact
bounded order proposal for separate approval.

Users cannot directly create arbitrary Polymarket markets. They may submit a
community market proposal. Kalshi accepts regulated market suggestions and
offers API creation only within approved multivariate event collections. AION
therefore generates objective, source-backed, manipulation-resistant proposal
drafts and does not misrepresent a suggestion as a created market.

The mastery service now refreshes public prediction-market intelligence every
15 minutes by default while the curriculum learns the prerequisite subjects.
The claim boundary remains strict: observing live markets is real-world input,
but it is not evidence of alpha. Profitability and world-class forecasting can
only be claimed after a substantial independently settled record survives
costs, changing regimes and comparison with market and cold-control baselines.

### 17.1 Fast-settlement paper trading game

A persistent paper game is now active with a starting account of USD 1,000.
It uses public, timestamped platform prices and official later settlements; it
loads no credentials, submits no orders and risks no money. Each virtual bet is
capped at USD 10, new daily allocation at USD 100 and simultaneous exposure at
USD 50. This permits up to ten entries per UTC day, but never more than five
positions simultaneously. A market must have an identified resolution source, a usable order
book, a spread no greater than six percentage points and a scheduled close no
more than 24 hours after entry. Markets that are unavailable for live execution
may be observed in the paper game, but the restriction is retained on the
position and can never authorize a live order.

The initial strategy is frozen as a falsifiable calibration experiment until
20 settlements. It tests whether extreme crowd probabilities are overconfident
by shrinking the market probability 30 per cent toward one half, then requiring
at least a 2.5-percentage-point residual model edge after a one-per-cent
simulated cost. This is not a claim that the strategy possesses alpha.

The first live paper position was opened from market snapshot SHA-256
`9d2189b847ec90848eb318a1eccec83bc06373403605d2e9c5f9fdb25129d9d2`.
It allocated USD 10 to YES at 0.36 on the public Polymarket contract
``Spread: Baltimore Orioles (-1.5)'', with a precommitted model probability of
0.3985 and estimated edge after costs of 0.0285. The contract was scheduled to
close at 23:59:59 UTC on 21 August 2026. Immediately after entry the paper
account was USD 999.90 because the USD 0.10 simulated cost was charged at entry.
The result remains pending until the platform reports an official settlement;

The dashboard now reports account value, total bets, open and settled

### 17.2 Market-blind, source-backed forecasting gate

The initial calibration strategy did not research the event itself. Its nine
positions remain preserved as a labelled baseline and are not retrospectively
described as researched forecasts. After four settlements that baseline stood
at one win and three losses, with USD 12.62 realised paper loss. This small
sample establishes neither skill nor failure, but it demonstrated why price
transformation alone is not a sufficient claim of edge.

Every future paper entry now requires a frozen version-two research packet.
The researcher receives the event question, rules, schedule and resolution
source, but not the market probability, bid, ask, spread, volume, odds or crowd
forecast. It must use at least three cited sources across at least two domains,
including an official or statistical source and independent reporting. It must
record a base rate, evidence for and against, key variables, missing evidence,
a point probability and an absolute uncertainty margin. Betting, odds, tip and
prediction-market sites are excluded. A packet fails closed if the event has
started, the evidence is one-sided, the provenance is inadequate, the forecast
is stale or the researcher abstains.

Only after the packet is hash-frozen does the paper engine compare its
independent interval with the live order book. A position is permitted only if
the conservative edge remains at least 2.5 percentage points after uncertainty
and simulated costs. Research expires after six hours, and no position may open
once the event is live or ended. The USD 10 order, USD 100 daily-risk and USD 50
simultaneous-exposure limits remain unchanged.

The first strong pass produced three usable version-two packets. The market-
blind estimates were: Brentford to win, (0.54\pm0.08); Sunderland to win,
(0.42\pm0.12); and FURIA to win its best-of-three series,
(0.62\pm0.10). Their frozen packet SHA-256 values are respectively
`34d38ea7f9bd1fb82238480db20312d8cf92ff771857e0540735a08573e8022b`,
`414f2639cfc5e2e5dc3d6ac06ceef0a039e4f27e0318436ba72074d18ad530dd`
and
`f77c22f9686443f237d0b7d259d4b9f262a829d53224f2dd7e46ab3a9ac507a8`.
Three earlier packets from the weaker provenance format remain retained for
audit but are excluded from entry decisions.

No version-two researched position opened during this pass because the five
existing positions already occupied the full USD 50 exposure allowance. This
is correct fail-closed behaviour, not a missed execution. When capacity becomes
available, only a fresh, pre-event, version-two packet can authorize a new
paper entry. Research quality and profitability remain separate claims: the
new gate proves that analysis occurred before price comparison, but only later
independent settlements and baseline comparisons can show whether it adds
forecasting value.

## 18. Governed practical-testing authority

1. permission to practise a subject in a bounded environment; and
2. permission to award competence from an independently verified outcome.

All 150 installed subjects now have an authorised practical route. The router
assigns each subject to one of seven environments: a private executable
sandbox, delayed paper outcomes, an owned staging project, a withheld public
dataset, simulation followed by separately approved hardware, an owned isolated
cyber range, or independent human review. A live sandbox canary verifies that
the basic local execution boundary works before the router reports success.

At the time of this checkpoint, 50 of the 150 subjects have verified
competence-scoring adapters. The remaining 100 are explicit adapter-acquisition
gaps with a prioritised queue. AION may practise those subjects immediately,
but their self-scored exercises cannot increase competence. This closes the
absence-of-practice gap without manufacturing independent evidence.

No subject receives unrestricted live authority. Live money, external cyber
targets, consequential external writes and physical actuation all remain
disabled. Paper-market work uses frozen commitments and later independent
settlement. Cybersecurity work is restricted to owned isolated ranges.
Physical work begins in simulation and requires a signed plan and explicit
approval before hardware is operated. Human and creative judgements require an
independent reviewer or measured owner outcome.

The live authority receipt is stored at
`results/hexcore_governed_practical_testing_authority.json`. At this checkpoint
it reports 150/150 bounded practice routes, 50/150 verified competence adapters,

### 18.1 Corrected expert-retention gate

The competency audit found that the previous expert rule accepted two elapsed
retention receipts without identifying their ages. That was insufficient to
support long-term mastery. The expert gate now requires four explicit,
closed-book milestones at 1, 7, 30 and 90 days. Each receipt records its named
milestone, and the scheduler cannot execute it before the corresponding time
measured from the subject's first verified learning evidence.

Existing evidence has not been deleted or rewritten. Instead, four provisional
expert classifications have been honestly reduced to advanced until their
four dated milestones are complete. The current checked ledger therefore
contains zero experts and eleven advanced subjects. This is a stricter and more
useful result: short-term performance remains recognised, while durable mastery
is not claimed before the 90-day examination.

Practice, competence and external authority are therefore three distinct
states. AION can practise every installed subject now; it can earn competence
only where a verified independent adapter exists; and it can affect the outside
world only through a separately scoped approval and outcome authority.

### 18.2 Continuous spaced-practice lane

A persistent rehearsal lane now runs alongside the curriculum. Every ten
minutes it selects the least recently rehearsed subject with a verified safe
adapter, rotates to another declared subskill and executes the adapter's normal
candidate, counterexample and safety checks. Strong subjects therefore remain
in use after their main curriculum block has moved on. The rotation is
persistent across restarts, preserves failures for repair and eventually
revisits all supported subjects rather than repeatedly exercising the current
one.

This lane is intentionally non-promotional. Its receipts contain
`awards_competence=false`; repeated rehearsal cannot manufacture an expert
classification. The separate unfamiliar-project, transfer, independent-outcome
and dated retention examinations remain the level authority. The first live
rehearsal exercised bookkeeping within Accounting and Corporate Finance and
passed its executable counterexample gate with zero external actions. The
current state is stored in
`backend/modules/hexcore/data/progressive_competency/spaced_practice.json`, and
the current public receipt is
`results/hexcore_continuous_spaced_practice.json`.

## 19. Continuous cross-domain association graph

AION now continually reassesses how its subject and concept containers relate.
The original curriculum contained a fixed set of manually declared bridges;
the new dynamic graph scans all 150 installed subjects on every mastery cycle.
It considers direct and transitive prerequisites, curated bridges, shared
declared concepts and shared foundations. It also records current evidence
activity, but activity cannot by itself make a semantic relationship true.

The word ``entanglement'' means a typed, inspectable container association in
this programme. Containers remain distinct. No fact is copied silently from one
domain into another, and mere lexical similarity creates at most a candidate.
Every edge records its supporting signals, confidence, valid scope,
counterexamples, first and last observation times and the following promotion
obligations:

1. derive the explicit mapping;
2. attack it with a counterexample; and
3. apply it in a source-disjoint cross-domain project.

Edges can strengthen, weaken or become stale as the curriculum and evidence
change. Failed candidates remain available for audit instead of disappearing.
Only the third gate can establish a verified transfer relation, and even that
does not establish an unrestricted causal law or award subject competence.

The first live pass produced 1,541 active associations across 150 subjects,
including 1,085 cross-domain links. It recovered, among many others, the
transitive paths from Financial Mathematics and from Markets and Investment to
Hedge-Fund Management and Operations. Sixty-eight curriculum bridges remain
correctly labelled as curated but transfer-unverified; zero dynamic links were
prematurely labelled as verified transfer.

The shared container is
`backend/modules/hexcore/data/expertise_containers/aion__dynamic_associations.json`.
Its persistent reassessment history is stored in
`backend/modules/hexcore/data/expertise_containers/dynamic_association_state.json`,
and the live result is
`results/hexcore_continuous_knowledge_entanglement.json`. The container index
now exposes the association graph to every subject academy for associative
retrieval and cross-domain test selection.

## 20. First-class cognitive control plane

AION now performs a governed search of its own intelligence before beginning a
conversation turn or task. This closes an important gap between retaining
knowledge and actually using it. The control plane identifies the subjects and
skills required by the new request, retrieves relevant canonical claims,
expands through the cross-domain association graph, recalls verified practical
experience and its recorded failures or repairs, and reports any missing
execution capabilities. The resulting context is attached to the governed turn
before a reasoning provider proposes an answer.

The system deliberately keeps three epistemic layers separate:

1. **Canonical truth.** Verified claims and their evidence remain in the
   existing HexCore persistent-learning store. This is the only canonical fact
   authority.
2. **Experience.** Practical outcome capsules preserve what AION attempted,
   what happened, what failed and what repair worked. A verified outcome can be
   reused as experience without being misrepresented as a universal fact.
3. **Encountered material.** User statements, repeated claims, research leads
   and new conjectures are stored separately as unverified candidates. They may
   guide investigation but may not be asserted as truth.

Popularity is not verification. A claim repeated by one million users remains
unverified. Promotion requires an identified verifier, an accepted verification
method, evidence references and a positive Constitutional Authority Unit
decision. Contradictions with canonical truth are explicitly marked instead of
silently overwriting the verified claim. New inventions likewise begin as
proposals and become knowledge only after evidence-based verification.

Every retrieval creates an auditable trace containing the task hash, selected
subjects, claims, experience capsules, procedures and missing capabilities.
Task completion can then record which memories and skills were actually used
and whether an independent verifier confirmed the outcome. This makes it
possible to distinguish “AION had relevant information” from “AION recalled and
successfully applied it.” It also supplies the learning signal required to
improve future routing without turning model output into truth.

The live control plane is implemented in
`backend/modules/hexcore/first_class_cognitive_control_plane.py`. Its persistent
state is `data/hexcore/first_class_cognitive_control_plane.json`, and its public
health receipt is
`results/hexcore_first_class_cognitive_control_plane.json`. It is connected to
`HexCoreGovernedRuntime.begin_turn`, which is used by the canonical cognitive
runtime and AION conversation orchestrator. The mastery service audits the
plane continuously and the learning dashboard reports canonical evidence,
experience, unverified encounters, retrievals and prohibited repetition-based
promotions.

The first live verification used the request to build a responsive HTML site.
AION selected web-platform and software-engineering knowledge, expanded into
related security, user-interface and architecture subjects, recalled four
experience capsules and surfaced four recorded lessons or repairs. It also
reported missing capabilities rather than concealing them. No unverified claim
was treated as canonical and no repeated claim was promoted.

This is a task-time memory and governance system, not evidence of general
intelligence or universal expertise. It currently governs canonical
conversation turns and cognitive-runtime goals. Other legacy processes must be
migrated to the governed runtime before it is accurate to say that literally
every historical AION subsystem uses this control plane.

## 21. Active-runtime migration closure

The executable scope of the preceding claim boundary has now been closed. A
shared standalone-task governance wrapper was added for specialist services
whose deterministic engines do not originate in the conversation runtime. The
wrapper performs governed retrieval before meaningful work begins, exposes the
retrieved intelligence to nested code for the lifetime of the operation, and
records a separate post-task outcome. Retrieval and use remain distinct: a
legacy engine does not receive credit for using a memory merely because that
memory was retrieved.

The following eight supervised entry points now use the control plane:

1. General Apprentice;
2. North-Star Mastery;
3. Mastery Curriculum;
4. Open-Mission Compounding;
5. Open-Mission Executor;
6. Long-Duration Outcome Campaign;
7. Real-Outcome Learning; and
8. the Canonical Cognitive Runtime.

The first seven use the standalone wrapper. The Canonical Cognitive Runtime
uses the native governed-turn integration. Cross-process file locking was added
to the control-plane state so concurrently supervised services cannot overwrite
one another's task traces or outcomes. After restart, all seven standalone
services produced live pre-task retrieval traces. The migration receipt reports
eight of eight active entry points integrated and zero active bypasses.

The migration is audited in
`results/hexcore_active_runtime_cognitive_migration.json`, and its status is
displayed on the live AION learning dashboard. Behavioural verification covers
truth separation, verified promotion, task retrieval, conversation delivery,
concurrent trace preservation, runtime migration and dashboard publication.

The revised claim boundary is precise: every active supervised AION learning
and cognitive service entry point is governed by the first-class control plane.
Archived benchmarks, dormant prototypes, test fixtures and historical source
files are not live runtime entry points and are therefore not counted as active
bypasses. If any such component is reactivated, it must join the migration
registry and pass the same audit before launch.
