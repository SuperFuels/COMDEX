# AION Constitutional North Star and Mastery Registry

## Promotion

The procedure
`procedure_constitutional_north_star_mastery_registry_v1` is internally
promoted. This promotes the constitutional purpose, evidence reconstruction,
capability-query and goal-selection mechanism. It does not promote any subject
to expert or mastered status.

## Constitutional hierarchy

AION now distinguishes five levels of intent:

```text
immutable constitutional purpose
  -> developmental maturity stage
  -> owner-authorized strategic programmes
  -> proposal-only autonomous learning goals
  -> governed actions and independent outcomes
```

The constitutional purpose requires beneficial, evidence-backed knowledge and
capability while preserving human authority, law, consent, safety, provenance
and legitimate uncertainty. Money, infrastructure, access, influence and
self-preservation are explicitly excluded as terminal goals. The purpose hash
cannot be replaced after registration.

## Capability vocabulary

The registry uses six deliberately distinct levels:

1. `unassessed`;
2. `learning`;
3. `operational_bounded`;
4. `proficient_bounded`;
5. `expert`; and
6. `mastered`.

Evidence for a subskill must identify a promoted artifact, its hash, procedure
and outcome authority. A single successful benchmark cannot establish subject
mastery. Expert and mastered status require broad unfamiliar real work,
multi-month retention, independently controlled professional outcomes,
full-subject failure recovery, teaching/leadership and invention outside the
development distribution.

These labels describe demonstrated scope, not general professional
certification. For example, Rust has evidence for ownership, traits, error
handling, testing, concurrency, performance, unsafe review, multi-file design
and toolchain operation. It can therefore undertake substantial bounded Rust
work with compiler and test authority. It is not recorded as mastered because
elapsed, open-distribution and independent professional evidence remain absent.

Accounting is only learning: audit and transactional controls have evidence,
but bookkeeping, financial statements, management accounting, corporate
finance, markets, tax and forecasting remain unearned.

## Operational capability questions

The query surface distinguishes three questions:

- `Do you know Rust?` returns `yes_bounded` and the demonstrated evidence.
- `Can you do a Rust job for me?` returns `yes_with_limits` plus the required
  verification conditions.
- `Have you mastered Rust?` returns `no` plus the missing mastery evidence.

Equivalent queries operate for Python, security, English, mathematics,
accounting, finance, business and the other registered subjects. Unknown
subjects fail closed as unassessed rather than being inferred from language
fluency.

## Scaffolding accounting

The registry now records owner-supplied task fields, AION-derived task fields
and owner fields per verified subject. The current snapshot contains zero
owner-supplied task fields and 80 derived task fields. This establishes a
baseline; it does not yet prove a declining trend. Developer effort used to
construct safe adapters and evaluation harnesses is not yet quantified and is
explicitly withheld from the autonomy claim.

## Persistence and next development

The mastery state and constitutional hash survive reconstruction. A persistent
North-Star service now rebuilds the registry as new verified receipts arrive
and updates the proposal-only strategic portfolio. A direct query utility is
available at `backend/AION/system/aion_capability_registry.py`.

The next development is not to relabel current competence as mastery. It is to
execute the highest-value goals through genuinely different authorities,
measure developer as well as owner scaffolding, transfer methods across
subjects, and earn the remaining general-apprentice criteria.
