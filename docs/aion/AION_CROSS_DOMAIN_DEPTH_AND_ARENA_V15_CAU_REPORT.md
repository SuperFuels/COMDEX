## Competency-control correction

The progressive assessor already required unfamiliar projects, independent
outcomes, sufficient trials and low scaffolding for Advanced competence. The
curriculum generator did not explicitly schedule all of those requirements.
Once familiar project count was satisfied it could move prematurely to
retention and later issue generic repeated trials. This mismatch is corrected.

The curriculum now explicitly schedules:

1. repeated knowledge and practical evidence for every declared subskill;
2. the required number of projects;
3. the independently separate unfamiliar-project threshold;
4. debugging recovery and source-disjoint transfer;
5. delayed closed-book retention;
6. independently owned outcomes, trial volume and declining scaffolding; and
7. fresh consistency work only after the preceding gates are satisfied.

Legacy retention appointments that were created before the unfamiliar-project
gate are retained in the ledger as `superseded_missing_prerequisite`. They award
no evidence and no previous evidence is deleted or rewritten.

## Useful-work-to-competency bridge

AION's live North-Star controller had already completed later-confirmed projects
under four relevant independent authority families. A new bridge rereads these
records and imports them as unfamiliar practical evidence only when all of the
following independently hold:

- a pre-action commitment exists;
- a later outcome hash exists;
- the later evaluator passed the work;
- at least sixty seconds separated commitment and authority;
- owner interventions were zero;
- the retained artifact exists; and
- its SHA-256 digest still matches the committed digest.

Twelve records passed revalidation: three each for Mathematics, English, Python
and Scientific Method. The bridge awarded no retention evidence and cannot by
itself award Advanced or Expert competence. Re-running it imports zero duplicate
records while rechecking all retained hashes.
