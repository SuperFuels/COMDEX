HexCore promoted `procedure_heterogeneous_project_contract_invention_v1`.
The cohort was drawn from genuine completed North-Star projects already present
in AION's delayed-outcome ledger. The inventor received only the objective,
success statement and artifact bytes. Family labels, prior verifier metadata

## Invented contracts

The projects required different verification semantics:

1. Independent integer recomputation for a machine-checkable mathematical witness.
2. Immutable-source digest and exact-span recovery for provenance-bound evidence.
3. Fresh-subprocess positive/negative discrimination for an executable guard.
4. Later public revision comparison for a repository research conclusion.
5. Later numerical-envelope checking for an environmental decision.

Each contract was hashed before its historical later consequence was opened.
Positive evidence alone was insufficient: every contract also had to reject an
appropriate adversarial mutation. An artifact outside the supported affordance
meta-grammar produced abstention rather than an invented authority claim.

## Integrated significance

This joins the previous earned-intelligence differential and open method arena:

```text
unfamiliar retained objective + artifact
  -> infer what property matters
  -> construct a typed authority program
  -> precommit its contract
  -> reveal the later independent consequence
  -> execute positive and counterexample checks
  -> promote, revise, or abstain
  -> retain across restart
```

## Boundary

The artifacts are genuine outputs of AION's earlier North-Star operation and
the outcome rows were collected later. However, cohort selection, the semantic
affordance meta-grammar, authority adapters and adversarial mutations remain
engineered. The result demonstrates bounded cross-project verifier selection
and construction, not unrestricted authority invention, AGA or AGI.
