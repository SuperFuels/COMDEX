\section{Governed Cross-Channel Sales Follow-Up}
\label{sec:tessaris-governed-cross-channel-sales-followup}

\subsection{Objective and Delivered Outcome}

This increment extended the Tessaris Sales Agent from a governed telephone
caller into a governed cross-channel qualification system.  The purpose is to
retain the natural conversational quality demonstrated by the live Retell call
while preventing the underlying language model from inventing business goals,
prices, bookings, evidence routes or follow-up actions.

The resulting workflow is:

\begin{quote}
customer enquiry $\rightarrow$ exact-approved call attempt $\rightarrow$
exact-approved retry $\rightarrow$ exact-approved written follow-up
$\rightarrow$ short email conversation $\rightarrow$ human booking review.
\end{quote}

The same frozen qualification contract follows the customer across telephone,
email and future messaging channels.  Confirmed information is retained, and
each subsequent message requests only the information that remains missing.
No call, email, message, appointment, price or work commitment is performed
merely because a model generated suitable language.

\subsection{Business-Configurable Contact Policy}

The follow-up policy is provider-neutral and configurable independently for
each business.  It is not hard-coded to Home Fixed.  Agent Studio now exposes
the following settings:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item maximum telephone attempts, bounded to one, two or three;
  \item fallback channel: email, SMS, WhatsApp Business or human review;
  \item number of questions per written message, bounded to one, two or three;
  \item the approved business email or document destination;
  \item the required qualification fields and desired call outcome;
  \item permitted actions, opening style, close strategy and conversation guidance.
\end{itemize}

The persisted contact policy is represented by the following logical contract:

\begin{verbatim}
contact_sequence = {
  maximum_phone_attempts: 1..3,
  fallback_channel: email | sms | whatsapp | none,
  continue_same_goal_contract: true,
  questions_per_written_message: 1..3,
  automatic_send: false,
  booking_boundary:
    request_or_prepare_only_until_verified_calendar_and_communication_approval
}
\end{verbatim}

Changing any Agent Studio setting creates a new agent version, invalidates the
previous simulation credit and requires the new exact version to pass the full
governance examination before promotion.

\subsection{Frozen Goal Contract Across Channels}

Every prepared production call contains a normalised
\texttt{call\_goal\_contract} and a canonical SHA-256 contract hash.  The
contract identifies:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item the desired outcome;
  \item required information fields;
  \item permitted outcomes and prohibited commitments;
  \item the approved photo or document route;
  \item whether written contact confirmation is required; and
  \item the applicable conversation and closing policy.
\end{itemize}

The call draft, retry decision, written fallback and subsequent email turns all
retain the same goal-contract hash.  A model can choose natural phrasing within
the conversation, but it cannot silently expand the objective or add authority.
This separates conversational intelligence from commercial authority.

\subsection{Deterministic Email Continuation}

After the configured telephone-attempt limit, Tessaris prepares a deterministic
written message.  The language model does not invent the operational content of
this message.  The template:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item identifies the relevant customer and enquiry;
  \item states that a telephone attempt was made;
  \item asks only the next configured number of missing qualification questions;
  \item tells the customer that confirmed facts will not need to be repeated;
  \item states that a person reviews any appointment, price or work; and
  \item remains in \texttt{exact\_approval\_required} state.
\end{enumerate}

When a governed email or SMS reply is received, the written-continuation service
accepts only fields present in the frozen goal contract.  Confirmed values
accumulate across turns in hash-protected
\texttt{aion.sales.written\_goal\_continuation.v1} records.  The next draft asks
only the next one to three missing questions.  Previous confirmations are
carried forward, preventing the customer from being asked to repeat the same
information.

When every required field has been established, Tessaris stops generating
qualification questions and changes state to:

\begin{verbatim}
qualification_goals_complete_human_booking_review_required
\end{verbatim}

This state may lead to an internally prepared appointment, but it does not
confirm an external booking.  Calendar creation and customer notification retain
their own independent authority and exact-approval gates.

  \item maximum telephone attempts: two;

  \item automatic booking, pricing, payment and work confirmation: prohibited.

The earlier \texttt{info@homefixed.com} value is a dummy website address and is

but automated WhatsApp continuation is not claimed.  Selecting WhatsApp fails
closed until a governed WhatsApp Business connector can authenticate inbound
and outbound messages and attach them to the canonical Sales opportunity.

\subsection{Interfaces and Principal Components}

The principal implementation components are:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \texttt{sales\_revenue\_service.py}: Agent Studio configuration,
        contact-policy normalisation, call-attempt numbering and frozen call contracts;
  \item \texttt{sales\_completion\_service.py}: deterministic post-call messages,
        unanswered-call decisions and multi-turn written continuation;
  \item \texttt{sales\_revenue\_api.py}: versioned Agent Studio configuration and
        governed call preparation;
  \item \texttt{sales\_completion\_api.py}: contact-fallback and written-continuation
        interfaces; and
  \item \texttt{aion\_sales\_revenue\_workspace.js}: the non-technical Agent Studio
        controls presented in the Tessaris desktop application.
\end{itemize}

The principal new HTTP operations are logically:

\begin{verbatim}
POST /api/aion/sales-completion/{business}/opportunities/{opportunity}/
     contact-fallback

POST /api/aion/sales-completion/{business}/opportunities/{opportunity}/
     written-continuation
\end{verbatim}

Both operations require an authorised acting person.  Neither operation sends a
message or creates a customer-visible booking.

\subsection{Verification Evidence}

The Home Fixed version was reset, re-examined and promoted only after all
\textbf{11/11} governance simulations passed.  The broader focused verification
set comprised \textbf{22} distinct checks covering Sales configuration,
Retell goal binding, deterministic confirmation, unanswered-call retry,
cross-channel contract continuity, accumulated written replies and desktop
interface integration.

The verification established that:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item the first unanswered attempt produces only an approval-gated retry;
  \item the second unanswered attempt produces an approval-gated email draft;
  \item the call-goal hash remains unchanged across telephone and email;
  \item already confirmed fields are excluded from later questions;
  \item replies accumulate across multiple written turns;
  \item completed qualification creates a human booking-review requirement;
  \item no test sent an email, WhatsApp message or additional telephone call;
  \item no test created a customer-visible appointment or commercial commitment; and
  \item the rebuilt Tessaris desktop application started with a healthy local backend.
\end{itemize}

The previous installed application remains recoverable at

\subsection{Current Honest Boundary and Next Work}

The governed telephone-to-email workflow is implemented, but fully unattended
email conversation ingestion still depends on the configured Gmail connector
passing authenticated replies into the written-continuation operation.  Message
preparation, exact approval and Gmail-draft creation are separate stages; this
increment did not enable automatic sending.

WhatsApp continuation requires a verified WhatsApp Business adapter with
business-specific credentials, customer consent, delivery receipts, inbound
thread correlation and the same exact-approval boundary.  Until that adapter is
implemented, Tessaris correctly reports WhatsApp as unavailable rather than
silently substituting email, SMS or another provider.

The architecture is therefore ready for controlled real-customer evaluation:
Retell supplies the high-quality conversational voice, while Tessaris/AION
supplies the business objectives, memory, authority separation, evidence routes,
cross-channel continuity and independently reviewable consequences.
