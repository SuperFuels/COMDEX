# AION Owner-Valued Work Under Delayed Accountability

## Objective

This campaign moves the continual learner beyond monitoring and executor
acquisition. AION now produces three concrete work products in every generation:

1. a source-backed technical research and release-risk brief;
2. a reusable software guard for public-outcome integrity and authority breadth;
3. a data-based environmental monitoring decision.

The objective, success criteria and artifact hashes are committed before the
next public observation exists. None of the artifacts can award itself credit.

## Delayed work cycle

\[
\text{public evidence at }t
\rightarrow
\text{owner objective and success contract}
\rightarrow
\text{research + software + data artifacts}
\rightarrow
\text{immutable artifact commitments}
\rightarrow
\text{wait for public row }t+1
\rightarrow
\text{independent scoring}
\rightarrow
\text{executive receipt or rejection}.
\]

The research brief is checked against later public source-control and package
registry values. The software artifact runs in a fresh subprocess against the
later genuine row and must reject a hash-tampered version. The data decision is
scored against the later Open-Meteo observation or abstains if that authority is
unavailable.

Every generation composes an executive work system before the delayed outcome.
Only a later-confirmed generation is written into the executive method-outcome
ledger.

## Live status

The campaign is running continuously beside the prospective public-outcome
stream. Two generations have already been confirmed by later rows with 100%
research, software and data-decision success. Nine artifacts have been produced,
and the next generation is waiting. One confirmed generation predates executive
work-system attachment and is retained for provenance but does not count as an
executive receipt.

Promotion requires at least three delayed-confirmed executive receipts, at least
75% success in every work lane, zero owner interventions and zero live repository
writes. The service automatically creates the next generation after closing the
previous one.

## Authority boundary

These are useful, auditable operational artifacts, but their contracts and
source portfolio remain engineered and narrow. This does not establish
open-ended economic agency, arbitrary research competence or AGI. The next
expansion must use unfamiliar owner objectives, later CI or actual usage for
software changes, and materially different research/data authorities.
