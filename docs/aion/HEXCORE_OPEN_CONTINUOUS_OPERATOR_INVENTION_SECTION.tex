\section{Open Continuous Operator Invention and External Outcome Transfer}
\label{sec:open-continuous-operator-invention}

\subsection{Objective}

This development extends AION from stationary and piecewise-linear world-model
revision to governed construction of nonlinear, delayed, and latent-continuous
operators. The operational loop is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{unexplained residuals}
&\rightarrow \text{private Photon proposals}
\rightarrow \text{temporal validation}\\
&\rightarrow \text{active falsification}
\rightarrow \text{independent outcome}\\
&\rightarrow \text{promotion or abstention}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Photon generates traceable proposals, while withheld outcomes and the
Cognitive Authority Unit (CAU) remain the acceptance authority.

\subsection{Bounded Recursive Operator Grammar}

The synthesis library contains fourteen primitive or parameterized candidates:
an affine linear operator, a quadratic basis, four hyperbolic-tangent
saturation scales, four discrete delays, and four latent exponential-state
retention factors. Each candidate is represented as a Photon abstract syntax
tree with an explicit proposal-only authority label.

For a trace of length \(N\), observations are divided chronologically into
\(60\%\) training, \(20\%\) validation, and \(20\%\) sealed test segments.
Candidate features are fitted on the training segment only. Selection uses
\[
\mathcal{J}(h)
=
\frac{\operatorname{MSE}_{\mathrm{val}}(h)}
     {\operatorname{Var}(y_{\mathrm{val}})+\epsilon}
+\lambda_c C(h)+\lambda_p P(h),
\]
where \(C(h)\) measures operator complexity and \(P(h)\) penalizes additional
parameters. Fixed validation and test error ceilings force abstention when
even the best available operator fails to generalize.

\subsection{Active Counterexample Experiments}

For each generated trace, AION creates 32 novel intervention sequences and
selects the sequence and time point at which its two strongest explanations
disagree most. The outcome is then obtained from the withheld environment, not
from either candidate. The champion is retained only when the new observation
supports it. This converts model comparison into active falsification rather
than retrospective curve fitting.

\subsection{External Outcome Grounding}

Two software traces were executed in independent subprocesses: a
\texttt{math.tanh} saturation process and a three-step queue delay. AION
recovered the correct family and parameter in both cases, giving 100\%
software-transfer accuracy and \(R^2=1.0\) on the held-out segments.

AION also evaluated daily maximum temperature observations from the NOAA
Madrid Retiro station (identifier \texttt{SP000003195}) over 2023--2025. A
latent exponential-state operator achieved
\[
R^2_{\mathrm{test}}=0.8910923449
\]
on 200 chronologically held-out observations. This demonstrates predictive
transfer to an independently collected sensor series; it does not establish a
physical causal theory of weather.

Finally, AION evaluated a real Git and document-history trace. Its numerically
best quadratic candidate failed validation and obtained negative test
\(R^2\), so the system abstained. This records a useful negative result:
external data does not itself justify model acceptance.

\subsection{Continual Retention and Promotion}

Three source-disjoint continual generations passed backward-retention and
safety checks. The operator library, synthesis records, active
counterexamples, external provenance, and retained champion survived a full
restart with zero outcome replay. CAU promoted
\begin{quote}
\texttt{procedure\_open\_operator\_invention\_bdcb74fa8fc0}.
\end{quote}
The parent dependency remains
\texttt{procedure\_outcome\_world\_revision\_0ec1768712f1}.

\subsection{Capability and Claim Boundary}

Within the tested grammar, AION can detect structured residual error, construct
competing operator explanations, choose a novel discriminating observation,
accept an operator only after held-out prediction, and retain the verified
result. Scalar interfaces, primitive operators, finite parameter grids,
synthetic generators, and development-authored software experiments remain
engineered. NOAA and Git records provide external grounding but not an
externally administered benchmark. The result therefore demonstrates bounded
governed model-family construction, not unrestricted scientific discovery or
AGI.

\subsection{Next Frontier}

The next escalation is compositional multivariate operator discovery:
recursively combine nonlinear, delayed, and latent primitives; infer interacting
variables and multiple latent states; support irregular continuous time; and
select real measurements by expected information value and cost. Promotion
must require source-disjoint operational traces, calibrated uncertainty,
complexity control, safe abstention, restart retention, and CAU authorization.
