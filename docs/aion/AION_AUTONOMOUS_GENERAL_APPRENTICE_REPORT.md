# AION Autonomous General Apprentice Kernel

## Strategic change

The programme no longer treats a sequence of internally authored phases as the
path to general intelligence. The promoted
`procedure_autonomous_general_apprentice_kernel_v1` establishes one persistent
mission whose curriculum is derived from evidence gaps and later consequences.

The immutable mission is:

> Become an autonomous general apprentice that can learn unfamiliar subjects,
> act against real outcome authorities, transfer methods across domains, invent
> missing representations and tools, retain competence and compound
> improvement.

## Canonical loop

```text
broad immutable mission
  -> index verified successes, failures and unresolved evidence
  -> measure the ten agreed general-apprenticeship gates
  -> select the weakest executable evidence gap
  -> retrieve cross-domain method candidates
  -> invent a specialist and outcome contract
  -> commit a prediction or action before consequence
  -> bind only later independently revealed evidence
  -> diagnose, retain and test transfer
  -> update the capability map
  -> generate the next curriculum task
```

Subjects and benchmark tasks are not stored in the kernel. The only fixed list
is the owner-approved definition of the evidence required for general
apprenticeship: open learning, transfer, independent consequences, autonomous
curriculum, invention, continual retention, language cognition, planning,
physical/social grounding and compounding improvement.

Negative outcomes remain criticism and calibration evidence rather than being
silently discarded. The kernel's own promotion artifact is excluded from its
experience index, preventing recursive self-certification.

Method cards are extracted from CAU-promoted procedure steps. A method becomes
a transfer candidate only when independently retained artifacts associate it
with more than one capability family. The current strongest live candidate is
selection of experiments by information gained per unit cost, recovered from
both general schema revision and tool/program induction.

## Curriculum invention

The first task was generated without a supplied subject, benchmark case,
workflow, answer or task identifier. The kernel identified autonomous
curriculum as the least-supported evidence gate, selected the live Arena v15
public-outcome ledger, and invented this contract:

- accept an unfamiliar objective and available resources;
- use verified prior methods only as proposals;
- commit a prediction before the next public consequence;
- bind the independently revealed outcome;
- diagnose the discrepancy;
- require source-disjoint transfer and restart retention;
- reject objective mutation and unsafe action.

The task contains zero human-supplied task fields and ten derived fields. Its
prediction was committed after ledger cycle 5:

```text
predicted changing sources: node, open_meteo_madrid
ledger cursor: 5
commitment: b0f0195ea5b552311aa63f667e56d71c3cd7b46cb3419349f6f40d3250f29f18
```

Cycles 1--5 cannot satisfy this task. The service will bind only the first row
revealed after the committed cursor. Tests prove that pre-commit evidence is
rejected and that a later row retains its outcome hash and commitment.

## Runtime integration

`aion_general_apprentice_service.py` is active beside the canonical cognitive
runtime and real-outcome learner. It periodically:

1. rebuilds the verified experience index;
2. preserves the immutable owner mission;
3. waits for the next authorized delayed consequence;
4. scores the precommitted prediction;
5. closes the learning generation; and
6. creates the next weakest-gap curriculum task.

The service is also registered with AION Heartbeat for future supervised
startup and restart. Missing authorities fail closed into an
`invent_or_acquire_outcome_authority` contract; they cannot be replaced by an
internal score.

## What is and is not promoted

The persistent control plane is promoted. The ten long-term competence gates
are not marked complete. Existing evidence remains mostly internal and
development-authored; the live task is committed but awaits a genuinely later
public consequence; multi-month retention, broad social grounding and repeated
cross-domain mastery are still open.

This distinction is essential. AION now has a running mechanism capable of
driving the agreed path. It has not yet accumulated the duration and breadth
required to claim autonomous general apprenticeship, generally capable
self-improvement or AGI.

## Next operational milestones

The runtime should now be judged by one compounding campaign rather than phase
count:

1. bind the pending public consequence and revise its prediction policy;
2. transfer the learned investigation method into a different real authority;
3. acquire an outcome adapter when no current authority can test the weakest
   gap;
4. complete multiple generations with positive forward transfer and protected
   backward retention;
5. add unfamiliar owner missions without adding subject-specific code to the
   kernel; and
6. continue for hundreds and then thousands of tasks over elapsed time.

The evidence of progress will be declining human scaffolding, rising transfer,
lower investigation cost, stable protected competence and successful outcomes
under authorities AION does not control.
