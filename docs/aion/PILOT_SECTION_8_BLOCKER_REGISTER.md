# Pilot Section 8 Blocker Register

Status: Pilot Fabric 0.46.0 — 30 August 2026

The locally enforceable Games and Learning foundation is complete. The following items
cannot be promoted to consumer or universal field claims without external evidence.

## Gaming provider and hardware dependencies

- **GeForce NOW authenticated telemetry:** NVIDIA must provide or authorize a telemetry
  adapter that can attest title identity, stream readiness and playing state. Until then,
  Pilot can prepare and observe the public surface but cannot universally prove playback.
- **Provider deep links and sign-in:** game-specific launch routes, entitlement and device
  sign-in behaviour require NVIDIA documentation, account authorization and regional
  qualification. Pilot never automates CAPTCHA, terms, MFA or credential entry.
- **Controller matrix:** LG Mobile Gamepad, Apple, Android, Bluetooth and USB controllers
  require real-device field qualification across supported television/browser versions.
- **Saved-game continuation:** the local persona-private contract is implemented; exact
  resumption still depends on provider content identifiers and authorized deep links.

## Learning review and platform dependencies

- **Independent pedagogy review:** a qualified primary-language/education specialist must
  review progression, feedback, age bands and adaptive thresholds before consumer claims.
- **Independent child-safety and privacy review:** retention, guardian controls and shared
  household behaviour require jurisdiction-appropriate assessment before distribution.
- **Accessibility field review:** screen-reader, colour, focus, motor, hearing and cognitive
  accessibility must be tested on target LG, Samsung, Sony/Google TV and phone platforms.
- **Pronunciation field calibration:** the local scorer must be calibrated with consented,
  representative child accents and noisy-room conditions without retaining child audio.
- **Natural local voice licensing:** production voice packs require distribution rights and
  real-device performance qualification.

These are blockers to external claims, not permission to weaken the evidence boundaries.
