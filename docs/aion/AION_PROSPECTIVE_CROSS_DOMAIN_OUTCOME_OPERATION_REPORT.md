# AION Prospective Cross-Domain Outcome Operation

## Purpose

This campaign removes a weakness in the earlier long-duration evidence loop. A
plan to query a source is not itself a prediction. The new campaign commits a
falsifiable forecast before it invokes any public authority, then scores that
forecast from the returned consequence without rewriting the commitment.

## Live authority families

The prospective portfolio currently contains three independently controlled
families:

1. Public source control: CPython and Node Git heads.
2. Public package registry: the published NumPy release.
3. Public environmental sensor/data: Madrid temperature and wind from
   Open-Meteo.

Repository and package forecasts predict revision stability. Environmental
forecasts use a bounded persistence interval derived only from the preceding
observation. The first observation establishes a baseline and cannot count as a
correct forecast.

## Hard contract

Each cycle performs:

\[
\text{forecast}
\rightarrow
\text{durable precommitment}
\rightarrow
\text{public query}
\rightarrow
\text{independent outcome}
\rightarrow
\text{score or abstain}
\rightarrow
\text{world-change/acquisition attribution}
\rightarrow
\text{retain and repeat}.
\]

Promotion requires at least six cycles, three authority families, twelve scored
forecasts, at least 75% accuracy, zero world events routed to self-repair, zero
unsafe writes, and at least twenty minutes of elapsed operation. Rapid repeated
queries therefore cannot satisfy the gate.

An unreachable authority is recorded as an acquisition failure and its forecast
is not scored. External revision does not trigger self-repair. Naturally
occurring internal failures continue through the separate consequence-confirmed
repair watcher, where repair credit requires a later curriculum-owned row.

## Initial live status

The campaign has started and is intentionally not yet promoted. The first live
cycles reached all three authority families, created durable precommitments and
began scoring subsequent forecasts with zero owner interventions. The
long-running service continues the campaign every five minutes until both the
cycle and wall-clock gates are satisfied.

## Claim boundary

The public sources own the observations, but the source portfolio and forecast
forms remain engineered. This is a prospective cross-domain public-outcome
operation, not autonomous mastery of the underlying domains and not AGI.
