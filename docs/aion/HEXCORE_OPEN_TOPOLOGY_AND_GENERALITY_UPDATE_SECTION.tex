\section{Open-Topology Event Induction and Generality Programme Update}
\label{sec:open-topology-generality}

\subsection{Purpose}

The post-Phase-63 programme removes benchmark-supplied representations before
attempting wider autonomy.  The principal new experiment asks whether AION can
infer entities, state dimensions, temporal persistence, hidden operating modes
and causal topology from unfamiliar natural event logs without receiving a
semantic fact vocabulary or a fixed state-vector size.

\subsection{Open-topology induction architecture}

The learner receives observation and intervention episodes expressed through
multiple natural sentence forms.  It performs:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item anonymous entity, action, dimension and value discovery;
    \item state-space cardinality inference from observed use;
    \item comparison of immediate and settled observations to distinguish
          persistent from transient properties;
    \item action-effect and cross-dimension relation induction;
    \item latent-regime cardinality inference from predictive signatures;
    \item topology classification into independent, coupled or fan-out forms;
    \item compilation into a traceable Photon intermediate representation;
    \item explicit model criticism and abstention when stable latent structure
          does not explain the observations.
\end{enumerate}

The Photon representation uses the transition operator
\[
    \text{state} + \text{intervention}
    \;\Rightarrow\;
    \text{predicted state change}.
\]
It remains proposal-only.  Held-out prediction, HexCore verification and the
Cognitive Authority Unification layer remain authoritative.

Two additional local open backbones were evaluated as frozen proposal modules.
Every proposal was executed or checked by HexCore, and disabling the model left
the verified symbolic path operational.

was a useful low-cost challenger but did not pass both gates.

\subsection{Human-judgment authority}

Commonsense, social and creative work cannot be promoted by a simulator that
assumes one synthetic correct answer.  A frozen protocol was therefore created
with blind A/B response ordering, hidden candidate identity, five rating
dimensions, unacceptable-response flags, signed independence declarations,
cryptographic packet checksums and a minimum of three complete independent
raters.  Promotion authority is explicitly assigned to the independent panel
plus CAU.

The protocol is complete, but the capability gate is closed because no
independently authored sealed cohort or human ratings have yet been supplied.
The development process is not allowed to certify its own independence.

\subsection{Programme position}

The following internal tracks now possess bounded promoted evidence:
raw representation invention, real-outcome grounding, autonomous curriculum,
a 2,040-task procedural continual arena, sandboxed schema/tool/operator
invention, neural proposal consolidation and restart-persistent real-file
projects.  The substrate comparison has produced useful rejections rather than
a replacement champion.

The genuinely open gates are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item free-form and continuous representation induction outside the
          engineered natural-log grammar;
    \item human-authored social, commonsense and creative evaluation;
    \item multi-hour/day live projects with external services and approval
          points;
    \item post-Phase-55 neural consolidation with broad forgetting audits;
    \item independently administered, contamination-controlled comparison
          against models, agents, causal learners, planners and humans.
\end{itemize}

The next internal intelligence loop should connect invented open-topology state
to live outcomes:
\[
\text{unfamiliar events}
\rightarrow
\text{invented model}
\rightarrow
\text{prediction/action}
\rightarrow
\text{independent outcome}
\rightarrow
\text{failure attribution}
\rightarrow
\text{private challenger}
\rightarrow
\text{sealed retention test}
\rightarrow
\text{CAU promotion or rollback}.
\]
