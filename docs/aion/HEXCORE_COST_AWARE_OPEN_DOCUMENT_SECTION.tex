\section{Phase 39: Cost-Aware Open-Document Reasoning}

Phase 39 gives HexCore a governed open-document workspace.  From a broad
objective, AION identifies the active policy, derives the information needed
for a decision, and chooses among verified memory, source retrieval,
deterministic tools, user clarification, safe probing and abstention.

For information action \(a\), the operational score is
\[
U(a)=\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{IG}\mid a]-0.25C(a)-R(a),
\]
where \(C(a)\) is declared cost and \(R(a)\) is epistemic or operational
risk.  Accepted evidence retains source identity, revision, authority,
verification status, locator and provenance.  Consequently, an anonymous
high-revision claim cannot supersede an authoritative source merely because
its revision number is larger.

The sealed cohort contained 40 projects across five domains.  Mean and
weakest-domain decision success were both 100\%, information-action routing
accuracy was 100\%, and mean action cost fell from an exhaustive 25.50 to
4.85, an 80.98\% reduction.  Provenance was complete, no unverified rumor was
accepted, all 40 verified outcomes were committed, and restart required zero
relearning.  CAU promoted
\texttt{procedure\_cost\_aware\_open\_docs\_09c260698e08}.

The production ingestion path also extracted and indexed a six-page Tessaris
PDF and the SQM/SQI Markdown overview, yielding 119 chunks and five traceable
citations.  This is an extraction and citation result, not a scored claim of
complete natural-document comprehension.  The documents, need classes,
utilities, tools and oracle in the sealed benchmark remain engineered.
