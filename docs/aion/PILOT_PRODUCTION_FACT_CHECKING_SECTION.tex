\section{Production Live Fact-Checking and Evidence Follow-Through}
\label{sec:pilot-production-fact-checking}

Pilot Fabric 0.35 extends the live companion from a one-shot model response into
a durable, inspectable evidence system.  The user may ask ``Pilot, fact check
that'' immediately after a statement.  Pilot bounds the recent local transcript
or owner-captured subtitle, decomposes it into checkable claims, retrieves public
evidence through the provider-independent research router, validates the returned
structure, and stores a canonical evidence record.  Television playback remains
visible; the short verdict is spoken and the complete evidence is presented on
the private phone.

\subsection{Evidence Record}

The persisted object uses schema \texttt{pilot.live-news.v2} and contains:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the captured statement and its provenance;
  \item individual claims with verdict, confidence, explanation, date scope and
        bounded source references;
  \item sanitized HTTPS evidence sources;
  \item contextual notes and an owner-correction timeline;
  \item provider route, research latency and evidence sufficiency;
  \item contradiction analysis and a canonical SHA-derived evidence hash; and
  \item explicit privacy and presentation constraints.
\end{itemize}
Only source indexes that survive URL and scheme validation may support a claim.
Private-network URLs and non-HTTPS evidence are discarded.  A search hand-off is
never upgraded to a verdict: its state remains \emph{Unverifiable} with zero
confidence.

\subsection{Source Quality Outside the Model}

Source quality is assigned by deterministic host and provenance rules rather
than by the language model.  Government or intergovernmental primary sources,
academic institutions and established editorial publishers receive distinct
classes, scores and human-readable rationales.  Other organizations remain
visible but carry a lower default quality signal.  The score is an evidence-
handling priority and not an assertion that an institution is infallible.  The
private controller reports source count, mean source quality, contradiction
count and measured end-to-end latency.

\subsection{Contradictions and Disagreement}

Pilot normalizes claim text and compares retained verdicts.  Conflicting
verdicts for the same normalized claim are stored as contradictions, while
claims explicitly labelled \emph{Disputed} retain their supporting source
indexes.  This analysis is outside the generative summary.  Consequently a
model cannot create the appearance of disagreement merely by writing that two
sources differ.

\subsection{Deterministic Follow-Up Dialogue}

The commands ``show me the evidence'', ``who disagrees?'' and ``explain the
difference'' operate on the frozen evidence object.  They do not run a new
unbounded model prompt.  Pilot ranks retained sources by the outside-model
quality signal, identifies established disputed claims or conflicting verdicts,
and compares date scopes, definitions and contextual notes.  Where the record
does not establish a difference or an opposing source, Pilot states that limit
explicitly.  Every follow-up contains the parent fact-check identifier, a new
content hash and the flags \texttt{derived\_without\_model} and
\texttt{playback\_preserved}.

\subsection{Privacy, Audit and Failure Behaviour}

Raw microphone audio, original screen pixels and raw provider responses are not
retained.  The bounded claim, structured evidence and hashes are sufficient for
correction, audit and later sharing without preserving protected programme
content.  Corrections never overwrite history; they appear in chronological
order between statement capture and evidence research.  If evidence acquisition
is absent, unsafe or insufficient, Pilot returns an honest limitation and does
not synthesize a confident verdict.

\subsection{Capability Boundary}

This stage provides production-shaped evidence handling but does not claim a
system overlay above protected third-party television applications.  LG reserves
that authority for system software.  Pilot therefore speaks a concise result,
keeps the programme visible, and uses the persona-bound private controller for
the detailed evidence surface.  A future signed native television application
may provide compact overlays where the platform explicitly grants that scope.
