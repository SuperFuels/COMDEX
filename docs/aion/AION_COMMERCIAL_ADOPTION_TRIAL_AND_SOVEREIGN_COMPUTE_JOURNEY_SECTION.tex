\section{AION Commercial Adoption, Department Trials and Sovereign Compute Journey}

\subsection{Decided product direction}

AION begins as a downloadable, customer-owned second brain. The customer does not have to buy a
special computer, rack or managed cloud contract before discovering whether the system is useful.
They install the brain on an existing Mac, personal computer, office server, private virtual
server or customer-controlled cloud environment. From the first day, the customer's identity,
Business Map, memories, policies, workflows, permissions, evidence, outcomes and receipts belong
to that customer.

The initial installation may use a small included local model such as Gemma, customer-supplied
model API credentials, a private model endpoint, rented customer-owned compute, or a governed
combination of those routes. AION remains the memory, policy, learning and execution authority;
models remain replaceable computational tools.

The commercial proposition is:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Download the sovereign brain. Prove its value using the intelligence available to you.
Employ it permanently in-house when the evidence justifies dedicated infrastructure.}
\end{quote}

The downloadable brain is therefore Level~1, not Level~2. Dedicated hardware is an optional
consequence of proven demand rather than a prerequisite for adoption.

\subsection{The three-level deployment journey}

\subsubsection{Level 1: download and prove AION}

At Level~1 the customer runs AION on infrastructure that already exists. The core brain is useful
without a Tessaris-hosted model and must not be reduced to a cloud-service client. During setup the
customer may:

\begin{itemize}
  \item install the qualified small local model;
  \item bind OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic or another provider through a private vault reference;
  \item connect a local or customer-owned OpenAI-compatible endpoint;
  \item connect rented GPU capacity held in the customer's own account; or
  \item permit AION to select between these routes under declared privacy, capability and cost
        limits.
\end{itemize}

Level~1 is also the measurement period. AION records verified work completed, plausible time
saved, decisions supported, errors prevented, opportunities influenced, provider expenditure,
local-versus-remote routing and task-class performance. Estimates remain visibly distinct from
verified financial outcomes. These records create a defensible case for retaining a department
operator or acquiring dedicated compute.

\subsubsection{Level 2: move the proven brain into dedicated infrastructure}

When continuing rented intelligence becomes operationally or economically inferior, AION may
recommend an AION Business Box, tower workstation, rack-mounted GPU server, multi-GPU rack or
customer-owned private-cloud deployment. This is not a new employee or a fresh brain. Governed
migration carries the same mother identity, Business Map, business containers, memory, department
agents, workflows, harnesses, permissions, outcome evidence, model performance, cost history and
execution receipts.

At Level~1 the brain is already owned by the customer, although some advanced thinking may be
rented. At Level~2 both the brain and most routine computational intelligence operate on
customer-owned capacity. External frontier models remain optional specialist consultants.

The hardware recommendation must be generated from observed workload rather than an arbitrary
business-size label. It should explain capacity, concurrency, privacy, estimated electricity and
support costs, the proportion of work that can move locally and an evidence-bounded payback range.

\subsubsection{Level 3: expand the intelligence stack}

A growing customer adds capacity without replacing the brain. Expansion may add GPUs, specialist
models, storage, backup nodes, geographic replicas, regulated-data zones, department-specific
compute, high availability and disaster recovery. AION treats all such infrastructure as a pool
of replaceable cognitive compute governed by the same business authority.

Multiple logical agents do not imply one permanently loaded model per agent. A job queue,
priority policy and warm-model scheduler allow many departmental agents to share a smaller number
of model runtimes. Concurrency, latency and service commitments determine when more capacity is
needed.

\subsection{Credential and ownership separation}

Four credential classes must remain technically and visibly separate:

\begin{description}
  \item[AION identity and licence.] Activates signed software capabilities, update channels,
        support and commercial entitlements.
  \item[Model-provider credential.] Gives the customer access to an optional external model and
        normally leaves model usage billed by that provider.
  \item[Private compute binding.] Connects AION to a local or customer-owned model endpoint.
  \item[Business connector credential.] Authorises a bounded connection to email, calendar, CRM,
        finance or another business system.
\end{description}

An AION licence must not give Tessaris access to the customer's Business Map, prompts, documents,
private memory or provider secrets. Ending a subscription may stop paid automation or support,
but it must never prevent the customer from reading, correcting, exporting, restoring or migrating
their brain and records.

\subsection{Commercial principle}

The decided commercial principle is:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{The brain is free. Operating the business is paid. Intelligence costs remain transparent.}
\end{quote}

There are three separate economic layers:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item AION software, operating capabilities, governance and support paid to Tessaris;
  \item optional external model consumption paid through the customer's provider account or an
        explicitly selected Tessaris-managed allowance; and
  \item local or private compute paid through hardware, electricity or the customer's cloud
        account.
\end{enumerate}

Tessaris must not levy a hidden token charge on inference performed by a model and hardware the
customer owns. Revenue is earned from valuable operating capability: continuous agency,
department functions, integrations, permissions, governance, collaboration, recovery, managed
infrastructure and support.

\subsection{A genuinely useful free foundation}

\textbf{AION Free} must remain useful indefinitely. It should include:

\begin{itemize}
  \item the downloadable sovereign brain and one owner identity;
  \item Personal Pilot and one business workspace;
  \item a basic Business Map and customer-controlled memory;
  \item the qualified small local model and customer-supplied model routes;
  \item document and local business-knowledge search;
  \item tasks, reminders, lists and Pilot conversation;
  \item manual workflows and a small number of connectors;
  \item a bounded allowance of manually approved verified actions;
  \item free Pilot-to-Pilot and agent-to-agent communication; and
  \item complete data export, recovery and model-provider replacement.
\end{itemize}

Self-employed people and microbusinesses may remain on the free brain, local Gemma-class inference
and occasional customer-funded API calls for a long time. This is an acceptable outcome. They
increase adoption and network utility, retain trust in the sovereign proposition and can activate
operational capability when genuine demand appears.

Agent-to-agent communication remains free because it is the natural distribution loop. External
carrier costs---including SMS, commercial WhatsApp delivery, telephone calls, managed email or
premium identity verification---remain separate and transparent.

\subsection{Full department trial}

The primary conversion mechanism is not a crippled demonstration. A new customer chooses one
complete department operator to experience for a bounded trial:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Install AION free. Choose one AI department to operate free for 30 days. Keep it only if
it proves its value.}
\end{quote}

Candidate trials include Sales, Marketing, Customer Support, Finance Monitoring, Operations, or
Website and Lead Response. A Sales trial may provide 24-hour website enquiry monitoring, email
lead detection, policy-bounded qualification and acknowledgement, CRM preparation, follow-up
tracking, appointment proposals, opportunity monitoring, escalation and a daily sales briefing.
Consequential communications and actions remain within the configured approval and automation
authority.

The trial should end at the first declared boundary, such as 30 days, a stated number of verified
business actions, or a stated managed-intelligence expenditure ceiling. The illustrative launch
hypothesis is 30 days, 1,000 verified actions or EUR~50 of Tessaris-managed intelligence. These
figures require commercial testing and must not become hidden limits.

Local inference and customer-supplied provider usage do not consume Tessaris-managed intelligence
credits. If a trial allowance is exhausted, AION falls back safely rather than abandoning the
business: it continues receiving enquiries, deterministic routing, agent-to-agent communication
and review preparation while pausing paid model processing and unapproved autonomous external
actions.

\subsection{Transparent trial conversion and billing}

A payment method may be collected when the customer deliberately activates a full department
trial. Automatic conversion is acceptable only after explicit agreement to the exact plan, price,
renewal date, included capacity and overage policy. Cancellation must be immediately available;
there must be no retrospective or surprise charge.

A proposed notification sequence is:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Day~1: the selected department activates and its authority is displayed;
  \item Day~7: the first evidence-backed value report is delivered;
  \item Day~14: operating activity and intelligence cost are summarised;
  \item Day~21: continuation is recommended only when supported by observed value;
  \item Day~27: the exact renewal terms are presented again; and
  \item Day~30: the customer continues the paid operator or returns to free review-only operation.
\end{itemize}

Trial conversion must be implemented as a governed commercial state machine with a signed consent
receipt, immutable price/version reference, renewal reminders, cancellation receipt, failed-payment
grace period and idempotent billing operation. Card data belongs with a certified payment provider;
AION stores only an opaque customer/payment-method reference and necessary billing status.

\subsection{What becomes chargeable}

Customers begin paying when AION assumes continuing operational responsibility. Chargeable value
includes continuous monitoring, automatic lead handling, scheduled work, follow-up responsibility,
department agents, governed external execution, advanced connectors, team permissions, approval
chains, higher operating volume, managed intelligence, private-cloud or rack management, recovery
and support.

The primary unit is an \emph{active operating capability with included capacity}, not a raw token.
A customer should not hesitate to ask Pilot for help because every internal thought, retry or
deterministic check appears as another charge. Plans include a generous declared allowance;
overage is capped, visible and separately approved.

A verified business action may include sending an approved email, updating a CRM record,
scheduling an event, processing an enquiry, preparing an invoice proposal, completing a monitored
follow-up, recording a business briefing or operating a scheduled workflow. Only normalized
receipts and privacy-safe metering fields are required for commercial counting.

\subsection{Capability-based expansion}

The natural upgrade boundary occurs when the customer asks AION to take responsibility:

\begin{itemize}
  \item drafting one reply can remain free; monitoring the inbox and preparing every reply is an
        operator capability;
  \item analysing selected leads can remain free; continuously qualifying incoming leads is a
        Sales Operator capability;
  \item showing overdue invoices can remain free; monitoring, preparing reminders and tracking
        responses is a Finance Monitor capability;
  \item creating a marketing plan can remain free; operating and measuring the campaign is a
        Marketing Operator capability; and
  \item creating a manual workflow can remain free; scheduling, supervising and recovering it is
        an operational AION Flow capability.
\end{itemize}

Department packs may include Sales Operator, Marketing Operator, Finance Monitor, Customer
Support, Project Operations, People and HR, Procurement, Website and Ecommerce, Advanced AION
Flow, and Governance and Audit. They extend one brain rather than creating disconnected products.

\subsection{Initial packaging hypotheses}

The following ranges are commercial hypotheses for controlled market testing, not final public
prices:

\begin{description}
  \item[AION Free.] Sovereign brain, local or bring-your-own-key intelligence, one owner, one
        personal and one business workspace, manual workflows, free agent-to-agent communication
        and a small verified-action allowance.
  \item[AION Operator.] Approximately EUR~29--59 per month for sole traders and microbusinesses,
        including continuous personal/business assistance, email and calendar support, website
        monitoring, tasks, follow-ups, one operator and a generous action allowance.
  \item[AION Boardroom.] Approximately EUR~99--299 per month according to capability and capacity,
        including multiple department agents, dashboards, business connectors, users, approvals
        and greater background operation.
  \item[Additional operators.] Approximately EUR~30--100 per active department each month,
        depending on responsibility, connector cost and included capacity.
  \item[AION Growth.] Capability-, usage- and support-based pricing for multi-entity, multi-location,
        delegated, regulated or higher-concurrency organisations.
  \item[AION Sovereign.] Contracted hardware, migration, private deployment, high availability,
        security, service commitment and managed-support pricing.
\end{description}

Pricing must follow requirements and operating value rather than arbitrary employee-count labels.
Per-seat charges may apply where identity, permission, support and collaboration costs genuinely
increase, but the product must not collapse into a conventional per-seat chatbot subscription.

\subsection{Private value meter and evidence-based recommendations}

AION maintains a customer-visible value ledger covering verified work, plausible human time saved,
leads processed, follow-ups completed, costs identified, provider expenditure, subscription cost,
local-versus-premium routing and suggested capability or compute changes. ``Revenue influenced''
must never be presented as ``revenue generated'' without adequate causal evidence.

Renewal and upgrade prompts use this ledger. A useful renewal statement describes the verified
actions, monitored enquiries, estimated time range, subscription cost and external intelligence
cost. A useful expansion recommendation explains observed unmet work, such as marketing follow-up
created by the active Sales Operator. A useful rack recommendation compares recent rented compute
with a measured local-workload profile and presents assumptions, uncertainty and payback range.

Outcome-percentage pricing is deferred. Attribution disputes make a percentage of alleged revenue
unsuitable for the initial commercial model. AION initially charges for operating capability,
included capacity, managed infrastructure and support. Outcome-based contracts may be introduced
later only for narrowly measurable and mutually agreed cases.

\subsection{Scalable commercial loop}

The resulting adoption and revenue loop is:

\begin{center}
Free sovereign brain $\rightarrow$ useful work $\rightarrow$ one complete department trial
$\rightarrow$ verified value $\rightarrow$ paid continuous operation $\rightarrow$ additional
departments and capacity $\rightarrow$ dedicated AION infrastructure.
\end{center}

Revenue therefore grows because AION is doing more valuable work, serving more operational
requirements and accepting more governed responsibility. It does not grow by holding the
customer's brain hostage or charging for local thoughts. This preserves the strategic distinction:
the intelligence relationship belongs to the business and AION, while every individual model,
provider and compute target remains replaceable.

\subsection{Implementation record}

The commercial design creates the following future build requirements:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item implement signed AION entitlement records that cannot authorize access to customer data;
  \item build the department-trial state machine and safe review-only fallback;
  \item integrate a certified payment provider using opaque payment references;
  \item define the normalized verified-action meter and privacy-safe commercial ledger;
  \item implement local, customer-funded and Tessaris-managed intelligence cost separation;
  \item build the private value dashboard with estimate and evidence labels;
  \item add department activation, renewal, cancellation and capacity controls;
  \item add evidence-based upgrade and rack-sizing recommendations;
  \item build governed whole-brain migration into dedicated hardware; and
  \item validate pricing, allowances, conversion language and willingness to pay through real
        customer trials before fixing the public tariff.
\end{enumerate}
