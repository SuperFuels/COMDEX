\section{Compositional Multivariate Scientific Discovery}
\label{sec:compositional-multivariate-discovery}

\subsection{Objective and Architecture}

AION now constructs compact continuous operators over multiple interacting
variables and irregular timestamps. The governed loop is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{multivariate observations}
&\rightarrow \text{recursive Photon compositions}\\
&\rightarrow \text{chronological validation and MDL criticism}\\
&\rightarrow \text{cost-aware active falsification}\\
&\rightarrow \text{external outcome}\\
&\rightarrow \text{retain, revise, or abstain}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Photon remains proposal-only. Withheld outcomes and the Cognitive Authority
Unit (CAU) retain acceptance and promotion authority.

\subsection{Irregular-Time Composition}

The bounded vocabulary includes raw, quadratic, saturating, continuously
delayed, continuous latent-state, interaction, and temporal-basis terms. The
engine evaluates compositions of up to three terms. It fits on the first 60\%
of a trace, selects on the next 20\%, and evaluates once on the final 20\%.
Candidate \(h\) is ranked by
\[
\mathcal{J}(h)
=
\frac{\operatorname{MSE}_{\mathrm{val}}(h)}
     {\operatorname{Var}(y_{\mathrm{val}})+\epsilon}
+\lambda_c C(h)+\lambda_p P(h).
\]
Continuous latent state is updated according to elapsed time:
\[
z_i =
\exp\!\left(-\frac{\Delta t_i}{\tau}\right)z_{i-1}
+
\left(1-\exp\!\left(-\frac{\Delta t_i}{\tau}\right)\right)x_i.
\]

\subsection{Cost-Aware Active Falsification}

For each sealed world AION constructs 48 novel multivariate interventions and
selects
\[
a^\star
=
\arg\max_a
\frac{
\left|\hat y_{\mathrm{champion}}(a)
-\hat y_{\mathrm{runner}}(a)\right|
}{
\operatorname{cost}(a)
}.
\]
The withheld environment then supplies the outcome. This reduced mean
measurement cost from \(1.50270\) to \(1.24485\), a relative reduction of
\(17.16\%\), while the selected champion won every falsification test.

\subsection{Verification and Claim Boundary}

Focused regression tested the parent operator-invention procedure and the new
multivariate procedure; both passed. The expensive complete historical replay
was intentionally deferred until several further stages have accumulated.

The primitive vocabulary, three-input interface, three-term limit, generated
worlds, cost formula, and evaluator remain engineered. Software was independently
executed but development-authored; NOAA is externally collected but not an
independently administered evaluation. The result establishes bounded
compositional discovery and cost-aware falsification, not unrestricted
scientific discovery or AGI.
