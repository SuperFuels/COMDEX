\section{Unified Mobile Learning, Games and Entertainment Continuity}
\label{sec:pilot-unified-mobile-experiences}

The unified Pilot phone application now presents AION Learning, governed cloud gaming and
private entertainment continuity as first-class Personal experiences.  The implementation reuses
the existing Fabric education, gaming, entertainment-personalisation and verified-execution
authorities.  It does not create a parallel curriculum, provider session store or viewing-history
database.

\subsection{Signed Private Boundary}

The mother grants two explicit mobile scopes: \texttt{experiences.read} and
\texttt{experiences.control}.  Every request is signed by the phone's non-exportable Ed25519 key,
bound to its certificate, lease and private person, and carries a bounded idempotency identifier.
Opening a game provider or executing an entertainment route additionally requires
\texttt{tv.control} and a live exclusive shared-screen session.  Consequently, possession of a
learning permission alone cannot control the television.

\subsection{AION Learning}

The Learning surface starts and continues the existing local fifty-question curriculum.  A
guardian or learner can select the learner slot, subject, age band and difficulty, answer on the
phone, repeat a prompt and advance after feedback.  The screen presents an original local visual,
question number, correct count, stars and streak.  Correct and incorrect results combine text,
symbols and colour.  The exposed session deliberately omits the hidden correct-answer index.

Spanish, mathematics and science exercises remain deterministic and usable without a paid model.
Open chat, advertising, purchases, external links and advertising profiles remain disabled for
the child surface.  Independent pedagogy and child-safety review remain required before consumer
claims are made.

\subsection{Governed Cloud Gaming}

The Games surface can request the allowlisted GeForce NOW origin on the active television and can
prepare continuation of the person's most recently verified saved game.  Gaming sessions are now
bound to a private person before storage; one person's latest game is not projected to another
person.  Controller advertisements, observed input compatibility, provider opening, search
results, stream readiness and playing are separate evidence states.

The phone therefore reports ``provider surface attempted'' after launch.  It reports playing only
after signed provider or device evidence confirms a selected title and active stream.  Provider
credentials remain owned by NVIDIA and are not retained by Pilot or projected to a device node.

\subsection{Verified Entertainment Continuation}

Entertainment continuation is intentionally split into three operations.  Pilot first prepares
the exact official route for the last verified title.  The person reviews and approves that route
privately.  Only then may the phone attempt to open it on the television.  Provider opening,
subscription entitlement and playback are shown independently; an opened application or web page
is never called playback.

The same surface exposes the person's private watchlist and remembered watched, liked, disliked or
avoided titles.  These records remain persona-bound unless the person explicitly chooses a
household-shared visibility in a separately governed flow.

\subsection{Interfaces and Verification}

The local mobile service publishes signed snapshot and control endpoints beneath
\texttt{/v1/personal/experiences}.  The responsive phone client renders dedicated Learning, Games
and Entertainment panels rather than mixing them into the remote control.  The implementation was
verified by focused authority, privacy, gaming and mobile-surface tests and by the optimized Next.js
production build.  No real provider login, game stream or television playback was changed during
automated verification.

Field qualification still requires a trusted phone, active shared-screen session, signed-in
provider account, compatible controller and direct observation of provider and playback states.
These external facts remain visible gates rather than simulated checkmarks.
