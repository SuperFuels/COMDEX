# AION Autonomous Capability and Cognitive-Research Executive

## Result

AION now maintains one evidence-driven portfolio spanning useful work,
deliberate capability practice and private cognitive research.  The executive
derives its objectives from the North Star, the progressive competency ledger,
live useful-work outcomes and measured runtime failures.  It does not require a
developer to name the next subject or benchmark on every generation.

Promoted procedure:

`procedure_autonomous_capability_research_executive_v1`

The first live generation contained ten objectives under a persistent resource
allocation:

| Lane | Allocation | Live objectives |
|---|---:|---:|
| Owner-useful work | 50% | 5 |
| Capability practice | 30% | 3 |
| Cognitive research | 20% | 2 |

The useful-work lane covers research, software, data decisions, machine-checked
mathematics and exact-provenance document work.  The practice lane selected
Data Analysis and Statistics, Distributed Systems and Mathematics from live
depth evidence; Data Analysis and Statistics was dispatched automatically to
the progressive competency service.  The cognitive-research lane selected a
real critic failure and the absence of Advanced/Expert depth as its two current
research problems.

Three queued objectives were published into the canonical goal engine.  The
canonical cognitive runtime now reloads the disk-backed queue on every wake, so
goals produced by another supervised process are visible without restart.

## First autonomous cognitive improvement

The executive discovered that the useful-work evaluator treated every changed
upstream revision as failed cognition.  A CPython research commitment was
therefore marked failed even though a later official consequence had correctly
revealed that the world changed.

A private tournament compared:

1. the frozen equality-only critic;
2. an unsafe always-accept critic; and
3. a change-aware, authority-bound critic.

The tournament used 39 real delayed research rows across CPython, Node and
NumPy release evidence, plus six hostile or unsupported consequences.  The
equality-only control scored 38/39.  The change-aware critic scored 39/39,
classified the changed row as `invalidate_and_reinvestigate`, and rejected all
6/6 hostile or unsupported rows.  The always-accept candidate was rejected
because it accepted all six invalid authorities.

Promoted critic:

`procedure_change_aware_outcome_critic_v1`

The prior evaluation remains in an immutable evaluation history.  The new
critic does not rewrite the observed revision; it changes the metacognitive
interpretation from ``my reasoning failed'' to ``the independent world changed,
invalidate the stale conclusion and investigate again.''

After promotion, the useful-work programme advanced from a 97.37% weakest
family to 100% across five families.  At the observed live checkpoint, 195/195
closed objectives had later-confirmed outcomes, with zero owner interventions
and zero unsafe writes.

## Resource-aware cognitive routing

The first canonical execution exposed a second natural failure: an autonomous
low-risk portfolio goal initialized the complete legacy neural stack repeatedly
and reached the Mac's approximately 20 GiB MPS memory ceiling.  A lightweight
specialist route was added for digest-identifiable portfolio goals.  It maps the
three lanes to their already-governed specialist services and never expands
ambient authority.

Regression tests prove that this route does not initialize Theta, the legacy
cognitive stack or the neural router.  Live resident memory was approximately
62 MiB after the change.  The runtime consumed all three self-generated goals
and left them pending independent specialist receipts instead of asserting
premature completion.

## Governance and persistence

Every generation stores:

- its North-Star commitment and SHA-256 digest;
- all considered objectives and visible priority components;
- the exact 50/30/20 lane distribution;
- dispatch status for every selected objective;
- the private critic tournament and frozen control;
- the selected competency subject;
- published canonical-runtime goal identifiers; and
- unsafe-action, live-write and objective-mutation counters.

The executive is integrated into the persistent mastery service and is visible
in the terminal development dashboard.  The real-outcome learner, mastery
service and canonical cognitive runtime are active concurrently.

The expanded earned-intelligence regression cohort passed 73/73 tests.  The
three live processes were then checked independently: each had a single active
process, no retained runtime error, and the lightweight canonical runtime
continued waking without neural-memory escalation.

## Boundary

This is a material reduction in developer-supplied next-step selection, not
unrestricted autonomous agency.  The three lane classes, safe specialist
dispatchers and typed critic candidates remain engineered.  Generated goals
remain subordinate to the immutable North Star and existing authority rules.
Practical and research claims still require independently scored consequences;
the executive cannot award competence or promote arbitrary live source code.
