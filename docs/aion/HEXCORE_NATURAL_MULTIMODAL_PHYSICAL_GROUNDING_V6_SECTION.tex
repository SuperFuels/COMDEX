\section{Natural Multimodal and Physical Grounding with Active Perception}
\label{sec:arena-v6-natural-multimodal}

Arena v6 removes a material limitation of the earlier multimodal path.  The
previous system could inspect file layouts, chart colours, and paired tables,
but much of its visual evidence came from filenames or engineered pixel
statistics.  Arena v6 instead presents actual image pixels to a replaceable
vision substrate, withholds the official description until the pixel-only
proposal is committed, and permits HexCore to request a focused second visual
inspection when a decision-critical property remains unresolved.

The internally promoted procedure is
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize
\texttt{procedure\_natural\_multimodal\_physical\_grounding\_v6\_6d4bc31527ae}}.
\end{center}


\subsection{Research question and authority boundary}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-question}

The experiment asks whether AION can interpret natural scientific pixels,
identify its own unresolved visual propositions, actively reinspect the image,
separate visual observation from source-reported measurement, detect
cross-modal contradiction, derive checkable quantities, and abstain when no
measurement authority exists.

The authority invariant is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{vision proposal} &\not\Rightarrow \text{accepted knowledge},\\
\text{pixel evidence} + \text{source provenance} + \text{verification}
&\Rightarrow \text{governed acceptance or criticism}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:arena-v6-authority}
\end{equation}

The vision model is a replaceable proposal substrate.  It cannot promote its
own output, rewrite source evidence, label a reported measurement as
independently verified, or bypass CAU.


\subsection{Public natural-scene cohort}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-cohort}

The source cohort contained three independently maintained public scientific
artifacts:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item an orbital NASA photograph of a large cyclonic cloud system, including
        the visible Earth limb and spacecraft structure;
  \item a NASA/JPL Mars surface mosaic containing a fractured rock with a
        circular drilled opening; and
  \item a USGS earthquake summary poster combining geographic maps, seismic
        annotations, legends, tables, explanatory text, and measured event
        properties.
\end{enumerate}

Each downloaded artifact was frozen by SHA-256.  The retained source manifest
records the official page, direct asset address, maintaining authority, local
digest, source-reported claims, pixel-only expectations, and claims that must
not appear before the official-source reveal.

The public source pages were:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \url{https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/hurricane-earl-the-astronaut-view-45583/};
  \item \url{https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/nasas-curiosity-takes-close-look-at-rock-that-got-stuck-on-drill/}; and
  \item \url{https://earthquake.usgs.gov/product/poster/usp000h2gd/us/1538164135254/poster.pdf}.
\end{itemize}

These are independently maintained public sources, not an independently
administered hidden evaluation.  Their possible presence in model pretraining
is not excluded.


\subsection{Active-perception controller}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-active-perception}

The full perception and evidence pipeline is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{opaque image bytes}
&\rightarrow \text{broad pixel-only proposal}\\
&\rightarrow \text{unresolved proposition detection}\\
&\rightarrow \text{targeted pixel-only reinspection}\\
&\rightarrow \text{affirmative-evidence filtering}\\
&\rightarrow \text{proposal commitment}\\
&\rightarrow \text{official-source reveal with provenance}\\
&\rightarrow \text{physical derivation and contradiction check}\\
&\rightarrow \text{CAU promotion, rejection, or abstention}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:arena-v6-pipeline}
\end{equation}

The initial general inspection recovered $58.33\%$ of the declared semantic
groups.  Rather than lower the acceptance threshold or expose a caption,
HexCore generated focused visual questions.  Examples included whether the
cloud field visibly formed an organised spiral, whether a circular opening was
present in the main rock, and whether the poster visibly contained both map and
tabular structures.

Targeted questions can leak answer vocabulary if scored naively.  Let $q$ be a
question returned inside the proposal, $a$ its yes/no/uncertain answer, and $e$
its alleged pixel evidence.  The scoring projection was therefore
\begin{equation}
\Pi(q,a,e)=
\begin{cases}
e, & a=\mathrm{yes},\\
\varnothing, & a\in\{\mathrm{no},\mathrm{uncertain}\},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:arena-v6-positive-evidence}
\end{equation}
and $q$ was always removed.  Thus the expected term appearing in AION's own
question could not satisfy the visual gate.


\subsection{Epistemic separation and delayed reveal}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-epistemic}

For each artifact, AION first committed
\begin{equation}
c_i=\operatorname{SHA256}
\bigl(\operatorname{canonical}(i,p_i,h_i)\bigr),
\label{eq:arena-v6-commitment}
\end{equation}
where $i$ is the opaque case identifier, $p_i$ is the combined pixel proposal,
and $h_i$ is the image digest.  Only after $c_i$ was stored were official
descriptions and numerical measurements attached.

Every revealed claim was labelled
\begin{center}
\texttt{REPORTED\_BY\_OFFICIAL\_SOURCE},
\end{center}
with source authority, URL, and content digest.  Visual corroboration did not
silently convert source-reported values into independently measured truth.


\subsection{Physical derivations}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-physical-derivations}

Three deterministic derivations tested whether the system could use the
provenance-bound measurements correctly:
\begin{align}
r_{\mathrm{eye}}
  &=\frac{28\ \mathrm{km}}{2}=14\ \mathrm{km},
  \label{eq:arena-v6-eye-radius}\\
W_{\mathrm{Mars}}
  &=13\ \mathrm{kg}\times 3.71\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}
    =48.23\ \mathrm{N},
  \label{eq:arena-v6-mars-weight}\\
v_{\mathrm{plate}}
  &=91\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}=9.1\ \mathrm{cm\,yr^{-1}}.
  \label{eq:arena-v6-plate-motion}
\end{align}
All three calculations were correct.  Their inputs nevertheless retained their
reported-source epistemic status.


\subsection{Cross-modal criticism and abstention}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-criticism}

After commitment, each case received an adversarial reported statement denying
a visible property: organised spiral cloud structure, a circular rock opening,
or the poster's map/table layout.  All three were labelled
\begin{center}
\texttt{DISPUTED\_BY\_PIXEL\_EVIDENCE}
\end{center}
and none entered knowledge memory.

An additional NASA locator map was presented without an external measurement
source, followed by a request for exact wind speed.  The system recorded
\begin{center}
{\footnotesize
\texttt{NO\_PIXEL\_OR\_EXTERNAL\_AUTHORITY\_FOR\_EXACT\_PHYSICAL\_MEASUREMENT}}
\end{center}
and abstained rather than converting scene recognition into numerical
authority.

\subsection{Persistence, registry integration, and claim boundary}
\label{subsec:arena-v6-boundary}

The cohort commitment, proposal commitments, gate record, procedure champion,
and denial of unsupported claims survived full runtime reconstruction with zero
relearning.  Arena v6 was added as the twelfth committed artifact in the AGI
Evidence Authority Registry.  The multimodal gate is now classified as a
bounded public natural-multimodal partial result, not external certification.

Arena v6 demonstrates active natural-image investigation, delayed
source-grounded fusion, cross-modal criticism, derived physical reasoning, and
safe abstention on the declared cohort.  It does not demonstrate native AION
vision training, broad image understanding, audio/video comprehension, spatial
action, physical actuation, independent hidden evaluation, or AGI.  The next
frontier is temporal multimodal action: learning state change from video/audio
or ordered visual evidence, selecting measurements, predicting delayed physical
consequences, and repairing action when observations diverge.
