\section{Activation-Sparse Expert-Column Gate}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-activation-sparse-20260911}

The dominant physical problem remains expert-weight traffic.  A changed-model
microgate therefore tested whether low-magnitude coordinates in the 2,880-value
expert input could be zeroed, allowing a future column-sparse kernel to avoid the
corresponding columns of the gate and up projections.  The unrestricted route,
four original experts, original gates, packed MXFP4 weights, biases, and residual
were preserved.  Only the expert input was sparsified.  The down projection was
correctly charged as dense, so the projected total expert-weight fraction was
$(1+2r)/3$ for retained coordinate fraction $r$.

Sixteen frozen real layer/position captures were evaluated.  The predeclared
advance gate required at least 1.5$\times$ projected traffic reduction in every
capture, p95 layer-output relative $L_2$ error no greater than 0.02, and p95
expert-contribution error no greater than 0.15.  No candidate passed.

Retaining 75\% of input absolute mass kept approximately 43.77\% of coordinates
at median and projected 1.5997$\times$ median traffic reduction (1.5865$\times$
worst case), but p95 output and contribution errors were 0.0991 and 0.3203.
Retaining 90\% reduced the errors to 0.0401 and 0.1349, but traffic reduction
fell to 1.3187$\times$ median and 1.3075$\times$ worst case.  Retaining 95\%
reached 0.0227 output error and 0.0788 contribution error for only
1.2084$\times$ median traffic reduction.  At 99\%, numerical error passed
comfortably (0.00786/0.02377) but the projected gain was only 1.0842$\times$.

Decision: \texttt{STOP\_ACTIVATION\_SPARSE\_COLUMNS}.  The input energy is too
widely distributed: enough coordinates can be removed for a meaningful traffic
gain only by exceeding the numerical gate, while numerically safe sparsity cannot
repay the indexing and irregular-kernel cost.  No sparse kernel or compact column
store will be built from this result, and no token-speed or semantic-quality claim
is made.  The evidence canonical SHA-256 is
\texttt{8dbbf6e5da48487da213bff21ee74f879f77e0f92801977621a74f648111c49b}.
