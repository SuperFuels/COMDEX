\section{Intermediate-Activation Block Addressing Gate}

\subsection{First-principles hypothesis}

The exact 120B path remains dominated by moving and evaluating four selected
experts in each of 36 layers.  A new mechanism tested whether the down
projection could be made block-addressable after the gate and up projections
had already calculated the exact SwiGLU intermediate.  If most intermediate
energy were concentrated in a small number of 32-coordinate blocks, AION could
retain the exact gate/up calculation and fetch only the consequential columns
of the down matrix from a warehouse-native layout.

This differs from the stopped router-input sparsity experiment: selection is
performed at the true internal dependency boundary, after each real expert's
nonlinear intermediate is known.  The experiment used original unrestricted
routes, router gates, MXFP4 weights and biases.  It then zeroed only the lowest
energy intermediate blocks before the down projection.  All source weights
were loaded for measurement, so the reported traffic reductions are
theoretical bounds rather than speed claims.

\subsection{Cross-family result}

The gate covered 681 real layer-position observations drawn from the frozen
arithmetic, extraction and unseen-reasoning capture sets.  The result was a
decisive falsification.

No candidate met the predeclared combination of at least $1.25\times$
theoretical traffic reduction, p95 output-relative $L_2\leq0.02$, and p95
expert-contribution-relative $L_2\leq0.15$.  Retaining only 25\% of down blocks
provided the sole material traffic bound, but its p95 output error was 0.2520.
Retaining 90\% reduced p95 output error only to 0.0391 while making the maximum
traffic benefit just $1.034\times$.  Status:
\texttt{STOP\_INTERMEDIATE\_BLOCK\_OMISSION}.

\subsection{Conclusion and evidence binding}

The nonlinear expert intermediate is dense in the sense relevant to accurate
down-projection execution.  An intelligent warehouse can address the blocks,
but it cannot omit enough of them to generate the missing multi-fold gain.
This closes unconditional energy-ranked intermediate block omission as a speed
branch.  It does not alter the promoted unrestricted exact frontier of
approximately 1.72--1.83 tokens/s or the non-quality top-1 physical ceiling of
28.62 tokens/s.
