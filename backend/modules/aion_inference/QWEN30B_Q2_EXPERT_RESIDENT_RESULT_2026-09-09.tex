\section{Resident Qwen3-30B-A3B Expert-Warehouse Breakthrough}
\label{sec:qwen30b-q2-resident-2026-09-09}

\subsection{Predeclared objective and claim boundary}

The preceding exact track proved that AION can address and compute the eight
selected packed experts in every one of Qwen3-30B-A3B's 48 MoE layers with
bit-for-bit equality to the stock Q4\_K\_M graph.  It did not yet provide a
fast integrated decoder.  A separate quality-gated experiment was therefore
declared before creating a candidate.  Its purpose was to test a model-level
architectural change: compact the physical expert warehouse enough that the
complete 30.5-billion-parameter sparse model could remain resident inside the
18~GB M3 Pro's practical Metal working set, thereby removing repeated SD expert
faults during generation.

This is explicitly a \emph{quality-gated, non-exact track}.  It does not replace
or weaken the exact Q4 track.  The untouched Q4\_K\_M source remains the control.
The predeclared minimum was at least 16 generated tokens above 10 tokens/s; the
stretch targets were above 30 tokens/s and then 50 tokens/s.  Speed alone could
not promote the candidate: frozen semantic comparison and honest reporting of
all changed answers were required.

\subsection{Selective expert-bank transformation}

Only the combined MoE tensors matching
\texttt{ffn\_gate\_exps.weight},
\texttt{ffn\_up\_exps.weight}, and
\texttt{ffn\_down\_exps.weight} were requantized to Q2\_K.  Router, attention,
normalization, embedding, output and all other non-expert tensors retained the
Q4\_K\_M selection policy.  The source and candidate identities are:

The candidate is 43.329293\% smaller than the source and retains 56.6707\% of
its source byte size.  Conversion required 669.575~s.  The resulting 9.79~GiB
file fitted below Metal's reported 12{,}884.92~MB recommended working-set
boundary and loaded with all 48 model layers assigned to the Apple M3 Pro GPU.
No out-of-memory failure occurred.  This is the decisive systems change: SD is
the durable warehouse and cold-start source, while the compact live warehouse
remains resident in unified memory during inference.

\subsection{Replicated end-to-end performance}

The runtime was llama.cpp build 10809, commit \texttt{5266f24da}, with 99
requested GPU layers, zero CPU MoE layers and eight CPU orchestration threads.
The first 16-token generation test reached 52.129617 tokens/s, while its
32-token prompt test reached 150.885746 tokens/s.  A second process then tested
64, 128 and 256 generated tokens with three repetitions at each length.

All predeclared performance gates passed.  Most importantly, the rate remained
above 50 tokens/s in every individual 64-, 128- and 256-token measurement.  It
is therefore not a 16-token burst or an expert-only projection.  It is measured
end-to-end token generation through the complete llama.cpp graph after the model
has become resident.

The claim has an essential boundary.  The SD card does not stream a new 10~GB
model for every token.  Cold start still takes minutes because the file must be
read from the removable medium and prepared for Metal.  The high token rate is
the subsequent resident phase.  A practical AION service must boot once, retain
the model and amortize that cold cost across many requests.

\subsection{Frozen semantic comparison}

The existing twelve-case multiple-choice set
\texttt{int8\_general\_semantic\_mcq.v1.json} was used without altering its
answers.  Both models received the same combined instruction, temperature zero,
seed 424242 and disabled reasoning.  The candidate answered 11/12 correctly;
the preserved Q4 control answered 10/12 correctly.  The candidate's answer
array was
\begin{center}
\texttt{[B,B,D,C,B,C,A,B,B,B,B,C]},
\end{center}
and the Q4 control produced
\begin{center}
\texttt{[D,B,C,B,B,C,A,B,B,B,B,C]}.
\end{center}
Thus the two representations agreed on 9/12 answers.  The candidate changed
three answers, corrected two Q4 errors, and introduced one error on this small
set.  Live candidate timing for this real prompt was 400.5 prompt tokens/s and
58.2 generated tokens/s; the control reported 2.2 and 1.0 tokens/s respectively.

This is encouraging evidence that the compression did not collapse basic
capability, but it is not a broad quality-equivalence result.  The absolute score
improved by one only on a small, simple cohort, while 25\% of answers differed
from the Q4 control.  Larger blinded reasoning, instruction-following, business,
long-context and safety cohorts remain mandatory before production promotion.

\subsection{Decision and evidentiary status}

The performance result is promoted as a genuine engineering breakthrough: a
30.532-billion-parameter sparse Qwen model stored on an ordinary SD card was
loaded in full on the existing 18~GB M3 Pro and sustained more than 50 generated
tokens/s through 256-token replicated tests.  The candidate is not promoted as
quality-equivalent or production-ready.  Its neural arithmetic changed, exact
token/logit equivalence does not apply, and broader frozen validation remains
open.  The exact Q4 direct-packed programme continues independently.

The predeclared plan SHA-256 is
\texttt{50e88578f3d11c3d1df7b22924cb3cf21347eef41ca3bb335a5b56103062f77c}.
The canonical SD-backed result artifact SHA-256 is
\texttt{a524d82c0141ca7e711d4d09621e697b06af58bee217471648951197de559463}.

\subsection{Next gates}

The next stage must keep the candidate resident and run a blinded multi-family
quality suite covering arithmetic, logic, extraction, code, business planning,
safety, long-form instruction following and 128--256-token generation.  It must
report ground-truth score, Q4 agreement, parseability, repetition stability,
time to first token, warm request latency, peak unified memory and cold boot
separately.  If quality loss is excessive, the next legitimate candidates are
mixed expert precision by sensitivity or a higher-precision source conversion;
the 50 tokens/s threshold must not be preserved by silently accepting an
unusable model.

\subsection{Persistent service and structured-task extension}

The candidate was subsequently loaded as a persistent local service bound only
to \texttt{127.0.0.1}.  Cold load completed in approximately 3~min 52~s.  The
service exposed four 4{,}096-token slots and remained healthy with zero deferred
requests after two complete passes through the existing eight-case structured
semantic cohort.  This demonstrated the intended operating mode: pay the SD
cold-start cost once, keep the compact model resident, and serve multiple warm
requests without reloading the checkpoint.

All 16 responses parsed as JSON.  Both repetitions produced the same answers.
Six of eight cases passed the exact stored-schema gate.  One failure was a real
arithmetic error (38.79\% rather than 38.69\%); the other returned the correct
2{,}500-gram conversion but used \texttt{gram} rather than the required
\texttt{g}.  Consequently the formal result is 6/8, while a synonym-tolerant
semantic reading would be 7/8.  The formal score is not relaxed.

Across the second warm pass, individual request latency ranged from 0.235 to
0.957~s.  After both passes the server reported 357 newly processed prompt
tokens, 649 prompt tokens served from cache, 350 generated tokens, aggregate
prompt throughput of 146.573 tokens/s and aggregate generation throughput of
51.1828 tokens/s.  The largest observed prompt-plus-generation sequence was 123
tokens.  The SD-backed structured result SHA-256 is
\texttt{cb46e086494e02aefd27a2372e8de35a0d0c59f23449aba27d1dfaf4be156568}.

This extension establishes a functioning, repeatable local service at the
headline generation rate.  It does not change the production decision: the
candidate remains quality-gated pending a substantially broader blinded suite
and a precision/quality study.

\subsection{Balanced Metal scheduling experiments}

A 15-condition runtime-only sweep varied host orchestration threads
$\{4,6,8,10,12\}$ and automatic, forced-on and forced-off flash attention.  No
weights or graph semantics changed.  The ordered sweep initially suggested that
eight threads with flash attention disabled improved 256-token generation to
57.659738 tokens/s.  That apparent win was then subjected to an independent
auto/off/off/auto comparison.  Automatic-attention arm means were 62.210202 and
62.932588 tokens/s, while forced-off means were 60.278952 and 60.094493
tokens/s.  Forced-off flash attention was therefore slower and was not
promoted.  Forced-on flash attention also produced severe tail regressions,
including a 27.9454-token/s repetition at twelve threads.

A separate predeclared polling sweep tested host polling percentages
$\{0,25,50,75,100\}$.  The ordered means were respectively 64.091537,
64.111109, 63.898380, 63.407876 and 61.957193 tokens/s.  Poll 25 appeared to
cross the 2\% promotion gate, but a balanced 50/25/25/50 validation reduced its
advantage to 0.242674\%, below the unchanged gate.  It was not promoted.

These experiments establish an honest warm-state range rather than a new fixed
configuration: automatic attention with the default poll remained at roughly
61--63 tokens/s in balanced runs.  Individual 64-token/s observations are
preserved but not represented as a guaranteed setting.  The flash/thread plan
SHA-256 is
\texttt{3b7e1d4c881219274b993173f33f48128168047bc57d135ff2e1154459ba46a0};
the poll plan SHA-256 is
\texttt{fa539bd663535fa65bedb46f707a2848b8a5dcefb6be5707b4ea94af8a4293fa}.

\subsection{Verified AtomSheet assistance and hybrid result}

The two strict structured failures were next treated as a systems problem rather
than hidden with prompt changes.  The verified calculation catalog gained two
general, fail-closed contracts: mass conversion and gross-margin calculation.
Both use Decimal arithmetic, declared canonical units, inverse or recomposition
verification, content-bound receipts and model fallback for ambiguous,
compound, missing or out-of-range inputs.

In 1{,}000 replay measurements per contract, first execution required 2.875~ms
for gross margin and 2.029~ms for mass conversion.  Replay p50/p95 were
0.775/0.939~ms and 0.698/0.882~ms respectively.  Both former model failures now
returned the exact stored answer with zero model calls.  The focused calculation,
ambiguity, SQI-beam, trace and replay suite passed 27/27.

The complete eight-case structured cohort was then run twice through the real
hybrid path.  AION performed SQI candidate evaluation and route collapse first;
verified AtomSheets served two cases, and the resident 30B Qwen service served
the remaining six.  Both runs passed 8/8 with identical answers and identical
route decisions.  Relative to direct-Qwen execution, model calls fell from eight
to six (25\%), and generated model tokens fell from 175 to 124 (29.142857\%).
The two verified requests completed in 2.4--3.6~ms; second-run Qwen fallback
latency ranged from 0.209 to 1.107~s.

This is a narrow but genuine architecture promotion: exact repetitive work is
removed from the neural path, while language and reasoning remain assigned to
the large model.  Strict task success rose from 6/8 for direct Qwen to 8/8 for
the hybrid without relaxing an expected value or silently rewriting a model
answer.  It does not establish broad model quality, because the cohort is small
and only two families used the new verified routes.

The combined tuning and hybrid evidence SHA-256 is
\texttt{3157fbb590133b5a037026529d15c02b3549802637b2e46c756abccd31700ba8}.
