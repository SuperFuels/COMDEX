\section{Measured Runtime Opportunity Before Correction Scaling}
The proposed prepared-quartet route slab was checked against the existing
record before implementation. That combination had already been falsified:
the unrestricted 96-position extraction trace reused only one admitted ordered
quartet (0.0289\% of routes), while the adaptive trace's reuse did not repay
its multi-gigabyte slab storage. No new slab inference experiment was run.

Instead, an offline runtime-opportunity analyzer was built and tested against
canonical-hash-bound, completed, repeatable runtime receipts. It examines
continuation positions only, validates the complete 36-layer grid, counts
actual active experts, and ranks layers by aggregate finish and delivery
time. It does not perform inference or consume unseen evaluation prompts.
Four focused tests passed.

On the quiet-system adaptive-0.80 reasoning control, observed continuation
rates were 7.001898 and 5.491965 tokens/s. Only 22.8682\% of layer calls used
four experts. Consequently a compiler that replaces only the fourth selected
expert cannot act on approximately 77.13\% of calls in this existing control.

For exploratory accounting only, route finish plus delivery time was divided
by its active expert count, and the previous local resident correction ratio
of 0.0773 was assumed. Full replacement coverage of eligible E4 calls then
gave hypothetical multipliers of 1.04924 and 1.06370. Replacement of the last
selected expert on every eligible two-, three- and four-expert call gave
hypothetical multipliers of 1.25993 and 1.27733. These are not measured
candidate speedups or rigorous physical ceilings: aggregate finish overhead,
unequal expert delivery cost, instrumentation overlap, controller work,
fallback frequency and changed locality are not isolated. The resident
microbenchmark ratio does not measure the fault path.

The practical conclusion is narrower than a speed breakthrough: E4-only
correction coverage may be too small in the current adaptive control to
justify a large controller investment. Correction of the last selected
contribution on two- and three-expert calls is a distinct hypothesis requiring
new authentic targets, fitting support and downstream quality validation.
The existing E4 cartridge cannot be re-labelled as a C2 or C3 certificate.
No validation gate is relaxed, and the current training queue continues.

\subsection{Implementation of ranked contribution targets}
The next compiler phase has begun with a capture extension: future teacher
processes retain the already-calculated gated second- and third-expert vectors
as well as the fourth. Each is a 2,880-element little-endian F32 vector
(11,520 bytes), identified by original ordered route and actual native gate.
This adds 23,040 bytes per four-expert observation, without another expert
read or neural calculation beyond existing counterfactual capture. Values
converted to F64 for diagnostics are converted back to their original F32
representation for target storage. Hash-bound verification checks both new
targets together whenever either is declared. Older captures with only E4
remain supported and are not retroactively declared to contain E2/E3.

The currently running arithmetic process loaded the earlier capture code:
it continues unchanged and will not acquire these new targets mid-run.
Subsequent teacher processes load the extension. Thus implementation is
complete, but successful future E2/E3 capture is not yet measured evidence.
Thirteen focused ranked-target, queue and opportunity tests passed.
Teacher vectors alone do not certify adaptive-state reuse: gating,
activation region, active count and reduction order require separately
validated correction training and downstream tests before substitution.

\subsection{Incremental local fitting gate now launched}
A bounded worker watches verified training completions and launches local
layer-12 fitting/development probes across different prompt families. It
does not wait for the full corpus and does not read partial captures or
selection/holdout prompts. Rank-specific targets are included only where
their hashed vectors exist. Models are fixed expert-specific affine kernel
ridge maps of activation to ungated contribution, rescaled by the actual
gate. Regularization is fixed at 0.001, fitting support at least four
observations, and fitting size at most 32. A 0.80 nearest-fitting-activation
cosine screen declines unsupported calls. This support screen is experimental,
not a sufficient downstream certificate.

Each model is saved and hashed before the different-family development calls
are evaluated. Supported calls must stay at or below 2\% local error and
improve over omission; stored arrays must fit the existing 25\% expert-size
ceiling. All supported development calls must pass to flag a local candidate.
Development evidence is not untouched final validation. Prediction timing
during teacher load is diagnostic only and cannot pass a relative compute
economics or generation-speed gate. Three focused fitting/support tests passed.
The worker limits BLAS to one thread and performs no new SD weight reads or
neural expert evaluations. Initial state is waiting for verified cross-family
completions, not achieved corrections. Full-generation speed remains untested
for this extension; no shortcut is injected automatically.

\subsection{First verified trajectory and real-data sanity result}
The first arithmetic trajectory completed verification: 1,332 observations
across all 36 layers, with repeatable routes, hidden state and logits. Its
16 continuation positions supply 576 real E4 calls. Coverage report canonical
SHA-256 is

\end{quote}
No cross-family identity support or certified coverage is inferred from one
family. The business-family capture then started with the extended E2/E3
target writer.

While independent continuation data was collected, a same-prompt chronological
sanity probe fitted only on prefill and evaluated continuation. The three
layer/expert pairs with at least four prefill targets and later demand were
layer 2/expert 0, layer 13/expert 0, and layer 18/expert 53. This choice used
training support counts, not test error. Stored models were 184,324, 149,764
and 149,764 bytes respectively. Their two, one and three continuation calls
were all rejected at the frozen 0.80 cosine boundary; observed similarities
were 0.1581--0.5985. Thus supported coverage was zero out of six calls.
No numerical error claim is made for rejected calls, and no speed or certificate
was promoted. This temporally dependent same-prompt smoke is not independent
validation. Its useful conclusion is that prefill activations cannot be assumed
to provide continuation support merely because the expert identity repeats.
The proper cross-family continuation probe remains running automatically as
verified completion data becomes available.

\subsection{Ranked correction integration: exact-contribution control}
An independent native audit extended the corrected reduction mechanism to
the last selected contribution on two-, three- and four-expert diagnostic
calls. The comparisons were one real expert plus the real gated second
contribution, two plus the real gated third, and three plus the real gated
fourth. All three outputs were bit-for-bit identical to their respective
original active-expert controls, with zero differing output elements.
The original left-associated addition order and residual boundary were
preserved. Existing exact and three-plus-C4 entry points were unchanged.

This audit used one CPU thread and already-existing local L2 frames, checked
read-only against all six source component hashes. It performed zero SD expert
reads, no cache admission and no cache deletion. Its canonical evidence hash is
\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
0d21ecacfd752304361670515b0470c733\allowbreak
fee5089aaff8765608e0ed46655f72
\end{quote}
No learned correction was used. Therefore this is an integration correctness
gate, not approximation accuracy, semantic certification or speed evidence.
It removes arithmetic-order contamination from future ranked correction tests;
cross-family support, numerical accuracy, downstream stability and charged
full-generation economics remain mandatory before any promotion.

\subsection{Coverage accounting without favorable-example selection}
Incremental local reports now use every development call of each rank as the
traffic denominator, including identities lacking a fitted model. They record
unmatched calls, activation-supported calls, individual numerical passes and
calls belonging to regions that pass the complete local probe. A region with
any supported numerical failure is not credited as a usable candidate region.
Missing rank targets yield an unavailable share, not zero certified coverage.
Certified traffic share remains explicitly unset: local training/development
passes cannot certify downstream semantics or adaptive execution states.
The focused suite including coverage-denominator and failed-region checks
passed 22 of 22 tests. These are software tests, not approximation or speed
promotion. Business teacher collection remains active; partial observations
are excluded from fitting until the complete repeatability seal is verified.
