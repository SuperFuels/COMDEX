\section{Overnight 120B Student and SD-Native Tokenizer Programme}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-overnight-route-student-20260912}

\subsection{Scope and inherited performance boundary}

This section records work performed during the overnight programme ending
12 September 2026.  Its purpose was to determine whether a compact resident
calculation could reproduce the contribution of omitted GPT-OSS 120B experts
accurately enough to approach the previously measured resident top-one
execution ceiling.

No new generated-token speed result was promoted during this work.  The
inherited exact unrestricted frontier therefore remains 1.72455 tokens per
second on the frozen arithmetic family and 1.82858 tokens per second on the
frozen extraction family.  The previously measured fixed top-one path remains
28.6174 tokens per second, but it is a physical execution ceiling only: its
repetitive and incoherent output fails the semantic-quality contract.

The overnight programme did not alter the exact router, the authoritative
four-expert teacher, the source MXFP4 weights, or the exact fallback.  Every
compact candidate described below belongs to a separately labelled
quality-gated research track.

\subsection{Pre-registered route-concentrated hypothesis}

The central hypothesis was that one original expert could remain as a fast
resident path while a substantially smaller learned correction reproduced the
combined contribution of the other selected experts.  For layer $\ell$,
activation $x$, selected expert identities $e_{1:4}$ and normalized gates
$g_{1:4}$, the proposed form was
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{M}_{\ell}(x)
 = g_1E_{\ell,e_1}(x)
 + C_{\ell}\!\left(x,e_{1:4},g_{1:4}\right),
\end{equation}
where $C_{\ell}$ is a compact resident route-conditioned correction.

The layer-level numerical promotion gate was fixed before testing:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item held-out relative-$L_2$ p95 no greater than 0.02;
  \item maximum relative-$L_2$ no greater than 0.05;
  \item certified non-fallback coverage of at least 10\%;
  \item at most half the teacher's expert bytes on accepted positions;
  \item lower accepted-path latency than the corresponding original packed
        expert path; and
  \item preservation of every failure and fallback in the evidence record.
\end{enumerate}
Whole prompt families, rather than individual positions, were separated for
training, selection and unseen evaluation.  Oracle coverage was treated only
as an upper bound and never as deployable coverage.

\subsection{Real-anchor local correction}

The first candidate used 33 genuine arithmetic anchors from layer 12,
covering 23 distinct omitted-expert identities.  For each anchor, 32
perturbations were sampled in a 24-dimensional empirical activation subspace.
The original SD-backed expert recalculated every target, yielding 1,056 exact
teacher perturbations.  Extraction data selected rank and regularization; the
21-position reasoning family remained unseen until the final gate.

The selected rank-16 expert-conditioned local linear cartridge occupied
12,186,061 bytes.  Its measured calculation latency was 0.02025\,ms at p50 and
0.02129\,ms at p95.  Speed and size were therefore excellent, but transfer
accuracy failed decisively.  On extraction, zero selected rows met the
two-percent error boundary and p95 relative-$L_2$ was 0.10258.  On unseen
reasoning, matching expert identities existed for 10 of 21 rows, but zero rows
were safe; relative-$L_2$ was 0.06752 at p50 and 0.07433 at p95.

The result was recorded as
\texttt{STOP\_ROUTE\_CONCENTRATED\_LOCAL}.  It showed that local linear
neighbourhoods around arithmetic activations do not reproduce the omitted
expert function across semantic families, even when every perturbation target
is recalculated from the original weights.

\subsection{Higher-capacity nonlinear correction}

A materially larger layer-12 student was then evaluated.  It consumed the
complete 2,880 dimensional activation, embedded the omitted expert identity,
included the omitted expert's gate value, and used two nonlinear hidden
layers.  Candidate capacity increased as far as 512 hidden units.  Model
selection used extraction; reasoning remained unopened until the development
gate.

The selected cartridge occupied 12,980,881 bytes and evaluated in
approximately 1.0004\,ms median.  It recognized omitted-expert identities for
9 of 22 extraction positions and 10 of 21 unseen reasoning positions, but
zero positions satisfied the two-percent safety boundary.  Extraction p95
relative-$L_2$ was 0.11362.  Reasoning error was 0.07343 at p50 and 0.08319 at
p95.  The result was
\texttt{STOP\_ROUTE\_CONCENTRATED\_NONLINEAR}.

This falsified the proposition that additional network capacity alone could
repair the missing cross-family structure in the arithmetic-centred training
manifold.

\subsection{Expanded genuine-family retraining}

The nonlinear architecture was frozen before genuine extraction trajectories
were added to training.  The expanded dataset contained 55 real arithmetic
and extraction anchors, 33 omitted-expert identities and 1,320 exact teacher
recalculations.  The preceding 512-unit architecture and regularization were
not retuned against reasoning.

Broader genuine-family training increased reasoning identity coverage from
10/21 to 12/21, but certified safe coverage remained zero.  On those covered
reasoning positions, relative-$L_2$ was 0.07193 at p50 and 0.08175 at p95.
The 12,980,561-byte cartridge evaluated in 0.9780\,ms median.  It was again
recorded as \texttt{STOP\_ROUTE\_CONCENTRATED\_NONLINEAR}.

The important negative result is that neither greater capacity, exact local
teacher perturbations, nor the addition of a second genuine semantic family
created a certifiable operating region.  Repeating the same architecture with
additional near-anchor samples was therefore stopped.

\subsection{Retained-expert low-rank oracle bounds}

Before implementing a more expensive adapter inside the retained expert, an
intentionally impossible oracle tested whether the available output directions
could satisfy the declared gate.  The oracle observed each held-out target and
selected its optimal coefficients in the complete span of the training
targets.  A deployable predictor would possess less information, so failure of
this oracle is a strong stop condition.

With the original 55-target fit corpus, the complete rank-54 span made only
1 of 21 unseen reasoning positions safe: 4.76\% coverage, below the declared
10\% minimum.  Relative-$L_2$ was 0.05833 at p50 and 0.07174 at p95.

To test whether the limit was simply caused by insufficient directions, AION
then composed 12 previously verified families containing 957 layer-position
records.  These included genuine arithmetic and extraction trajectories, BOS,
four independent synthetic tokens and two independent eight-position
KV-linked trajectories.  Rebuilding the layer-12 top-three residual dataset
increased the fit set from 55 to 78 targets.  Even the complete rank-77 span
still admitted only 1 of 21 reasoning positions, again 4.76\%.  Error improved
only slightly to 0.05744 at p50 and 0.07101 at p95.

Both oracle experiments were recorded as
\texttt{STOP\_RETAINED\_EXPERT\_LOW\_RANK\_ADAPTER}.  This avoided an
unjustified adapter-training cycle: better coefficient prediction cannot
create output directions that do not exist in the fitted span.

\subsection{SD-native GPT-OSS tokenizer recovery}

The failed students exposed a separate experimental dependency: broader
genuine semantic trajectories could not be collected cleanly without a trusted
local GPT-OSS tokenizer.  AION therefore reconstructed the tokenizer directly
from the verified virtual GGUF header in the compressed SD warehouse.

The implementation reads a 201,088-token vocabulary and 446,189 BPE merges
from the SD-resident model representation.  It implements the GGUF-declared
\texttt{gpt-4o} pre-tokenization rule, byte encoding, ranked BPE merging,
special-token addressing and the GPT-OSS Harmony user wrapper.  It neither
downloaded another model nor depended upon an externally installed GPT-OSS
tokenizer.

Against the frozen public prompt ``What is 17 multiplied by 23?'', the
SD-native implementation reproduced all 15 reference token identifiers
exactly, including Harmony control tokens.  The tokenizer gate status is
\texttt{PASSED}, and the focused integrity suite passed 27/27 tests.

This is a genuine enabling result, but it is not an inference-speed or model-
quality result.  It makes the compressed SD warehouse self-describing for
prompt construction and permits new teacher trajectories to remain
cryptographically bound to the same model representation.

The corpus contains only family identifiers, public-prompt hashes, token IDs,
capture positions and the requested layer.  It contains no personal data or
customer data.  Its status is ``ready for teacher capture.''

The explanation family subsequently completed a real GPT-OSS 120B capture.
Both passes traversed all 36 attention/MoE blocks for each of the 16
teacher-forced tokens.  Layer-12 evidence was captured at ten positions.
Routes were repeatable, and the final hidden state and logits were bitwise
repeatable.  The result status is \texttt{PASSED}.  Because the run was a
teacher-trajectory capture rather than a balanced continuation benchmark, no
tokens-per-second claim is derived from it.

The next business-family capture was interrupted before a final evidence
artifact was written.  It is therefore excluded from all counts and claims.
It must be rerun from the frozen token corpus; the incomplete execution is not
treated as either a pass or a failure.

\subsection{Conclusions and next gate}

The overnight programme produced an enabling infrastructure breakthrough, not
a generated-token speed breakthrough.  It established that several attractive
small corrections are fast enough but lack the expert-specific, route-specific
and semantic structure required for safe substitution.  The impossible-oracle
results further show that this deficiency cannot be repaired merely by a
better selector over the current low-rank target span.

At the same time, the SD-native tokenizer removes the dependency that had
prevented broad, controlled teacher capture.  The next admissible experiment
is therefore to finish the four frozen public-family captures, keep one whole
family blind, and train a route-concentrated student from genuinely broader
semantic trajectories.  It may advance to bounded multi-layer substitution
only if it passes the existing numerical boundary on the blind family.

Until that happens, the scientifically valid statement is:
\begin{quote}
The exact unrestricted GPT-OSS 120B runtime remains approximately
1.72--1.83 tokens per second.  A 28.6174-token-per-second top-one execution
ceiling has been measured but fails quality.  Overnight compact correction
candidates did not close that gap; however, AION can now tokenize new GPT-OSS
prompts directly from its compressed SD warehouse and has completed the first
repeatable new-family teacher capture needed for the next student design.
\end{quote}
