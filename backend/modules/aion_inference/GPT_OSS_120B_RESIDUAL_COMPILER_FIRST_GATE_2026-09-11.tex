\section{Authorized Compact Residual Expert Compiler: First Gate}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-residual-first-gate}

\subsection{Authorization and claim boundary}

The owner explicitly authorized training and injection of compact correction
cartridges for the separately labelled quality track.  The authorization is
recorded in
\texttt{GPT\_OSS\_120B\_RESIDUAL\_TRAINING\_AUTHORIZATION\_2026-09-11.json}.
It permits a resident original top-1 expert plus a learned correction for the
three omitted expert contributions.  It does not permit modifying the source
checkpoint, describing the candidate as bit-exact unrestricted GPT-OSS 120B,
ingesting personal or customer data, publishing, or weakening the exact
fallback.  The original four-expert path remains the teacher and authoritative
fallback.

\subsection{Target calculation}

For layer input $x$, let the unrestricted router choose experts $e_1,\ldots,e_4$
with gates $g_1,\ldots,g_4$.  The exact mixture contribution is
\begin{equation}
  y_{\mathrm{MoE}}(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{4}g_i E_{e_i}(x).
\end{equation}
The proposed fast calculation retains the original first expert and learns a
compact correction $C_\theta$:
\begin{equation}
  \widehat y_{\mathrm{MoE}}(x)=g_1 E_{e_1}(x)+
  C_\theta\!\left(x,\,g,\,e,\,\ell\right).
\end{equation}
The supervised target is therefore the measured omitted contribution
\begin{equation}
  r(x)=\sum_{i=2}^{4}g_i E_{e_i}(x).
\end{equation}
The candidate changes the model calculation and belongs solely to the
quality-gated track.  A low-confidence decision must invoke the original
four-expert calculation.

\subsection{First diagonal cartridge}

The first deliberately small probe used a coordinate-aligned ridge correction.
Candidate features were selected only with grouped cross-validation over the
training families.  They included the top-1 contribution, post-attention input,
router input, omitted gate mass and gate entropy.  Calibration and holdout
families were excluded from feature selection.  This cartridge was cheap enough
to satisfy the intended runtime shape, but it failed the numerical gate.

\subsection{Full training-span oracle}

Before increasing model complexity, an intentionally impossible upper-bound
test was performed.  For every sampled layer, the oracle received the complete
centred linear span of all same-layer training residuals and was allowed to
choose the best coefficients separately for each holdout target.  A deployable
predictor would have strictly less information and freedom.

The oracle still produced holdout output relative-$L_2$ p95 of $0.120623$ and
omitted-contribution relative-$L_2$ p95 of $0.898632$, far outside the declared
two-percent output target.  The result is labelled
\texttt{STOP\_CURRENT\_CAPTURE\_SPAN}; canonical hash
\texttt{fc68d89b17f7fd2d1677446881a1fc747d63892e6a0047dfc314d34b309cad07}.
This falsifies a whole class of more elaborate predictors trained only on the
same synthetic/two-trajectory corpus.  It does not falsify compact residual
correction itself: it proves that more representative teacher captures are
required first.

\subsection{Bound derived dataset and frozen real-prompt cohort}

A reusable derived dataset of 288 rows was built from hash-verified captures.
It contains the original top-1 contribution and exact four-expert-minus-top-1
residual, but no prompt text or personal/customer data.  Its manifest status is
\texttt{READY\_FOR\_AUTHORIZED\_RESIDUAL\_TRAINING}, canonical hash
\texttt{63937d6642939fecb1c42884cf50a251f2b85bc6379e2a6cf9995bb6aa5a431a}.

Before collecting additional data, the next cohort was frozen in
\texttt{GPT\_OSS\_120B\_RESIDUAL\_COHORT\_2026-09-11.json} (file SHA-256
\texttt{eeac9709b77319ab08e5e97491d8e6e52dbd411c5a6552233b9a5261d20243fe}).
Arithmetic and extraction are training families; reasoning is development;
business and writing are blind holdouts.  Twelve layers spanning the complete
36-layer stack are sampled.  The writing prompt is synthetic and was tokenized
with the model-declared chat template through the verified header-only GGUF
shell.  Architecture and hyperparameters may see training; thresholds and
stopping may see development; neither blind family may be opened until the
candidate and acceptance rules are frozen.

\subsection{Conclusion and next gate}

The first authorized correction was not promoted.  Its failure is informative:
the missing three-expert effect is neither coordinatewise nor contained in the
variation of the current small capture corpus.  The next valid experiment is to
capture unrestricted four-expert teacher states from diverse real continuations,
fit a compact cross-coordinate residual model using only the frozen training
families, freeze it on development, and finally open the two blind families.
Promotion requires internal repeatability, semantic parity, bounded memory,
measured traffic and speed improvement, and a working exact fallback.  No
tokens-per-second claim is made for the residual compiler at this gate.
