\section{Disk-Guarded Authentic Teacher Capture Restart}
The initial all-layer arithmetic capture was interrupted when available Mac
storage fell below the declared safety reserve. Its approximately 117 MB of
partial data is preserved and is not counted as verified evidence. Following
authorized application and disposable-cache cleanup, a fresh training-only
queue was launched under the local results directory with suffix
\texttt{20260912\_retry01}; the existing frozen corpus and warehouse are unchanged.

The queue now checks local free space every five seconds during each child
capture, as well as between captures. If space falls below 3 GiB, it sends an
interrupt to permit normal inference-priority lease cleanup, waits up to
30 seconds, then escalates to termination and, if necessary, kill. Partial
files remain preserved without a completion certificate. This is a sampled
safety guard, not an atomic disk reservation: other applications can still
consume storage between checks. Failed or interrupted captures are never
counted as completed training observations.

Restart-time free space was approximately 4.8 GiB. The target remains up to
24 training prompts, family-balanced across all 36 layers, subject to the
3 GiB reserve. No additional 20 GiB delivery cache is allocated. Thirteen
focused queue and economics tests passed, including interruption on exhausted
reserve and normal guarded completion. No model arithmetic, certificate gate,
selection/holdout boundary, or validated token-rate claim is changed.

\subsection{Second preserved interruption and fresh restart}
The first retry subsequently crossed the same 3 GiB reserve and was stopped
by the during-capture guard. It left 117 partial layer observations and no
final teacher receipt or verified completion. These observations are not
counted as completed evidence. After further space recovery, the Mac reported
approximately 19 GiB free at the next restart. A new queue root with suffix
\texttt{20260912\_retry02} was launched for up to 24 training-only captures;
both earlier roots remain preserved. The initial arithmetic job declared
1,332 expected layer-position observations. That count is a target, not an
achieved result. Thirteen focused checks passed again before launch. All
existing numerical, quality, integrity and memory gates remain unchanged.
