\section{AtomSheet, SQI, and GHX Assistance Gate}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-atomsheet-sqi-ghx}

This experiment separated two propositions that must not be conflated:
AtomSheets and SQI may prevent suitable tasks from reaching GPT-OSS 120B,
whereas GHX activation addressing might reuse internal neural calculations.
The former measures completed-task throughput; the latter could affect raw
decoder work if safe matches exist.

\subsection{GHX activation-addressed expert reuse}

A layer-12 GHX bank was populated from the arithmetic family.  Each entry bound
the layer, original expert identity, normalized real router activation, and the
original expert's exact ungated MXFP4 output.  For extraction, SQI selected the
nearest entry belonging to the same expert.  The current router gate was always
preserved, and every miss executed the original expert.  Similarity thresholds
were selected on extraction under a maximum relative layer-output error of
0.02, then frozen for the unseen reasoning family.  Business and writing
remained blind.

There were zero exact activation-address matches.  The highest threshold that
satisfied the extraction error gate was 0.975, but it reused zero of 88 expert
calls.  Applied to reasoning, it reused zero of 84 expert calls.  Traffic
reduction was therefore exactly $1.00\times$.  At threshold 0.95, three of 88
extraction calls were reused, but maximum relative layer-output error rose to
0.066585.  At threshold 0.85, six calls were reused and maximum error was
0.067552.  More permissive thresholds reached up to $1.49\times$ estimated
traffic reduction while producing errors as high as 0.345477.

The decision is \texttt{STOP\_GHX\_ACTIVATION\_ADDRESS}.  GHX may safely reuse
identical states created by repeated prefixes or deterministic workflows, but
cosine-near activations from changing tokens are not interchangeable expert
inputs.  No approximate contribution was injected into the model.

The GHX canonical report SHA-256 is
\texttt{2e2123447553dbad58fcb496109aa527428ba6299e261c77f7351099d7e82bd9}.

\subsection{Governed AtomSheet and SQI task bypass}

A fresh immutable run on the SD evidence volume exercised 12 declared gateway
tasks.  Eight deterministic calculations or verified replays completed without
a model call; four language or coding tasks were correctly deferred to a model.
All routes were correct, all immediate answers were correct, every proof receipt
verified, the trace chain verified, and every beam collapse used the declared
highest-assurance-then-lowest-estimated-cost rule.

This is a genuine AION system-level speed mechanism: qualifying work is solved
in milliseconds rather than being decoded by the 120B model.  The 66.67\%
figure describes only the declared deterministic fixture and is not claimed as
a population-wide business workload percentage.  It also does not change the
raw unrestricted GPT-OSS 120B decode frontier of approximately 1.72--1.83
tokens/s.  AtomSheets increase successful tasks per unit time; they do not
replace arbitrary neural reasoning.
