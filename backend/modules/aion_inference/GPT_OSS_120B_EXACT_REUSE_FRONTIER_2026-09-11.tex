\section{Exact Response-Reuse Frontier and the Sub-GiB Memory Cliff}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-exact-reuse-frontier}

\subsection{Purpose and method}

After exact read/compute overlap reached 1.72455~t/s on arithmetic and
1.82858~t/s on extraction, the remaining settled runs still delivered
approximately 42--45~GB from the internal L2 tier.  A hash-bound offline
analyzer therefore measured response-only expert reuse after removing each
family's existing protected RAM pool.  It calculated weighted reuse distance,
the immutable first-touch floor, capacity-versus-avoided-delivery curves, and
both whole-expert and six-component retention frontiers.

The analysis assumes that an admitted immutable entry remains resident.  It
does not predict wall-time benefit or account for memory-pressure cost.
Component-granularity results describe a hypothetical mixed-residency kernel;
they are not runtime evidence.

\subsection{Physical reuse limits}

Arithmetic's 33 continuation positions made 2,930 L2 expert accesses to 1,186
unique layer-experts.  Their decoded delivery was 36.1665~GiB, of which the
first-touch floor alone was 14.6394~GiB.  Thus at most 59.52\% could be removed
by perfect response-local retention.  Eliminating 50\% would require 381
additional complete experts occupying 4.7029~GiB.  The median weighted reuse
distance was 3.2215~GiB; p90 was 8.3652~GiB and p95 was 10.8139~GiB.

Extraction's 22 continuation positions made 1,885 L2 accesses to 1,028 unique
layer-experts.  Delivery was 23.2675~GiB and the first-touch floor was
12.6891~GiB.  Consequently, even unlimited retention within that response can
remove only 45.46\%; a 50\% reduction is physically impossible without reuse
across requests or changing the model.  Median weighted reuse distance was
2.0614~GiB, p90 6.8136~GiB and p95 8.6050~GiB.

Six-component selection did not open a meaningful finer-grained frontier.  At
0.5~GiB it improved predicted reduction by only 0.1004 percentage points for
arithmetic and 0.0959 points for extraction.  At 4~GiB the advantage was below
0.004 percentage points.  All six components of a selected expert are consumed
by its packed calculation and inherit the same reuse frequency; splitting them
therefore adds complexity without material capacity benefit.  Decision:
\texttt{STOP\_COMPONENT\_GRANULARITY\_AS\_A\_CACHE\_STRATEGY}.

\subsection{Live 0.5 GiB boundary test}

The smallest plausible whole-expert candidate combined the promoted adaptive
overlap path with only 0.5~GiB of additional demand-admitted hot experts.  The
40-entry allowlist was hash-bound by a new cartridge plan.  The total declared
expert pool was 8.4~GiB; observed residency was 8.393~GiB.  The original
unrestricted four-expert router and all weights remained unchanged.

The mechanism behaved correctly.  All 40 experts were admitted, settled SD
fallbacks were zero, and settled L2 delivery fell from 45,129,243,480 to
39,483,117,864 bytes, a 12.51\% reduction.  Routes, generated tokens, final
hidden states and step logits remained bit-for-bit identical.

Nevertheless, settled continuation rates were 1.58565 and 1.41689~t/s, median
1.50127~t/s.  This is 12.95\% below the promoted overlap-only median of
1.72455~t/s.  Expert-finish time rose to 14.87 and 16.24~s, and attention time
also rose on the slower pass.  Even the sub-GiB resident extension therefore
crossed the useful memory/cache-pressure boundary on this 18~GB host.

Decision:
\texttt{NOT\_PROMOTED\_STOP\_ALL\_ADDITIONAL\_RAM\_EXPERT\_HOTSETS}.  The
planned 1.0~GiB test was cancelled rather than repeating the same physical
failure.  The promoted exact configuration remains the existing 7.9--8~GiB
family pool plus adaptive L2-read/compute overlap.

\subsection{Consequence for the next architecture}

The remaining order-of-magnitude target cannot be reached by retaining more
original expert bytes in unified memory, and component-level caching offers no
capacity escape.  Exact-track work must now reduce transient delivery or
consume packed data without enlarging the durable resident set.  Candidates
include a genuinely direct packed L2 kernel with bounded scratch space or a
different lossless physical representation whose decode cost is lower than the
bytes it removes.  Reduced experts, mixed precision and transformed weights
remain separate quality-track experiments.

\subsection{Stopped dependency-staged L2 pipeline}

A final bounded-scratch exact probe split each expert at its true dependency
boundary.  Gate and up components were read and evaluated first while the down
projection was fetched concurrently; the resulting F32 SwiGLU activation was
then consumed by the original packed MXFP4 down calculation.  A resident-only
gate over eight captured experts first established bitwise equivalence and
measured only 4.55\% splitting overhead, permitting an L2 microgate.

The real process-verified L2 gate used eight captured four-expert routes and six
balanced rounds, producing 48 observations per mode.  All outputs remained
bit-for-bit identical.  Nevertheless, whole-expert overlap required a median
4.9073~ms per route while staged projection required 7.3853~ms.  The
candidate/control speed ratio was 0.6645.  The extra file operation, graph call
and synchronization boundary cost substantially more than the short gate/up
compute interval could hide.  Decision:
\texttt{STOP\_STAGED\_PROJECTION\_IO}; do not integrate it into full generation.

The stopped runtime's canonical/stored SHA-256 values are
\texttt{f10a46617ed6ef1129b4f3919837981836d34d040b34bcca6c3981447bc9559d}
and
\texttt{33fe30fe9bb7fb54f9ab29d503dad0d36b4a2772656a51053fba929de55e3c4b}.
