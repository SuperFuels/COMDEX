\section{Bridging the 120B Quality Track to the Measured Top--1 Ceiling}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-quality-ceiling-bridge}

\subsection{Objective and invariant boundaries}

The immediate objective was to approach the measured top--1 physical ceiling of
$28.6174$ generated tokens per second while retaining useful GPT-OSS 120B
behaviour.  This ceiling is not a quality result: fixed top--1 continuation is
repeatable and fast but produces repetitive or incoherent answers.  The exact
unrestricted reference remains $1.72455$ tokens/s on arithmetic and $1.82858$
tokens/s on extraction.  Every experiment in this section is therefore labelled
either exact or quality-track, and no reduced-expert result is represented as
bit-exact unrestricted GPT-OSS 120B inference.

All experiments used the existing 18~GB M3 Pro Mac, the verified compressed
GPT-OSS 120B warehouse on the 64~GB SD card, original model weights, exact
unrestricted prompt processing, and an 8~GiB bounded request workbench.

\subsection{Layer-only top--1 ablations}

Two depth-only policies were tested before building a learned selector.  Using
top--1 only in layers 28--35 diverged from the correct 0.75 preserved-mass
trajectory at the fifth continuation token.  Restricting top--1 to layers
32--35 delayed, but did not remove, the defect: divergence occurred at the
eleventh continuation token.  Both candidates were internally repeatable and
were stopped.  Their canonical evidence hashes are respectively
\texttt{9855788439fa0719844789c6d7569a4e167a4a46e2b41e1811611a1461ef8a80}
and
\texttt{b064c90566dff361b61413d444e48b3d25c030dc5110c121c8734ef3d799e153}.
The result falsifies layer depth alone as a sufficient safety signal.

\subsection{Activation-bound counterfactual corpus}

A metadata-only counterfactual collector was added to the real 120B runtime.
For twelve continuation positions and all 36 layers it evaluated each selected
expert contribution from the original packed MXFP4 weights and recorded router
mass, gate entropy, gate gap, activation norms, and local output error.  This
produced 432 provenance-bound observations.  Reconstruction of the separately
calculated contributions matched the original layer output with maximum
relative $L_2$ error $4.761\times10^{-8}$.

The corpus showed that omitted experts were rarely negligible.  For top--1,
median local output relative $L_2$ error was $0.14165$ and p95 was $0.26957$.
For top--2 among the 399 three-expert observations, median error was $0.07378$
and p95 was $0.14990$.  No top--1 sample passed the conservative local gate of
output error at most $0.005$ and contribution error at most $0.1$; only three
of 432 passed even the loose $0.02/0.2$ gate.  Only seven of 399 top--2 samples
passed the loose gate.  The canonical corpus-run hash is
\texttt{d7a5308f90daadaae00c390dd291b1a90652f2ec892bc92008882d2238a1c058}.

This is important negative evidence: a selector based only on layer identity or
large router mass cannot safely turn most layers into top--1 execution.

\subsection{Reduced-route request cartridge}

The request-pool builder was extended to accept verified one--four-expert route
evidence and an explicit token offset.  For the first ten continuation
positions of the aggressive 0.65 preserved-mass draft, the exact active union
contained 597 layer--expert pairs and occupied 7,912,494,720 raw bytes
(approximately 7.37~GiB).  It therefore fit inside the declared 8~GiB
workbench.  The cryptographically bound pool has canonical hash
\texttt{dc69730fc9c0ed81fa1d582c123aeca7652e39e0a9facd62ba5f16a73a30fa15}.

With that pool preloaded, continuation expert lookup fell to approximately
$0.5$~ms/token, proving that the cartridge removed SD lookup from the measured
continuation critical path.  Throughput nevertheless remained $1.5826$--
$1.6395$ tokens/s in the first full probe because two- and three-expert
calculation, rather than lookup, had become dominant.  The run hash is
\texttt{90321c9158c509551f6f40c29d99fcbf609fe17b578b10cef7b9b149357d4d19}.

\subsection{Stable graph shape and persistent single-expert execution}

Apple/GGML execution showed strong sensitivity to graph shape.  Padding a
one--three-expert route to a stable four-branch graph with zero gates retained
the same first five token IDs and hidden-state hashes as the adaptive reference.
The settled five-token rate increased from $2.3357$ to $4.5863$ tokens/s.
However, the complete 33-token quality gate falsified this approach: small
floating-point differences accumulated, the route diverged, the request pool
was escaped, and sustained throughput collapsed to $0.1168$ tokens/s.  The
short and complete run hashes are
\texttt{1811dbf64983000304b3ae0a1436efa0e90a277a4fb02660746377f6fcf6aeee}
and
\texttt{47cb58c31fedd6fdd22a2b1089a69a403187c87d4d992a08109e4cb8eeeb1a3d}.
Decision: \texttt{STOP\_PADDED\_FOUR\_AS\_SUSTAINED\_QUALITY\_PATH}.

A second implementation constructed one persistent single-expert graph and
changed only activation, packed-weight, bias, and scalar-gate pointers.  Native
left-associated reduction preserved the adaptive reference bit-for-bit.  It
improved the settled five-token rate from $3.2435$ tokens/s for rebuilt serial
graphs to $4.5381$ tokens/s.  This was a real graph-reuse improvement, but it did
not exceed the existing correct 0.75 control at $4.6953$ tokens/s.  Its canonical
hash is
\texttt{14da05070e67f83c7e396aab28fd6d77bc863f60b865313fefd39bc9ff99035e}.
Decision: retain the primitive as supporting infrastructure; do not promote it
as the missing multiplier.

\subsection{Exact top--1 with low-bit correction experts}

A directly executable mixed representation retained the strongest routed
expert in original MXFP4 and requantised the other three experts to GGML Q2\_0.
Across four held-out real routes it preserved all local argmax decisions and
reduced selected weight bytes by $1.545\times$.  It nevertheless produced
median layer-output relative $L_2$ error $0.11815$ (p95 $0.17390$), median
contribution error $0.62632$ (p95 $0.82358$), and only $1.0077\times$ median
compute speedup.  It failed both quality and performance gates and was stopped.
Canonical hash:
\texttt{4f6b9c0f2f3a7f11fa1ae37e3f1041c4db4066344cee077dc8e19e6d985aa9e1}.

The missing factor is not SD lookup, graph construction, or redundant output
projection.  It is the information and physical work carried by experts two
through four.  The next architecture is therefore a confidence-gated top--1
draft with selective original-weight correction and exact fallback.  Its
selector must combine layer identity, the full gate distribution, activation
features, recent route state, and an explicit uncertainty gate.  It must be
frozen on a development cohort and evaluated on blinded arithmetic,
extraction, reasoning, business, and writing families.  Promotion requires
coherent and factually correct answers, internal repeatability, measured traffic
reduction, and balanced same-process timing.  Until those gates pass, $28.6174$
tokens/s remains a physical ceiling rather than a usable 120B result.

Focused tests after these changes passed 18/18.
