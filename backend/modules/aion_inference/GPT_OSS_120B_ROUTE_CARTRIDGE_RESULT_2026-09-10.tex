\section{Exact 120B Route-Cartridge Gate and Quality-Track Pivot}
\label{sec:gptoss-120b-route-cartridge}

\subsection{Question and predeclared acceptance rule}

The next candidate attempted to turn the verified GPT-OSS 120B SD warehouse
into a bounded resident route cartridge.  The intended mechanism retained
between 6 and 10~GiB of original packed experts, left the original router
authoritative, and used the exact verified SD fault path whenever a required
expert was absent.  To justify integration, every prompt family had to achieve
at least 90\% held-out route coverage, corresponding to a minimum tenfold
reduction in expert faults.  The threshold was frozen before inspecting the
result.

\subsection{Method}

Three independent route families were drawn from previously passed,
bitwise-repeatable GPT-OSS 120B executions: an eight-token BOS-conditioned
sequence, the four-token \texttt{Hello from AION} sequence, and the nine-token
Harmony \texttt{Hi} sequence.  For each family, the first half of the token
sequence was used to construct a deterministic frequency-per-byte cartridge;
the remaining tokens were held out.  Separate leave-one-family-out trials
trained on the other two families.  Resident and missed bytes were calculated
from the exact component lengths in the \texttt{COMPLETE\_VERIFIED} warehouse
manifest rather than from a constant expert-size assumption.

\subsection{Result}

The exact cartridge failed its promotion gate.  Within-family prefix-to-future
coverage was 48.7847\% for the BOS sequence, 30.2083\% for
\texttt{Hello from AION}, and 66.1111\% for the Harmony sequence.  Increasing
the ceiling from 6 to 8 or 10~GiB did not improve these measurements: the
additional future experts had not appeared in the observed prefix and could not
be selected exactly without speculation.  Minimum leave-one-family-out coverage
was 36.9792\%, 42.0139\%, and 46.1806\% at the three respective ceilings.  The
decision is therefore \texttt{STOP\_EXACT\_ROUTE\_CARTRIDGE}.

The canonical report SHA--256 is
\texttt{295232993424e95c44cac975933210dd5bbc3883dbc39cee30099ec21711df4c}.
The repository and SD copies are byte-identical, with stored-file SHA--256
\texttt{d76d9ab5d5d43539b494516ced869427f3c58008916670da0c2e47e9e690b99d}.
Six focused tests, including the existing verified expert-store tests, passed.

\subsection{Meaning and next boundary}

This result falsifies an exact prefix-trained cartridge as the route to
30--50 generated tokens/s on the present 18~GB M3 Pro.  It does not invalidate
the lossless SD warehouse, complete transformer execution, or the exact 0.0515
token/s later-token control.  It prevents a cache-coverage projection from being
reported as executed speed.

The next permitted experiment is explicitly a quality-gated changed-model
track.  Each layer may be constrained to a bounded resident pool selected from
the original GPT-OSS experts; original router scores choose the top four within
that pool.  The full 36-layer attention and residual backbone remains present.
Evaluation data and the unrestricted baseline must be frozen first, and results
must report semantic quality, probability divergence, memory, time to first
token, and genuine later-token throughput.  This constrained subnetwork must
never be labelled bit-exact full-model inference.  Removal of expert SD faults
is also insufficient by itself: the persistent resident execution boundary is
approximately 2.4 seconds per evaluation, so persistent Metal attention and
shared-tensor execution remain necessary before a 30-token/s claim is
physically credible.
