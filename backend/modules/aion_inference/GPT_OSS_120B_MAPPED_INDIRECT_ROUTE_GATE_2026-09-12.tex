\section{Mapped Indirect Multi-Expert Route Gate}

The next exact-work candidate removed the charged 52.9\,MB route copy from the
previous batched calculation.  Each of the four independently stored expert
projections was virtually mapped into a single strided tensor.  GGML's indirect
matrix operation then selected the four expert slices while sharing one input
activation, rather than copying four experts into a new contiguous arena or
replicating the input four times.  The original MXFP4 codes, biases, expert IDs
and router gates were retained.

On an initial favourable layer-0 observation the mapped indirect route was
$1.300\times$ faster than the ordinary pointer graph.  This justified a
predeclared three-route transfer at layers 0, 12 and 35.  The respective
speedups were $1.1144\times$, $1.1033\times$ and $1.2473\times$, for a median
of only $1.1144\times$.  Only the final route exceeded the $1.15\times$ narrow
gate.  A thread sweep on that route did not reproduce the favourable result:
four, eight and ten threads achieved $1.0548\times$, $1.0094\times$ and
$0.8186\times$.  The candidate is therefore
\texttt{NOT\_PROMOTED} as a general route mechanism.

The indirect operation changed floating-point reduction order.  Relative
$L_2$ disagreement with the ordinary CPU route was very small,
$3.39\times10^{-8}$ to $4.28\times10^{-8}$, and every route retained the same
output argmax, but outputs were not bitwise identical.  It consequently cannot
enter the exact track.  The tiny numerical difference does not rescue the
failed and unstable speed gate, so a costly full-model semantic run is not
justified.

The three transfer-report canonical hashes are
\texttt{b8325682cd687ef4aff5d780ec933de84dadfcf4fec24b11650df16edf85175c},
\texttt{200546e8b008bda9b4ad4399ab9fdc8c56e931c88a2fc95888d2a180acabff2e},
and
\texttt{9972d93287bd73c402a2c31a87450cc54b5a7d71fa83c2ada048f874fe68f597}.
This establishes that virtual mapping successfully removes route preparation,
but the remaining indirect-kernel gain is too small to bridge the measured
120B gap.  The unrestricted exact frontier remains approximately
1.72--1.83 tokens/s.
