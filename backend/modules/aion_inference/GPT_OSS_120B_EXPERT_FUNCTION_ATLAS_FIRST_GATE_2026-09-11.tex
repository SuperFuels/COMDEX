\section{Expert Function Atlas: First Weight-Informed Gate}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-expert-function-atlas-first-gate}

\subsection{Architecture and relation to the other atlases}

Three distinct AION atlases are now separated.  The Context Atlas selects the
smallest relevant external-memory working set and primarily improves prefill,
time to first token and completed-task throughput.  The Capability Atlas selects
the model, harness, deterministic tools and fallback path.  Neither directly
raises unrestricted decode tokens per second.  The Expert Function Atlas is the
decode-path experiment: it attempts to replace physical delivery and execution
of selected expert weights with a compact resident representation derived from
the experts' real functions.

For the first gate, all 128 original experts in transformer layer 12 were loaded
from the verified SD warehouse, one expert at a time.  Each expert was evaluated
against the exact live router activations captured from the frozen arithmetic
training family.  A shared output basis and expert-specific activation-to-basis
maps were compiled.  The separate extraction family was not used to fit the
atlas and served as the first unseen-family numerical test.

\subsection{One-family compilation result}

The best one-family cartridge occupied 561,358 bytes on disk, compared with
approximately 13.25~MiB for one complete original expert.  Its live correction
calculation required 0.01558~ms median.  On unseen extraction activations, simply
omitting the fourth expert produced layer-output relative-$L_2$ p95 of 0.13098;
the atlas reduced this to 0.09516.  Thus it achieved a real 27.3\% error reduction
while satisfying both the size and calculation-time gates.  It did not satisfy
the declared 0.02 p95 accuracy gate and was labelled
\texttt{STOP\_ATLAS\_LINEAR\_MAP}.  Canonical evidence hash:
\texttt{f840a6c016d6bfa52d9edfbf6c0b490b50d3c60db769ce83fd51c422b84719f5}.

\subsection{Two-family compilation and reasoning development gate}

The predeclared reasoning development prompt was then executed through the
complete exact 36-layer teacher twice, while recording only layer 12.  Twenty-one
development states were captured.  Routes and final hidden state/logits repeated
bit-for-bit; canonical evidence hash
\texttt{2d1fdc2288804df662d0c765c88aa9de63617cf157dcd4fd239149ea9c0ac440}.
Business and writing blind holdouts remained sealed.

The atlas was recompiled from 55 arithmetic-plus-extraction training
activations per expert and evaluated on the unseen reasoning activations.  The
best cartridge used activation rank 32 and output rank 48.  It occupied
2,367,529 bytes and executed in 0.03196~ms median including live activation
projection.  The drop-fourth baseline p95 was 0.10369 and the atlas achieved
0.07715, a 25.6\% reduction.  A local radial-basis atlas was also tested; its best
p95 was 0.07931 and therefore did not improve on the quadratic map.  The combined
result is labelled \texttt{STOP\_ATLAS\_LOCAL\_MAP}; canonical hash
\texttt{5e242e4fc73a8cc5915409ea7405b12b15f5edaa5584285e1574d5b9b7ccf1ea}.

\subsection{Interpretation and next compiler}

This is the first compact correction that improved an omitted expert across
unseen task families.  The improvement is not sufficient for integration: the
two-percent accuracy gate remains unchanged, and no full-model quality or
tokens-per-second claim is made.  Increasing shared output rank and replacing a
global quadratic map with local interpolation both plateaued.  The unresolved
error is therefore expert-specific information not captured by a single shared
activation atlas.

The next candidate is a multi-resolution, weight-derived atlas:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item retain a very small shared basis for global expert behaviour;
  \item compile compact expert-specific block or factor corrections directly
        from each original gate, up and down weight tensor;
  \item certify only expert/activation regions that satisfy the numerical gate;
  \item calculate two or three original experts when a lower-risk bridge is
        required; and
  \item invoke the unrestricted four-expert teacher whenever certification is
        absent.
\end{enumerate}
This is a changed-arithmetic quality track under the recorded authorization.
The exact teacher remains unmodified and authoritative.
