\section{Why the strategy changed from C4 only to ranked corrections}
The current quiet adaptive control uses four experts on only 22.8682\% of
layer calls. Approximately 77.13\% already use two or three. Therefore
replacing only E4 cannot act on most calls in the approximately 7 t/s path.

An offline analyzer divided aggregate route-finish plus delivery time by
active expert count and assumed the earlier resident correction cost ratio
0.0773. Complete E4 coverage yielded hypothetical multipliers 1.04924 and
1.06370. Replacing the last contribution on eligible two/three/four calls
yielded hypothetical multipliers 1.25993 and 1.27733. These are exploratory
accounting estimates, \emph{not} measured gains or rigorous ceilings. They do
not isolate overhead, unequal expert costs, fallback rates, changed locality,
instrumentation overlap or the SD fault path.

The next hypothesis is consequently:
\[
 4\rightarrow3+\widehat C_4,\qquad
 3\rightarrow2+\widehat C_3,\qquad
 2\rightarrow1+\widehat C_2.
\]
Each rank needs its own identity, gate, activation/state support and downstream
validation. An unrestricted teacher's rank target cannot automatically certify
the same rank under adaptive changed activations or renormalized gates.

\subsection{Target schema and verification}
Each gated contribution is a 2,880-value little-endian F32 vector,
11,520 bytes. New E2/E3 capture adds 23,040 bytes per observation without
another neural evaluation beyond existing counterfactual capture. Original
ordered identities and native F32 gates are retained. Diagnostic F64 values
are converted back to their original F32 target representation.

Only completed seals are eligible. Verification checks receipt canonical
hash, status and exact repeatability; corpus binding; full layer-position
grid; vector sizes/hashes; both E2/E3 vectors whenever either is declared;
full-vocabulary 201,088-F32 logits per captured position; and the seal's entire
file inventory. Partial binary files, planned counts and unsealed directories
are never successful evidence.

\subsection{Affine kernel-ridge model}
The fitter groups by rank and expert within the selected layer. It requires
at least four aligned finite fitting calls, retains at most 32, and requires
positive gates. Let $\mu$ be mean activation and $s$ the mean centered norm;
anchors are $z_i=(x_i-\mu)/s$. With $K_{ij}=z_i^Tz_j+1$, coefficients are
\[
 A=(K+0.001I)^{-1}Y_g,
\]
where row $i$ of $Y_g$ is the gated target divided by its gate. Center, scale,
anchors, coefficients and unit anchors are stored as F32 arrays. At runtime
the predicted ungated vector is multiplied by the actual gate.

The nearest raw-activation cosine must be at least 0.80. This mask is an
experimental support heuristic, not a proof of semantic safety. Supported
local error is $\|\widehat c-c\|_2/\|y_{\mathrm{teacher}}\|_2$, using the
\emph{full layer output} as denominator; it must be at most 0.02 and less
than omission error. Every supported development call in a fitted region
must pass. Stored arrays must be less than 25\% of nominal expert-frame size.
That storage gate does not establish compute-cost economics.

Models are stored and hashed before different-family development evaluation.
Both prompts currently belong to the training split; this is exploratory
internal development, not sealed holdout evaluation. Fit and development
prompts must differ in identity and family. The corpus separately freezes
24 training, six selection and six untouched holdout prompts over arithmetic,
business, explanation, extraction, reasoning and writing. Planned total
44,064 observations is not completed evidence.

\section{What to do next: advancement ladder and stopping rules}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Complete and verify broad training trajectories. Quantify layer/rank/
expert support and activation recurrence. Do not equate frequent identity with
certifiable function support or completed short captures with semantic success.
\item Inspect fixed exploratory cross-family probes. If support is inadequate,
collect more authentic training calls rather than claim that a tiny sample
certifies a region. If errors persist above 2\%, reject that region/model.
\item For a sufficiently supported local candidate, freeze model bytes,
training/development assignment, support rule and cost contract before
selection and untouched testing. C2/C3 require their own changed-active-state
targets/certification design.
\item Build an isolated, schema-validated ranked-correction runtime interface.
The native reducer is ready, but the current affine compiler output is not
automatically accepted by the old C4 injection path. Add integrity, gate/state
support, exact fallback and false-acceptance tests before enabling substitution.
\item Begin at one position and one layer, outside any active teacher job.
Compare local output, all downstream router scores and ordered/set identities,
hidden-state drift, full logits, top-two margins and emitted token. Preserve
original reduction order. Do not call token stability trajectory exactness.
\item Test untouched trajectories and then completed blinded answers across
arithmetic, extraction, reasoning, business, explanation and writing. Keep
selection data separate from final holdout evidence.
\item Charge support/controller/correction/fallback overhead. Require the
declared correction cost below 25\% of the omitted real expert under relevant
resident/fault conditions, not just small storage or an isolated multiply.
\item Only after earlier gates pass, alternate control/candidate conditions
within one loaded process. Balance order, separate warm-up/settled and
cold/warm storage, report p50/p95, bytes, peaks, failures and completed-answer
quality. A rate clearly above the tested approximately 7 t/s control is the
next meaningful quality-track milestone; 8--10 t/s is a target, not a claim.
\end{enumerate}
If a local family repeatedly fails despite meaningful new coverage, reconsider
the representation (for example weight-informed or router-sensitive local
corrections) through a new declared development experiment. Do not keep the
