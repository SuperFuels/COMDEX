\section{Top-Three Original Experts Plus a Compact Fourth Expert}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-top3-q2-fourth}

The first direct bridge toward the three-expert operating point retained the
three strongest routed experts in their original MXFP4 representation and
requantized only the lowest-gate fourth expert to directly executable Q2\_0.
The unrestricted router, all four expert identities, original gates, biases,
SwiGLU-OAI function, expert order, and residual addition remained unchanged.
This is a changed-weight quality-track experiment with exact MXFP4 fallback.

The candidate was evaluated on all 21 available layer-12 activations in the
opened reasoning development family.  It was not trained on these activations,
and the business and writing cohorts remained blind.

The decision is \texttt{STOP\_COMPACT\_EXPERTS}.  Compacting only the fourth
expert was materially narrower than the previously stopped all-expert and
top-one-original/three-compact experiments, but the fourth expert's quantization
error still propagated into a 0.123597 p95 layer-output error.  The measured
$1.0746\times$ compute gain and $1.1333\times$ weight reduction are also far too
small to approach the required 3--6 tokens/s bridge.

This result independently agrees with the stopped learned top-three residual,
whose family-held-out p95 error was approximately 0.10.  Plain low-bit
requantization and the existing small residual predictors therefore do not
preserve the lowest-gate expert's consequential information.  No full-model
integration or token-rate claim is permitted.
