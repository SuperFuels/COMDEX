\section{GPT-OSS 120B: Correction Integration, Teacher Coverage and Ranked Expert Compilation}
\label{sec:gptoss-ranked-correction-20260912}

\subsection{Scope and claim boundary}
This section consolidates recent correction experiments and runtime preparation
on the existing M3 Pro Mac with 18 GB unified memory and the compressed
GPT-OSS 120B SD warehouse. The model has 36 transformer layers, 128 experts
per layer and four selected experts per unrestricted layer call. No new
full-generation speed result is promoted by the work described here.

The frozen exact short-window results remain 1.72455 tokens/s for arithmetic
and 1.82858 tokens/s for extraction. The adaptive quality track has produced
a correct tested reasoning path near 7 tokens/s, but this is not a general
quality guarantee. Its quiet paired observations were 7.001898 and 5.491965
tokens/s. The earlier 28.6174 tokens/s top-one result remains an unusable
quality-failing physical execution measurement, not a usable 120B result.
Exact execution and approximate quality-gated execution remain separate.

\subsection{The correction hypothesis}
For an ordered route with $n$ selected experts, let $c_i=g_iE_i(x)$ denote
the actual gated expert contribution at activation $x$. The proposed
candidate retains $n-1$ real contributions and computes a compact estimate
$\widehat c_n$ of the last contribution:
\[
 y_{\mathrm{candidate}}=r+
 \bigl(((c_1+c_2)+\cdots+c_{n-1})+\widehat c_n\bigr).
\]
Here $r$ is the feed-forward residual input. Parentheses represent the
original floating-point reduction order, not merely algebraic equivalence.
The target is less physical expert delivery and computation, with true-expert
fallback outside validated support. A local approximation is not an exact
replacement and cannot be promoted without downstream testing.

\subsection{Earlier local feasibility and the addition-order defect}
The initial layer-12/expert-22 local correction reduced the recorded
fourth-expert omission error from 4.12\% to 1.66\%, below the fixed 2\%
local gate. Its 11,520-byte payload was extremely small. This established
local feasibility, not broad coverage: only one of 41 extraction calls
entered the declared joint region.

An integration audit subsequently exposed a confound: even substituting
the real exact fourth contribution through the old integration changed
838 output elements in one audit and 883 in another. The cause was a changed
floating-point addition order. The repaired reducer produced zero differing
elements with the real contribution. Earlier drift therefore could not be
attributed solely to approximation error.

A richer secant correction used 46,080 bytes of fitted payload, with a
reported loaded footprint of 69,288 bytes. On the recorded prospective
training-family activation, its local error was approximately 0.774\%, versus
1.577\% for the constant correction on that same activation. This is a
same-activation comparison, not the earlier 4.12\%-to-1.66\% comparison.
Its region covered one of 39 calls. A local charged resident-compute ratio
of 0.0773 was recorded; this is not the SD fault-path cost.

The downstream test retained token IDs over 42 total positions, but only
15 positions were at or after the substitution. It still produced 28 ordered
route changes, 15 expert-set changes and one top-expert change; maximum
chosen-logit drift was 0.5266 and the recorded maximum token-margin danger
was 11.41\%. The appropriate status remains
\texttt{TOKEN\_STABLE\_DOWNSTREAM\_DRIFT}, not trajectory-exact certification
or long-horizon safety. No speed gain follows from this result.

\subsection{Measured opportunity: why fourth-expert-only coverage is insufficient}
An offline analyzer examined completed canonical-hash-bound receipts,
continuation positions and complete 36-layer observation grids. In the quiet
adaptive-0.80 reasoning control, only 22.8682\% of layer calls used four
experts; approximately 77.13\% already used two or three. A fourth-expert-only
compiler cannot operate on those other calls.

For exploratory accounting, aggregate route-finish plus delivery time was
divided by active expert count and the local correction ratio 0.0773 was
assumed. Complete eligible E4 coverage yielded hypothetical multipliers
1.04924 and 1.06370. Replacing the last selected contribution on eligible
two-, three- and four-expert calls yielded hypothetical multipliers 1.25993
and 1.27733. These are estimates, not measured speedups or rigorous ceilings:
non-removable overhead, unequal expert costs, changed locality, instrumentation
overlap, controller expense and fallback frequency were not isolated.

The resulting implementation direction is rank-specific C2/C3/C4 compilation,
not relabelling an E4 correction as universally valid. The prepared ordered
quartet-slab proposal was also checked against existing evidence rather than
blindly repeated: the unrestricted 96-position extraction trace had only one
post-admission quartet reuse, 0.0289\% of routes. The recorded adaptive slab
reuse did not repay multi-gigabyte storage. No new slab speed claim was made.

\subsection{Teacher corpus, disk protection and first completed trajectory}
The declared corpus contains 36 prompts across six families, split into
24 training, six selection and six untouched holdout prompts. Its planned
44,064 layer-position observations are a programme total, not completed data.
Earlier collection roots were interrupted under local disk pressure and are
preserved as partial data, not verified evidence. The second restart writes
local results, retains a 3 GiB free-space reserve, checks that reserve during
capture and between jobs, and interrupts safely if it is exhausted.
The protected expert workbench is 8 GiB; no additional L2 allocation was made.
Competing full-model inference is excluded during teacher capture.

The first restart-two arithmetic trajectory reached
\texttt{COMPLETE\_VERIFIED}: 1,332 observations, 37 captured positions across
all 36 layers, with routes, hidden state and logits repeatable. Its 16
continuation positions provide 576 real E4 calls. It used the previously
declared arithmetic prompt and is not an untouched new prompt.
One family alone establishes neither cross-family support nor certification.
At this reporting snapshot the following business capture is still active;
its partial observations are excluded from fitting and measured completion
counts until the final verification seal exists.

\subsection{Ranked contribution targets and verification}
Future teacher jobs save gated second and third contributions in addition to
the fourth. Each target is a 2,880-element little-endian F32 vector containing
11,520 bytes. Original ordered expert identities and actual native F32 gates
are retained. Adding C2/C3 targets costs 23,040 bytes per four-expert
observation without another expert read or calculation beyond the existing
counterfactual capture. Diagnostic F64 representations are returned to their
original F32 target representation.

The first arithmetic process loaded older E4-only code. It is not counted as
C2/C3 coverage. Follow-up business capture uses the new writer, but its
completion remains unverified at this snapshot. Verification checks both new
targets together whenever either is declared, binary sizes and hashes,
complete layer-position grids, full-vocabulary hashes, teacher repeatability
and the completion's hash-bound file inventory. Selection and holdouts remain
unused. Unrestricted teacher targets do not automatically certify altered
adaptive activations or gates.

\subsection{Incremental local compiler and frozen support rules}
A bounded one-BLAS-thread worker watches verified training completions and
fits layer-12 probes using different training prompt families for fitting
and development. It performs no new SD expert reads or neural evaluations.
It never consumes partial files or sealed selection/holdout prompts, and does
not inject corrections automatically.

The model is an expert-specific affine kernel-ridge map. Activations are
centered and scaled; the affine kernel is an activation inner product plus
one. Regularization is fixed at 0.001. Gated targets are divided by their
positive native gate during fitting and predictions rescaled by the gate.
At least four fitting calls are required, with at most 32 retained. A nearest
fitting-activation cosine below 0.80 rejects the call. The fitted arrays are
saved and hashed before different-family development evaluation.

For a supported call, local error is the norm of correction-target difference
divided by the original full layer-output norm. It must be at most 0.02 and
improve over omission. Every supported development call in a region must pass;
resident arrays must occupy less than 25\% of the nominal 13.25 MiB expert
frame. This storage check is not a charged compute-cost gate. Single-call
timing under active teacher load is diagnostic, not performance evidence.
Passing status is only a local candidate requiring untouched validation.

\subsection{Negative real-data result: identity recurrence is not activation support}
A same-prompt chronological sanity test fitted on prefill and evaluated later
continuation, without claiming independent transfer. Support-count selection
identified layer 2/expert 0, layer 13/expert 0 and layer 18/expert 53. Their
stored array sizes were 184,324, 149,764 and 149,764 bytes respectively.
The two, one and three continuation calls all failed the frozen 0.80 support
screen: observed cosine values ranged from 0.1581 to 0.5985. Supported coverage
was zero of six. This is a coverage miss, not numerical accuracy failure,
and does not disprove correction feasibility. It shows that repeated expert
identity alone cannot justify prefill-to-continuation substitution.

\subsection{Native integration breakthrough: exact ranked-contribution audit}
The native repaired reduction was generalized to one, two or three retained
experts plus an already-gated final contribution. Existing exact and original
three-plus-C4 entry points remain unchanged. On a real cached layer-12 route,
the audit compared diagnostic two-, three- and four-expert controls against
one plus real C2, two plus real C3, and three plus real C4 respectively.

All three comparisons were bit-for-bit identical with zero differing output
elements. One CPU thread was used. All six components per retrieved expert
were read from existing local L2 and checked against source-manifest lengths
and hashes. The audit performed zero SD expert reads, no admission and no
deletion. This is a genuine integration correctness result, not learned
approximation accuracy, semantic certification or speed evidence.

\subsection{Coverage accounting and software validation}
Reports now include every development call of each rank in their denominator,
including unmatched expert identities. They separately count unsupported
identities, activation-supported calls, numerical passes and calls in regions
that pass the complete local probe. A region with any supported numerical
failure is not credited as usable candidate traffic. Missing rank targets
produce unavailable shares. Certified traffic share remains explicitly unset.

The latest focused suite passed 22 of 22 tests, covering fitting/support,
ranked target integrity, queue verification and disk protection, runtime
opportunity accounting, read-only cache integrity and full-denominator
coverage. These software passes do not prove model quality or tokens/s.
Isolated commits include \texttt{81dea819} for the native reduction audit and
\texttt{396187e2} for coverage accounting.

\subsection{Remaining promotion contract}
The next advancement requires sufficient authentic cross-family support,
frozen local corrections, untouched numerical and downstream route/logit/token
tests, measured fallback frequency and charged correction cost below the
declared 25\% ceiling. Only then may balanced same-process completed-answer
control/candidate generation establish a new throughput result. Local passes,
microsecond calculations, hypothetical multipliers and incomplete captures
must not be presented as a raw-speed breakthrough.

\subsection{Subsequent completion: first cross-family numerical result}
After the snapshot above, business01 completed exact verification with 1,188
layer-position observations. It contains 1,188 second-, third- and fourth-
contribution targets each, with repeatable routes, hidden state and logits.
The explanation01 teacher then started automatically. Thus the new ranked
target writer now has completed measured evidence, not merely implementation.

The first arithmetic-to-business layer-12 probe fitted expert 64 from four
arithmetic continuation calls. One business call at position 20 met the frozen
support screen (cosine 0.8910833). Its omitted-contribution error was
6.939687\%; the correction reduced this to 3.246070\%, approximately a 53.2\%
relative reduction. The fitted arrays occupy 149,764 bytes and pass the
storage-size check, but accuracy fails the unchanged 2\% gate. The result is
\texttt{NOT\_CERTIFIED}, with zero locally passing calls out of all 16
development E4 calls. Fifteen calls lacked a fitted identity. The older
arithmetic fitting job has no E2/E3 targets, so those ranks have no fitted
models; this is not an E2/E3 approximation failure. Timing under active teacher
load is not performance evidence. No correction was injected.

The two completed teachers now supply 1,152 continuation E4 calls across all
36 layers. None yet meet the exploratory eight-call, two-family identity
support criterion. This criterion measures identity recurrence only, not
certification. Certified traffic share remains unset. Gates were not relaxed
after this result and no raw-speed frontier changed.
