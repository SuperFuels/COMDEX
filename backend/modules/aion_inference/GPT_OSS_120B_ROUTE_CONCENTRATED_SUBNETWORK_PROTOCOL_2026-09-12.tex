\section{Route-Concentrated Subnetwork: Pre-registered Quality-Track Protocol}
\label{sec:gptoss120b-route-concentrated-protocol}

\subsection{Reason for the architectural change}

The exact unrestricted runtime remains approximately 1.72--1.83 generated
tokens per second, whereas a resident fixed top-one path demonstrated a
28.62-token-per-second physical ceiling but failed semantic quality.  Exact
consequence oracles subsequently showed that omitting selected experts can
remove only approximately 3.8\% of four-expert work on the measured cohort.
The remaining gap therefore cannot honestly be closed by another cache,
lossless encoding, expert-omission rule, or route-copy optimization alone.

The next candidate changes the learned calculation.  GPT-OSS 120B remains the
authoritative teacher and exact fallback, while a resident route-concentrated
subnetwork attempts to reproduce the combined four-expert layer output with
one original expert plus a compact learned correction.  This is explicitly a
quality-gated derived model; it is not bit-exact unrestricted GPT-OSS 120B.

\subsection{Candidate structure}

For layer $\ell$, activation $x$, selected expert identities $e_{1:4}$ and
normalized router gates $g_{1:4}$, the candidate is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{M}_{\ell}(x)=g_1 E_{\ell,e_1}(x)+
 C_{\ell}\!\left(x,e_{1:4},g_{1:4}\right),
\end{equation}
where the first term uses one original packed expert and $C_{\ell}$ is a
resident correction.  The correction must materially differ from the stopped
global affine, RBF, low-rank, Taylor-Atlas and 2.08\,MB AtomSheet calculators.
Its first admissible form is layer-specific and route-conditioned, with
expert-identity embeddings, activation-region addressing, a shared nonlinear
trunk, and a bounded low-rank output head.  Training targets are produced only
by the locally installed unrestricted teacher under the existing signed
authorization; captures contain no prompt text, personal data, or customer
data.

\subsection{Frozen data separation}

Whole prompt families, not individual positions, are assigned to training,
development and blind evaluation.  Model capacity, optimizer settings,
confidence thresholds and fallback rules are selected without opening the
blind reasoning, business and writing families.  Synthetic perturbations may
be generated around training activations to improve local coverage, but every
such target must be recalculated by the original selected experts and bound to
the source activation, route, teacher-weight manifest and random seed.

\subsection{Promotion ladder}

The first gate is restricted to one real layer and then two non-adjacent
layers.  A candidate advances only if all of the following hold:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item held-out layer-output relative-$L_2$ p95 is at most 0.02;
  \item maximum relative-$L_2$ is at most 0.05;
  \item the certified non-fallback coverage is at least 10\%;
  \item the resident correction plus the retained original expert moves at
        most half the bytes of the four-expert teacher on accepted positions;
  \item accepted calculation latency is below the corresponding two-expert
        packed path and the confidence decision costs less than 0.05\,ms;
  \item no evaluation example, fallback, or failed route is silently omitted.
\end{enumerate}

Passing the numerical gate permits a bounded full-transformer substitution on
unseen generated positions.  Promotion then additionally requires coherent
answers, semantic-score parity with the unrestricted teacher on arithmetic,
extraction, reasoning, business and writing families, internal repeatability,
and a positive balanced same-process speed result.  Results are reported at
successive rungs of 5, 8, 12, 15, 20 and 25 tokens per second; the target is
not redefined when a rung fails.

\subsection{Fallback and claim boundary}

The confidence system may use only information available before calculating
the omitted experts.  If the layer, route, activation region or uncertainty is
uncertified, the runtime executes all four original experts.  Oracle coverage
may be reported only as an upper bound and never as deployable coverage.
Exact-track results remain separate and unchanged.  A fast candidate that
fails quality is a physical-ceiling experiment, while a quality-passing
candidate that fails speed is preserved as \texttt{NOT\_PROMOTED}.

This protocol converts the remaining problem into a falsifiable question:
whether a compact, resident, route-conditioned student can absorb the useful
effect of three experts often enough to approach the measured top-one hardware
ceiling without surrendering the teacher's behaviour.

\subsection{First real-anchor local candidate and falsification}

The first implementation increased both teacher coverage and model locality
relative to the stopped Atlas candidates.  It used 33 real arithmetic anchors
from layer 12, spanning 23 distinct fourth-expert identities.  Around every
anchor, 32 perturbations were sampled in a 24-dimensional empirical activation
subspace.  The original SD-backed expert recalculated every target, producing
1,056 additional training rows.  Extraction alone selected rank and ridge
regularization; the 21-position reasoning family remained unopened until the
final gate.

The selected candidate used a rank-16 expert-conditioned local linear map with
ridge 0.1 and occupied 12,186,061 bytes.  It covered 9 of 22 extraction rows,
but none met the two-percent error boundary; extraction p95 relative layer-
output error was 0.10258.  On the unseen reasoning family, matching expert
identities existed for 10 of 21 rows.  Again, none met the boundary.  Reasoning
relative-$L_2$ was 0.06752 at p50 and 0.07433 at p95.  Exact fallback would
therefore be required for every observed position.

The result is \texttt{STOP\_ROUTE\_CONCENTRATED\_LOCAL}.  It is a useful
falsification because it materially strengthened the prior calculator: targets
were generated from the original weights around route-specific real anchors,
the model was conditioned on the actual omitted expert, and whole families
remained separated.  The failure shows that small local linear neighborhoods
around arithmetic activations do not transfer the omitted expert function to
extraction or reasoning.  Increasing the number of perturbations around the
same anchors cannot repair this missing cross-family coverage.  A successor
must learn a genuinely nonlinear layer student from substantially broader
teacher trajectories, or directly alter/train the retained expert, rather than
add another nearest-anchor or local-Jacobian calculator.

The augmentation dataset contains no prompt text, personal data or customer
data.  Its canonical report hash is
\texttt{5c32cc730b58b075c73251da5118edb8db79cc97f0a435b7c4778926804165f2}
and its dataset SHA-256 is
\texttt{c4cb50a0704ee2cf435c6d45ad5885e675875ec743f749d57055afefc15d9286}.
The final gate canonical hash is
\texttt{88ed9bfa47c06414c1b53133ddc42c4b53b77da6fbdebe7592b9c90e572e70b5}.
The focused integrity suite passed 26/26 tests.

\subsection{Higher-capacity nonlinear student}

A materially larger nonlinear successor was then trained on the same isolated
arithmetic teacher data.  Unlike the stopped 64-unit global calculator, this
model was specific to layer 12, consumed the full 2,880-dimensional activation,
embedded the omitted expert identity, included the fourth gate value, and used
two nonlinear hidden layers.  Three candidates up to 512 hidden units were
selected using extraction only.  The chosen cartridge occupied 12,980,881
bytes and contained approximately 1,089 training rows after combining anchors
and exact teacher perturbations.

This additional capacity did not solve transfer.  It covered 9 of 22 extraction
rows with known expert identities, but zero were within the two-percent error
boundary; p95 was 0.11362.  On the unopened reasoning development family it
covered 10 of 21 rows, again with zero safe positions.  Reasoning relative
$L_2$ was 0.07343 at p50 and 0.08319 at p95.  Median measured Python/PyTorch
calculation time was approximately 1.0004\,ms.  The decision is therefore
\texttt{STOP\_ROUTE\_CONCENTRATED\_NONLINEAR}.

The canonical report hash is
\texttt{17baac2033d589a49f28d8fad17976f4973916dfa76dac0c4d3ab848d82e072d}.
Together with the real-anchor local failure, this result closes small supervised
correction networks trained around one prompt family's activation manifold.
The next admissible student must first expand genuine teacher trajectories
across prompt families and expert identities; simply adding parameters or more
near-anchor perturbations is not a credible route to the missing speed factor.

\subsection{Expanded genuine-family retraining}

The nonlinear architecture was frozen before adding extraction to its training
set.  A new teacher augmentation contained 55 real arithmetic and extraction
anchors, 33 distinct omitted-expert identities, and 1,320 exact recalculations.
The 512-unit architecture and $10^{-4}$ weight decay selected by the preceding
gate were not retuned against reasoning.

Broader genuine-family training increased reasoning identity coverage from
10/21 to 12/21, but it did not create a safe operating region.  Zero of the 21
reasoning positions met the two-percent boundary.  Among covered positions,
relative layer-output error was 0.07193 at p50 and 0.08175 at p95.  The
12,980,561-byte cartridge evaluated in approximately 0.9780\,ms median.  This
is again \texttt{STOP\_ROUTE\_CONCENTRATED\_NONLINEAR}.

The canonical report hash is
\texttt{b933932701be63a9aea8ae3481379a826cbf23f2eb6bd2ff3d0c26f44b532d10}
and the augmented dataset SHA-256 is
\texttt{2e4a2c658771363b8ca84ba720079570910d077ed0414bd0f141605139ac4313}.
The result closes the present stand-alone correction-network family: neither
capacity, real-anchor teacher perturbations, nor combining two genuine prompt
families yielded even oracle-certified reasoning coverage.  The next model
change must train the retained expert/subnetwork itself, or use a substantially
larger same-tokenizer student, with the original four-expert path retained as
teacher and fallback.

\subsection{Retained-expert low-rank adapter oracle}

Before capturing and training an adapter on the top expert's internal SwiGLU
features, an intentionally impossible output-space oracle tested whether such
work could satisfy the gate with the present teacher cohort.  The oracle was
given each unseen reasoning target itself and allowed to choose its optimal
coefficients in the complete rank-54 span of the 55 arithmetic-and-extraction
omitted-expert targets.  A deployable adapter would have less information.

Even this upper bound admitted only one of 21 reasoning positions at the
two-percent boundary, or 4.76\% coverage, below the predeclared 10\% minimum.
Its p50, p95 and maximum relative layer-output errors were respectively
0.05833, 0.07174 and 0.08183.  Lower ranks admitted no additional positions.
The result is \texttt{STOP\_RETAINED\_EXPERT\_LOW\_RANK\_ADAPTER}.

This saved a misleading implementation cycle: capturing the retained expert's
internal feature can improve coefficient prediction, but it cannot create
missing output directions when the adapter is restricted to the present
learned low-rank target span.  The next admissible retained-subnetwork design
must add broader genuine-family teacher targets or learn new full output
directions through direct expert-weight training.  Its canonical report hash
is
\texttt{dee9800ef0d050989c8babd5113d6e3b8934fa058565ed9e80c6f2318295bf8d}.

\subsection{Expanded-trajectory oracle}

To test whether the adapter bound was merely a consequence of too few teacher
directions, AION composed 12 previously verified families containing 957
layer-position records.  Training combined the genuine arithmetic and
extraction trajectories with BOS, four independent synthetic tokens and two
independent eight-position KV-linked trajectories.  Reasoning remained the
untouched semantic development family.  Rebuilding the top-three-expert
dataset produced 78 layer-12 fit targets, rather than 55.

The complete rank-77 teacher-target span still admitted only one of 21 unseen
reasoning positions, or 4.76\%.  Relative layer-output error improved only
slightly to 0.05744 p50 and 0.07101 p95, with a maximum of 0.07955.  The result
remains \texttt{STOP\_RETAINED\_EXPERT\_LOW\_RANK\_ADAPTER}.  Its canonical
hash is
\texttt{c2d6cffbe982f886a24d3b4b329a03d23a59235d67ca798801aa0bf3ac4183d6}.

This is a stronger boundary than the 55-row gate: adding existing independent
token and trajectory directions did not increase safe reasoning coverage.
The missing structure is not repaired by mixing more synthetic states into the
same low-rank output span.  Further work must collect substantially broader
genuine semantic trajectories and train new expert-weight directions, not
repeat target-span projection.

\subsection{SD-native tokenizer recovery}

Collecting broader genuine semantic trajectories required a trustworthy local
tokenizer without downloading another model or tokenizer package.  AION now
reads the 201,088-token vocabulary and 446,189 BPE merges directly from the
verified virtual GGUF header in the compressed SD warehouse.  It implements
the GGUF-declared \texttt{gpt-4o} pre-tokenization rule, byte encoding, ranked
BPE merges, special-token addressing and the GPT-OSS Harmony user wrapper.

Against the frozen public prompt ``What is 17 multiplied by 23?'', the new
tokenizer reproduced all 15 previously external token IDs exactly, including
Harmony control tokens.  The canonical gate hash is
\texttt{c2a2a3bd46a81573def806ccf91b07a66eb39c5c8508972d0331aaec8ef81f80}.
The focused suite now passes 27/27 tests.

This is not a token-per-second result.  It removes an experimental dependency:
AION can now construct additional public/generic GPT-OSS prompt families from
the same SD-resident model representation, capture genuine teacher
trajectories, and keep the resulting student-data lineage bound to the
warehouse itself.  The next gate uses this capability to widen semantic
coverage before any new expert-weight training.
